## **Materi 2.3.1: Pengenalan Text Coherence**

### **Apa Sih Coherence Itu?**

Bayangin kamu lagi chatting sama teman, terus dia tiba-tiba ngomongin hal yang nggak nyambung. Pasti kamu bingung kan? Nah, itulah pentingnya **coherence** dalam sebuah teks!

**Coherence** itu adalah "kejelasan hubungan antar ide dalam teks". Sederhananya, coherence memastikan bahwa semua kalimat dalam teks saling terhubung secara **logis**, sehingga pembaca bisa mengikuti alur pemikiran penulis dengan mudah tanpa merasa "loncat-loncat".

### **Coherence vs Cohesion vs Unity: The Dream Team!**

Ketiga konsep ini seperti trio superhero yang bekerja sama untuk membuat teks berkualitas:

**1. Coherence (Kepaduan Logis)**
- Ini adalah "jiwa" dari teks
- Fokus pada **logical connections** antar ide
- Pertanyaannya: "Apakah ide-ide dalam teks ini masuk akal dan saling mendukung?"
- Lebih abstrak, berkaitan dengan **meaning** dan **logic**

**Contoh Teks Tanpa Coherence:**
> "Climate change is a serious problem. My cat loves to sleep. Scientists recommend reducing carbon emissions. Pizza is my favorite food."

Kalimat-kalimatnya berdiri sendiri dan nggak ada hubungan logis!

**Contoh Teks dengan Coherence:**
> "Climate change is a serious problem. Scientists warn that rising temperatures threaten ecosystems worldwide. To address this issue, experts recommend reducing carbon emissions through renewable energy adoption."

Semua ide saling terhubung secara logis: masalah → peringatan → solusi.

**2. Cohesion (Keterkaitan Linguistik)**
- Ini adalah "lem" yang mengikat kalimat-kalimat
- Fokus pada **linguistic devices** yang menghubungkan kalimat
- Pertanyaannya: "Apakah ada kata/frasa yang menunjukkan hubungan antar kalimat?"
- Lebih konkret, menggunakan **transition words, pronouns, synonyms**

**Contoh Cohesion Devices:**
- Pronoun reference: "The scientist discovered a new species. **She** named it..."
- Transition words: "First... Next... **Finally**..."
- Repetition: "Education is crucial. **This education** must be accessible..."

**3. Unity (Kesatuan Fokus)**
- Ini adalah "disiplin" teks
- Fokus pada **one main idea per paragraph**
- Pertanyaannya: "Apakah semua kalimat mendukung satu topic sentence?"
- Tidak ada informasi yang **irrelevant** atau **off-topic**

**Contoh Paragraph Without Unity:**
> "Social media affects teenagers' mental health. Many teens spend hours on Instagram and TikTok. My grandmother doesn't understand social media. Studies show increased anxiety among heavy users. I prefer reading books. Cyberbullying has also become more common."

Ada kalimat yang nggak relevan: nenek saya, preferensi membaca.

**Contoh Paragraph With Unity:**
> "Social media affects teenagers' mental health. Many teens spend hours on Instagram and TikTok, comparing themselves to others. Studies show increased anxiety among heavy users. Cyberbullying has also become more common, further damaging young people's self-esteem."

Semua kalimat fokus pada satu topik: dampak media sosial terhadap kesehatan mental remaja.

### **Mengapa Coherence Penting dalam SNBT?**

Di soal SNBT, kamu bakal sering ketemu pertanyaan yang menguji apakah kamu bisa:
1. **Mengidentifikasi kalimat yang nggak nyambung** (irrelevant sentence)
2. **Mengurutkan kalimat acak** (jumbled sentences)
3. **Melengkapi kalimat yang hilang** (sentence completion)
4. **Memahami hubungan antar paragraf**

Semua ini butuh pemahaman tentang coherence!

### **The Golden Rule of Coherence**

> **"Setiap kalimat harus punya alasan jelas untuk ada di situ, dan harus connect dengan kalimat sebelum/sesudahnya."**

### **Quick Check: Apakah Teks Coherent?**

Tanyakan 3 hal ini:
1. ✅ **Logical flow**: Apakah ide berkembang secara masuk akal?
2. ✅ **Clear connections**: Apakah hubungan antar kalimat jelas?
3. ✅ **Consistent focus**: Apakah tetap pada satu topik utama?

Kalau ketiga pertanyaan dijawab "yes", berarti teks punya coherence yang baik!

### **Hubungan dengan Organization of Ideas (Topik 2.1)**

Coherence adalah **hasil** dari organization yang baik. Kalau ide-ide disusun dengan pola yang jelas (chronological, cause-effect, problem-solution, dll), otomatis teks akan lebih coherent.

Think of it this way:
- **Organization** = the blueprint (rancangan)
- **Coherence** = the finished building (hasil jadi yang rapi)

### **Tips Praktis untuk Soal SNBT**

**1. Baca Sambil "Nge-track" Ide**
Saat baca teks, coret atau tandai mental:
- Main idea paragraf
- Keyword yang berulang
- Transition words

**2. Cari "Red Flags" yang Merusak Coherence**
- Kalimat yang topiknya **tiba-tiba berbeda**
- Kalimat yang **nggak ada hubungan** dengan kalimat sebelumnya
- Informasi yang **terlalu random**

**3. Perhatikan First dan Last Sentence**
- First sentence: biasanya introduce topic
- Last sentence: biasanya wrap up atau transition
- Kalau keduanya nggak connect, ada masalah coherence!

### **Common Jebakan dalam Soal**

**Jebakan #1: Kata dari Teks Muncul, Tapi Nggak Relevan**
> Teks tentang "climate change solutions"
> 
> Opsi: "Climate change has been studied since the 1950s."

Kata "climate change" muncul, tapi nggak related dengan **solutions** (fokus teks).

**Jebakan #2: Kalimat Benar Secara Fakta, Tapi Nggak Coherent**
> Paragraf: "Renewable energy like solar and wind power is becoming more affordable. Installation costs have dropped by 70% in the last decade."
> 
> Opsi irrelevant: "Coal is a fossil fuel formed millions of years ago."

Fakta tentang coal benar, tapi nggak connect dengan discussion tentang renewable energy.

**Jebakan #3: Terlalu General atau Terlalu Specific**
- **Terlalu general**: nggak add value, too vague
- **Terlalu specific**: detail yang nggak penting untuk main discussion

### **Mental Model: The Chain Link Analogy**

Bayangkan teks seperti rantai:
- Setiap **kalimat** = satu mata rantai
- **Coherence devices** = sambungan antar mata rantai
- Kalau satu mata rantai putus atau nggak connect → rantai rusak!

Teks yang coherent seperti rantai yang kuat: semua mata rantai saling mengait dengan sempurna.

## **Materi 2.3.2: Analisis Coherence Devices**

### **Apa Itu Coherence Devices?**

**Coherence devices** adalah "alat-alat" atau teknik linguistik yang digunakan penulis untuk menghubungkan ide-ide dalam teks. Bayangkan ini seperti **jembatan** yang menghubungkan pulau-pulau (kalimat) sehingga pembaca bisa "menyeberang" dengan lancar dari satu ide ke ide lainnya.

Tanpa coherence devices, teks akan terasa **choppy** (terputus-putus) seperti kamu lagi jalan di jalan berlubang!

### **5 Jenis Coherence Devices Utama**

---

#### **1. PRONOUN REFERENCE (Kata Ganti)**

**Fungsi:** Menghindari repetisi dan menunjukkan bahwa kita masih membahas hal yang sama.

**Cara Kerja:**
- Pronoun merujuk ke **noun** (antecedent) yang sudah disebutkan sebelumnya
- Menciptakan **connection** antar kalimat

**Jenis-jenis Pronoun:**

**a) Personal Pronouns**
- Subject: I, you, he, she, it, we, they
- Object: me, you, him, her, it, us, them
- Possessive: my, your, his, her, its, our, their

**Contoh:**
> "**Dr. Sarah Chen** completed her groundbreaking research on marine biology. **She** discovered three new species of coral. **Her** findings were published in Nature journal."

- **She** = Dr. Sarah Chen
- **Her** = milik Dr. Sarah Chen

**b) Demonstrative Pronouns**
- This, that (singular)
- These, those (plural)

**Contoh:**
> "Plastic pollution threatens ocean life. **This problem** requires immediate action."

- **This problem** = plastic pollution (merujuk ke keseluruhan konsep sebelumnya)

**Special Note:** *This* dan *that* bisa merujuk ke:
- **Satu noun** spesifik: "The company launched a new product. **This product** is eco-friendly."
- **Whole idea/clause**: "Scientists warned about rising sea levels. **This** alarmed many governments."

**c) Relative Pronouns**
- Who, whom, whose (untuk orang)
- Which, that (untuk benda)

**Contoh:**
> "The students **who** studied consistently performed better on the exam."

**Tips Penting:**
- Kalau ada pronoun, SELALU lacak antecedent-nya!
- Distance matters: pronoun biasanya merujuk ke **noun terdekat** yang agree dalam number (singular/plural) dan gender

**Common Jebakan:**
> "John told **Mark** that **he** passed the exam."

Siapa yang lulus? John atau Mark? Ambiguous! Di soal SNBT, kamu harus bisa identify dari context.

---

#### **2. REPEATED KEY TERMS (Pengulangan Kata Kunci)**

**Fungsi:** Menekankan topik utama dan maintain focus pembahasan.

**Cara Kerja:**
- Kata kunci (biasanya noun atau noun phrase) diulang beberapa kali
- Menciptakan **thematic coherence** (koherensi tema)

**Contoh:**
> "**Artificial intelligence** is transforming healthcare. **AI** can diagnose diseases faster than human doctors. The **technology** also helps develop personalized treatment plans. Despite these advances, **AI** still requires human oversight."

Key terms: Artificial intelligence → AI → the technology → AI

**Why It Works:**
- Pembaca tahu kita masih ngomongin hal yang sama
- Creates a "thread" yang mengikat paragraf

**Variasi yang Sering Digunakan:**
- Full term → Abbreviation: "World Health Organization" → "WHO"
- General → Specific: "the problem" → "air pollution"
- Abstract → Concrete: "this innovation" → "the smartphone"

**Tips:**
- Pengulangan exact word = strong connection, tapi bisa boring
- Pengulangan dengan variasi = maintains interest sambil tetap coherent

**Jebakan dalam Soal:**
- Kata kunci muncul tapi dalam **konteks berbeda** → nggak related!
- Pastikan repeated term memang refer to the same concept

---

#### **3. SYNONYMS dan WORD VARIATIONS (Sinonim & Variasi Kata)**

**Fungsi:** Menghindari repetisi membosankan sambil maintain coherence.

**Cara Kerja:**
- Gunakan kata berbeda dengan makna sama/mirip
- Shows lexical sophistication (kehebatan vocabulary)

**Contoh Synonym Chains:**

**Chain 1: "Doctor"**
> "The **physician** examined the patient. The **doctor** recommended immediate surgery. The **medical professional** explained the procedure carefully."

- Physician = doctor = medical professional

**Chain 2: "Important"**
> "This research is **crucial** for understanding climate change. The **significant** findings will inform policy decisions. Such **vital** information cannot be ignored."

- Crucial = significant = vital

**Types of Word Variations:**

**a) Synonyms (Kata dengan makna sama)**
- Study → research, investigation, examination
- Show → demonstrate, illustrate, reveal, indicate
- Problem → issue, challenge, difficulty, concern

**b) Superordinate-Subordinate (Umum-Khusus)**
- Vehicle (umum) → car, bus, motorcycle (khusus)
- Animal (umum) → dog, cat, elephant (khusus)

**Contoh:**
> "Scientists observed unusual behavior in **dolphins**. The **marine mammals** appeared to communicate using new patterns. These **animals** demonstrated problem-solving abilities."

- Dolphins (specific) → marine mammals (less specific) → animals (most general)

**c) Paraphrase (Mengubah struktur)**
- "reduce pollution" → "lower contamination levels"
- "improve education" → "enhance learning quality"

**Strategic Use:**
- **First mention**: use specific term
- **Subsequent mentions**: use synonyms/variations untuk variety
- **Important points**: repeat exact term untuk emphasis

**Warning:**
Tidak semua synonym benar-benar interchangeable!
- *Cheap* vs *inexpensive*: sama-sama "murah", tapi *cheap* bisa negative (murahan)
- *Skinny* vs *slender*: sama-sama "kurus", tapi konotasi beda

---

#### **4. PARALLEL STRUCTURES (Struktur Paralel)**

**Fungsi:** Menciptakan rhythm dan emphasis untuk ide-ide yang setara.

**Cara Kerja:**
- Menggunakan **grammatical pattern yang sama** untuk ide sejenis
- Creates balance dan makes text memorable

**Rules:**
- Noun with noun
- Verb with verb
- Adjective with adjective
- Clause with clause

**Contoh Benar:**
> "The program aims **to reduce** poverty, **to improve** healthcare, and **to promote** education."

Semua menggunakan **to + verb** → PARALLEL ✅

**Contoh Salah:**
> "The program aims **to reduce** poverty, **improving** healthcare, and **education promotion**."

Mixed structures (to+verb, -ing, noun) → NOT PARALLEL ❌

**Parallel Structures dalam Berbagai Bentuk:**

**a) List dengan Items Paralel**
> "Successful students are **organized**, **motivated**, and **disciplined**."

Semua adjective → parallel

**b) Comparison dengan Struktur Paralel**
> "Reading books is more beneficial than **watching** TV."
> 
> NOT: "Reading books is more beneficial than **to watch** TV." ❌

**c) Correlative Conjunctions**
- Not only... but also...
- Either... or...
- Neither... nor...
- Both... and...

**Contoh:**
> "The policy will benefit **not only** students **but also** teachers."

**Why Parallel Structures Matter:**
- Easier to read dan process
- Creates expectation (pembaca tahu format berikutnya)
- Emphasizes equal importance of items

**Dalam Soal SNBT:**
- Kalau ada list, check parallelism!
- Kalau nggak paralel → might be wrong answer atau irrelevant sentence

---

#### **5. TRANSITION WORDS dan PHRASES (Kata/Frasa Transisi)**

**Fungsi:** Explicitly show hubungan logis antar ide.

**Cara Kerja:**
- Signal jenis hubungan: addition, contrast, cause-effect, time, example, dll
- Guide pembaca through the text's logic

**Ini adalah SUPER IMPORTANT coherence device!**

**Kategori Lengkap Transition Words:**

---

**A) ADDITION/CONTINUATION (Menambah Informasi)**
- Moreover, furthermore, in addition, additionally, besides, also, as well
- Similarly, likewise, equally important

**Contoh:**
> "Regular exercise improves physical health. **Moreover**, it enhances mental well-being."

Signal: kita akan add more positive info tentang exercise.

---

**B) CONTRAST/OPPOSITION (Menunjukkan Kontras)**
- However, nevertheless, nonetheless, yet, still, on the other hand
- In contrast, conversely, whereas, while, although, though, despite

**Contoh:**
> "Technology has improved communication. **However**, it has also reduced face-to-face interactions."

Signal: kita akan present downside/opposite view.

**Subtle Difference:**
- **However** = strong contrast, unexpected twist
- **On the other hand** = balanced comparison, different perspective
- **Nevertheless** = contrast tapi outcome tetap sama

---

**C) CAUSE-EFFECT/RESULT (Sebab-Akibat)**
- Therefore, thus, hence, consequently, as a result, accordingly
- For this reason, because of this, due to this

**Contoh:**
> "Air pollution reached dangerous levels. **Consequently**, schools were closed for three days."

Signal: kita akan show result dari situation sebelumnya.

**Positioning Matters:**
- **Because** = di awal/tengah kalimat: "**Because** of rain, the game was cancelled."
- **Therefore, thus** = di tengah/akhir: "It rained; **therefore**, the game was cancelled."

---

**D) TIME SEQUENCE (Urutan Waktu)**
- First(ly), second(ly), third(ly), finally, lastly
- Then, next, after that, afterward, subsequently
- Meanwhile, simultaneously, at the same time
- Previously, formerly, earlier, before, later

**Contoh:**
> "**First**, heat the oil. **Then**, add the vegetables. **Finally**, season with salt."

Signal: kita akan follow chronological steps.

---

**E) EXAMPLE/ILLUSTRATION (Memberi Contoh)**
- For example, for instance, such as, like, including
- To illustrate, namely, specifically, in particular

**Contoh:**
> "Many countries face water scarcity. **For instance**, Cape Town nearly ran out of water in 2018."

Signal: kita akan provide concrete example.

**Note:**
- **Such as** + noun: "Countries **such as** Japan and Korea..."
- **For example** + full clause: "**For example**, Japan has advanced technology."

---

**F) SUMMARY/CONCLUSION (Menyimpulkan)**
- In conclusion, to sum up, in summary, in short, overall
- All in all, on the whole, ultimately

**Contoh:**
> "**In conclusion**, renewable energy is the key to sustainable development."

Signal: ini adalah wrap-up dari discussion.

---

**G) EMPHASIS/CLARIFICATION (Menekankan/Memperjelas)**
- Indeed, in fact, actually, certainly, surely
- That is (to say), in other words, namely, specifically

**Contoh:**
> "The project failed. **In fact**, it was the company's biggest disaster."

Signal: kita akan emphasize/make the point stronger.

---

**H) COMPARISON (Membandingkan)**
- Similarly, likewise, in the same way, equally, just as

**Contoh:**
> "Dogs are loyal pets. **Similarly**, cats provide companionship."

Signal: kita akan show similarity.

---

**Strategic Use of Transitions:**

**1. Position Matters**
- Beginning of sentence: strong signal, easy to spot
- Middle of sentence: smoother, less abrupt
- Between paragraphs: crucial untuk paragraph connection

**2. Not Every Sentence Needs One**
- Overuse = annoying, mechanical
- Use when relationship nggak obvious dari content alone

**3. Match Transition dengan Logic**
- Kalau content shows contrast, gunakan "however", bukan "moreover"
- Kalau wrong transition → destroys coherence!

---

### **Combining Multiple Coherence Devices**

**Good writers combine devices untuk maximum effect:**

**Contoh Teks dengan Multiple Devices:**

> "**Climate change** poses serious threats. **This environmental crisis** affects every nation. **The problem** has worsened over recent decades. **Moreover**, scientists predict **it** will intensify unless we take action. **Therefore**, governments must implement **renewable energy policies**."

Devices used:
1. **Repeated key terms**: Climate change → environmental crisis → the problem → it
2. **Pronouns**: it (refers to climate change)
3. **Transition words**: Moreover (addition), Therefore (cause-effect)
4. **Synonyms**: climate change = environmental crisis = the problem

---

### **Analyzing Coherence Devices dalam Soal SNBT**

**Strategi Saat Baca Teks:**

**1. Highlight/Tandai Devices**
- Circle pronouns dan cari antecedent
- Underline transition words
- Tandai repeated key terms

**2. Track Connections**
- Setiap pronoun → ke noun mana?
- Setiap transition word → tunjukkan hubungan apa?
- Repeated terms → same concept atau beda?

**3. Check Logical Flow**
- Apakah devices match dengan actual relationship?
- Apakah ada gap dalam connections?

**Common Mistakes to Avoid:**

**Mistake #1: Pronoun tanpa Clear Antecedent**
> "The teacher told the student that she was wrong."

Siapa yang salah? Teacher atau student? AMBIGUOUS!

**Mistake #2: Wrong Transition Word**
> "Exercise is healthy. **Therefore**, many people avoid it."

**Therefore** implies cause-effect, but content shows contrast! Should be **However**.

**Mistake #3: Broken Chain**
> "Pollution is increasing. Renewable energy is the solution."

Missing link! Better: "Pollution is increasing. **To address this**, renewable energy is the solution."

---

### **Practice Tips**

**Saat latihan soal:**

✅ **Identify semua coherence devices** dalam teks
✅ **Verify connections**: apakah devices benar-benar connect ideas?
✅ **Look for missing links**: kalau terasa loncat, mungkin missing transition
✅ **Check consistency**: apakah devices used throughout text?

**Remember:**
> **"Coherence devices are road signs that guide readers through your text. Without them, readers get lost!"**

## **Materi 2.3.3: Analisis Paragraph Unity**

### **Apa Itu Paragraph Unity?**

**Unity** dalam paragraf adalah prinsip bahwa **semua kalimat dalam satu paragraf HARUS mendukung SATU main idea (topic sentence)**. Tidak boleh ada informasi yang "nyeleweng" atau off-topic.

Bayangkan paragraf seperti **tim sepak bola**: semua pemain harus bekerja sama untuk satu tujuan (mencetak gol). Kalau ada pemain yang tiba-tiba main bola basket di tengah lapangan, ya jelas nggak nyambung kan? Itulah yang terjadi kalau paragraph nggak punya unity!

**The Golden Rule of Unity:**
> **"One paragraph = One main idea. Period."**

### **Mengapa Unity Penting?**

**1. Clarity (Kejelasan)**
- Pembaca tahu exactly apa yang dibahas
- Tidak confused dengan informasi random

**2. Focus (Fokus)**
- Paragraph stays on track
- Tidak ke mana-mana

**3. Effectiveness (Efektivitas)**
- Setiap kalimat punya purpose jelas
- Tidak ada "kata sia-sia"

**4. Reader-Friendly**
- Easy to follow
- Pleasant reading experience

### **Komponen Penting untuk Unity**

---

#### **1. TOPIC SENTENCE (Kalimat Topik)**

**Definisi:** Kalimat yang menyatakan **main idea** atau controlling idea dari paragraf.

**Karakteristik Topic Sentence:**
- ✅ **General** (umum), tapi **specific enough** untuk satu paragraf
- ✅ **Complete sentence** dengan subjek dan predikat
- ✅ **Controlling idea** yang jelas (aspek spesifik yang akan dibahas)
- ✅ Menentukan **scope** (cakupan) pembahasan paragraf

**Fungsi:**
- Announces topik utama
- Sets direction untuk kalimat-kalimat selanjutnya
- Creates expectation untuk pembaca

**Posisi Topic Sentence:**

**a) Di Awal Paragraf (PALING UMUM - 80% kasus)**

**Contoh:**
> "**Regular exercise provides numerous health benefits.** It strengthens the cardiovascular system, reducing the risk of heart disease. Exercise also improves mental health by releasing endorphins. Additionally, physical activity helps maintain healthy body weight."

**Topic sentence:** "Regular exercise provides numerous health benefits."
- **Topic:** Regular exercise
- **Controlling idea:** provides numerous health benefits
- **Scope:** kalimat selanjutnya akan explain health benefits apa saja

**Why at the beginning?**
- Direct dan straightforward
- Reader langsung tahu what to expect
- Most common pattern dalam academic writing

---

**b) Di Tengah Paragraf (JARANG - 10% kasus)**

**Contoh:**
> "Many students struggle with time management. They often procrastinate and leave assignments until the last minute. **Effective time management is crucial for academic success.** Students who plan their schedules perform better on exams. They also experience less stress and anxiety."

**Topic sentence:** "Effective time management is crucial for academic success."

Positioned after **background/context** sentences.

**Why in the middle?**
- Builds up to main point
- Provides context first
- Creates transition dari problem ke solution

---

**c) Di Akhir Paragraf (SANGAT JARANG - 5% kasus)**

**Contoh:**
> "Students arrive late to class. Assignments are submitted past deadlines. Participation in discussions is minimal. Exam scores have declined significantly. **It is clear that student engagement has decreased dramatically this semester.**"

**Topic sentence:** "It is clear that student engagement has decreased dramatically this semester."

Positioned as **conclusion/summary** dari examples sebelumnya.

**Why at the end?**
- Inductive reasoning (specific examples → general conclusion)
- Builds evidence first, then states conclusion
- Rhetorical effect: makes conclusion more powerful

---

**d) Tersirat/Implied (VERY RARE - 5% kasus)**

**Contoh:**
> "The alarm rings at 5 AM. She drags herself out of bed. Coffee is the only thing that helps. The commute takes an hour through heavy traffic. By 9 AM, she's already exhausted. Eight hours of meetings await."

**Implied topic:** "Her daily routine is exhausting."

Tidak ada explicit topic sentence, tapi pembaca bisa infer main idea dari accumulation of details.

**Why implied?**
- Literary/narrative writing style
- Creates mood/atmosphere
- Reader actively engages dengan text

**Note untuk SNBT:** Implied topic sentence JARANG muncul di teks akademik/informatif yang biasa diujikan. Tapi tetap penting tahu konsepnya!

---

**Tips Mengidentifikasi Topic Sentence:**

**Ciri-ciri:**
1. ✅ Paling **general** dalam paragraf
2. ✅ **Forecasts** isi paragraf
3. ✅ Bisa **stand alone** dan masih make sense
4. ✅ Kalimat lain **support/develop** kalimat ini

**Quick Test:**
> **"Kalau kalimat ini dihilangkan, apakah paragraf kehilangan direction?"**
> 
> Kalau **YES** → itu topic sentence!

---

#### **2. SUPPORTING SENTENCES (Kalimat Pendukung)**

**Definisi:** Kalimat-kalimat yang mengembangkan, menjelaskan, atau membuktikan topic sentence.

**Fungsi:**
- **Develop** main idea dengan details
- **Support** claim di topic sentence
- **Elaborate** concept yang disebutkan

**Jenis-jenis Supporting Sentences:**

---

**a) EXAMPLES (Contoh Konkret)**

**Fungsi:** Memberikan ilustrasi spesifik dari general statement.

**Signal words:** for example, for instance, such as, like, including

**Contoh:**
> **Topic Sentence:** "Technology has revolutionized education."
> 
> **Supporting with Examples:**
> "For example, students can now access online courses from universities worldwide. Virtual reality allows them to experience historical events firsthand. Additionally, AI tutors provide personalized learning support 24/7."

**Why effective?**
- Makes abstract concept concrete
- Easier to understand dan remember
- Provides proof

---

**b) FACTS dan STATISTICS (Data Objektif)**

**Fungsi:** Memberikan bukti numerik atau verifiable information.

**Signal words:** research shows, studies indicate, according to, data reveals

**Contoh:**
> **Topic Sentence:** "Air pollution poses serious health risks."
> 
> **Supporting with Facts:**
> "According to WHO, air pollution causes 7 million premature deaths annually. Research shows that prolonged exposure increases lung cancer risk by 30%. Studies also link polluted air to heart disease and stroke."

**Why effective?**
- Adds credibility
- Objective dan measurable
- Hard to dispute

---

**c) EXPERT OPINIONS dan QUOTATIONS (Pendapat Ahli)**

**Fungsi:** Menggunakan authority untuk strengthen argument.

**Signal words:** experts claim, Dr. X states, according to Professor Y

**Contoh:**
> **Topic Sentence:** "Climate change requires immediate action."
> 
> **Supporting with Expert Opinion:**
> "Dr. Jane Smith, a leading climatologist, warns that 'we have less than a decade to prevent irreversible damage.' The UN Secretary-General called climate change 'the defining issue of our time.'"

**Why effective?**
- Leverages expertise
- Adds weight to argument
- Appeals to authority

---

**d) DESCRIPTIONS dan DETAILS (Deskripsi Rinci)**

**Fungsi:** Menjelaskan characteristics, features, atau aspects.

**Contoh:**
> **Topic Sentence:** "The rainforest ecosystem is incredibly diverse."
> 
> **Supporting with Description:**
> "The canopy layer rises 100 feet high, forming a dense roof. Below, the understory remains dim and humid. The forest floor is covered with decomposing leaves. Thousands of insect species inhabit every layer."

**Why effective?**
- Creates vivid mental image
- Helps reader visualize
- Engages senses

---

**e) EXPLANATIONS dan REASONS (Penjelasan Mengapa/Bagaimana)**

**Fungsi:** Menganalisis cause, effect, process, atau reason.

**Signal words:** because, since, due to, as a result of, this is caused by

**Contoh:**
> **Topic Sentence:** "Regular sleep is essential for cognitive function."
> 
> **Supporting with Explanation:**
> "During sleep, the brain consolidates memories from the day. This process strengthens neural connections, improving recall. Sleep also clears toxins that accumulate during waking hours. Without sufficient rest, attention span and decision-making abilities decline."

**Why effective?**
- Satisfies reader's "why?"
- Shows deep understanding
- Demonstrates critical thinking

---

**Karakteristik Good Supporting Sentences:**

**1. RELEVANT (Relevan)**
- Directly relates to topic sentence
- Stays within scope
- Doesn't introduce new main topics

**2. SPECIFIC (Spesifik)**
- Provides concrete details
- Not too general/vague
- Adds substantial information

**3. SUFFICIENT (Cukup)**
- Enough support untuk make point convincing
- Not too little (underdeveloped)
- Not too much (repetitive)

**4. ORGANIZED (Terorganisir)**
- Logical order
- Smooth transitions between supports
- Clear progression

---
**How Many Supporting Sentences?**

**No fixed rule**, tapi generally:
- **Minimum:** 2-3 kalimat (atau paragraf terlalu pendek)
- **Typical:** 4-6 kalimat untuk paragraf medium
- **Maximum:** depends on complexity, but avoid overly long paragraphs

**Quality > Quantity**
- Better to have **3 strong supports** than 7 weak ones
- Each sentence harus add value

---

#### **3. NO IRRELEVANT INFORMATION (Tidak Ada Informasi Tidak Relevan)**

**Ini adalah KUNCI unity!**

**Irrelevant Information = Information yang:**
- ❌ **Off-topic**: tidak relate dengan main idea
- ❌ **Too general**: vague dan tidak add specific value
- ❌ **Too detailed**: minor detail yang tidak penting
- ❌ **Out of scope**: beyond what topic sentence promises

**Contoh Paragraph WITHOUT Unity:**

> "**Social media affects teenagers' mental health.** Many spend hours on Instagram and TikTok. Studies show increased anxiety among heavy users. **My grandmother doesn't understand social media.** Cyberbullying has become more common. **I prefer reading books to scrolling feeds.** Research links excessive use to depression."

**Analisis:**
- ✅ Sentence 2, 3, 5, 7: **RELEVANT** - directly support main idea
- ❌ Sentence 4: **IRRELEVANT** - nenek nggak understand ≠ teenage mental health
- ❌ Sentence 6: **IRRELEVANT** - personal preference ≠ general impact

**Contoh Paragraph WITH Unity:**

> "**Social media affects teenagers' mental health.** Many spend hours on Instagram and TikTok, comparing themselves to filtered images. Studies show increased anxiety among heavy users, particularly regarding body image. Cyberbullying has become more common, with teens facing harassment online. Research links excessive use to depression and feelings of inadequacy."

**Analisis:**
- ✅ **All sentences** relate to impact on mental health
- ✅ **Consistent focus**: teenage users dan psychological effects
- ✅ **No tangents**: tetap pada scope yang dijanjikan topic sentence

---

**Why Do Irrelevant Sentences Appear?**

**1. Writer loses focus**
- Terlalu excited tentang related (but different) topic
- Association leads away dari main point

**2. Mixing personal dengan general**
- Topic sentence: general claim
- Irrelevant sentence: personal anecdote yang nggak illustrate point

**3. Including interesting but unrelated facts**
- "Fun fact" yang menarik tapi tidak support main idea

**4. Unclear topic sentence**
- Kalau topic sentence vague, sulit tahu apa yang relevant

---

**How to Spot Irrelevant Sentences:**

**Test #1: The Alignment Test**
> **"Does this sentence directly support or develop the topic sentence?"**
> 
> - If **YES** → keep
> - If **NO** atau **UNSURE** → likely irrelevant

**Test #2: The Removal Test**
> **"If I remove this sentence, does the paragraph lose essential information?"**
> 
> - If **YES** → relevant
> - If **NO** (paragraph still complete) → likely irrelevant

**Test #3: The Question Test**
> **"Does this sentence answer the question raised by the topic sentence?"**
> 
> Topic: "Exercise benefits health." Question: "What are the health benefits?"
> 
> - Sentence: "Exercise strengthens muscles." → **RELEVANT** (answers question)
> - Sentence: "Gyms are expensive." → **IRRELEVANT** (doesn't answer question)

**Test #4: The Keyword Test**
> **"Does this sentence contain keywords related to the main topic?"**
> 
> - If shares topic keywords → likely relevant
> - If introduces totally different keywords → likely irrelevant

---

**Contoh Analisis dalam Soal SNBT:**

**Paragraf:**
[1] Deforestation contributes significantly to climate change. [2] Trees absorb carbon dioxide, a major greenhouse gas. [3] When forests are cleared, this carbon is released into the atmosphere. [4] Amazon rainforest is home to indigenous tribes. [5] Deforestation also reduces Earth's capacity to absorb future emissions.

**Question:** Which sentence is NOT relevant to the paragraph?

**Analisis:**
- **Topic sentence:** Deforestation contributes to climate change
- **Scope:** connection between deforestation dan climate impact

**Sentence by sentence:**
- [1] ✅ **Topic sentence** - establishes main idea
- [2] ✅ **Relevant** - explains mechanism (trees absorb CO2)
- [3] ✅ **Relevant** - explains what happens when deforested (carbon released)
- [4] ❌ **IRRELEVANT** - indigenous tribes ≠ climate change impact
- [5] ✅ **Relevant** - further climate impact (reduced absorption capacity)

**Answer:** Sentence [4]

**Why irrelevant?**
- Topic: deforestation + climate
- Sentence [4]: indigenous tribes (social/cultural aspect, bukan climate impact)
- Does NOT support controlling idea tentang climate change contribution

---

#### **4. CONSISTENCY dalam FOCUS**

**Definisi:** Paragraf harus maintain consistent focus dari awal sampai akhir. Tidak boleh berpindah-pindah topik.

**The Rule:**
> **"Start with one main idea, develop ONLY that idea, end with that idea."**

**Contoh INCONSISTENT Focus:**

> "**Online learning offers flexibility for students.** They can study at their own pace from anywhere. This saves commuting time. However, traditional classrooms provide face-to-face interaction. Teachers can immediately answer questions. **Online learning requires strong self-discipline and motivation.**"

**Problem:**
- Mulai: benefits of online learning (flexibility)
- Tengah: **switches** to benefits of traditional classroom (wait, what?)
- Akhir: back to online learning (challenges)

**Inconsistent!** Paragraf confused antara comparing online vs traditional OR explaining online learning benefits.

**Contoh CONSISTENT Focus:**

> "**Online learning offers flexibility for students.** They can study at their own pace, pausing and rewinding lectures as needed. Students can access materials from anywhere with internet connection, eliminating commuting time. This flexibility particularly benefits working professionals and parents who manage multiple responsibilities. The asynchronous format allows learners to study during their most productive hours."

**Analysis:**
- ✅ **Start:** online learning flexibility
- ✅ **Middle:** ALL sentences explain aspects of flexibility
- ✅ **End:** still about flexibility benefits
- ✅ **Consistent focus** throughout!

---

**Types of Focus Shifts to AVOID:**

**1. Topic Shift**
> Topic sentence: "Exercise improves health."
> 
> Irrelevant shift: "Many gyms offer membership discounts."

**Problem:** Shifts dari health benefits → gym pricing (different topic)

---

**2. Aspect Shift**
> Topic sentence: "Solar energy is environmentally friendly."
> 
> Irrelevant shift: "Solar panel installation costs vary by region."

**Problem:** Shifts dari environmental benefits → economic cost (different aspect)

---

**3. Scope Shift**
> Topic sentence: "Teenagers use social media extensively."
> 
> Irrelevant shift: "The internet was invented in the 1960s."

**Problem:** Shifts dari specific (teenager usage) → overly broad (internet history)

---

**4. Time Shift (without justification)**
> Topic sentence: "Current education system faces challenges."
> 
> Irrelevant shift: "In ancient Greece, education focused on philosophy."

**Problem:** Shifts dari present problems → historical context (unless connection is made clear)

---

**How to Maintain Focus:**

**Strategy #1: Constant Reference Back**
- Frequently use keywords dari topic sentence
- Use pronouns yang clearly refer to main topic

**Strategy #2: Transition Phrases That Maintain Topic**
- "This benefit also..." (keeps talking about benefits)
- "Another advantage..." (stays on advantages)
- "Similarly..." (maintains parallel discussion)

**Strategy #3: Avoid Tangents**
- If a sentence makes you think "Wait, why are we talking about this now?" → probably off-focus
- Stay disciplined: ONLY information that develops main idea

**Strategy #4: Mental Check**
> After each sentence, ask: **"Am I still talking about [main idea]?"**

---

#### **5. CONCLUDING SENTENCE (Optional tapi Powerful)**

**Definisi:** Kalimat penutup yang reinforces main point atau provides smooth transition.

**NOT every paragraph needs concluding sentence**, tapi when used effectively, it strengthens unity.

**Functions of Concluding Sentence:**

---

**a) RESTATE Main Idea (in different words)**

**Contoh:**
> **Topic Sentence:** "Regular exercise provides numerous health benefits."
> 
> **Supporting Sentences:** [explain cardiovascular, mental, weight benefits]
> 
> **Concluding Sentence:** "Clearly, maintaining an active lifestyle is essential for overall well-being."

**Effect:** Reinforces main point, gives sense of completion.

---

**b) SUMMARIZE Key Points**

**Contoh:**
> **Topic Sentence:** "Three factors contribute to academic success."
> 
> **Supporting Sentences:** [explain motivation, time management, study habits]
> 
> **Concluding Sentence:** "Together, motivation, effective time management, and consistent study habits form the foundation for achieving academic goals."

**Effect:** Ties together multiple supports into unified conclusion.

---

**c) PROVIDE Transition to Next Paragraph**

**Contoh:**
> **Concluding Sentence:** "While these benefits are clear, the implementation challenges require careful consideration."

**Effect:** Bridges to next paragraph yang will discuss challenges.

---

**d) MAKE Final Comment atau Implication**

**Contoh:**
> **Concluding Sentence:** "Given these serious health risks, reducing air pollution should be a global priority."

**Effect:** Extends beyond facts to broader significance atau call to action.

---

**When to Use Concluding Sentence:**

**USE when:**
- ✅ Paragraph is **long** dan complex (helps wrap up)
- ✅ Want to **emphasize** main point
- ✅ Need **transition** to next paragraph
- ✅ Making **important** argument yang deserves reinforcement

**DON'T use when:**
- ❌ Paragraph is **short** dan simple (would be redundant)
- ❌ Next paragraph continues same point (would interrupt flow)
- ❌ Would just **repeat** topic sentence exactly (adds nothing)

---

**Signals of Concluding Sentences:**

**Conclusive Phrases:**
- In conclusion, to sum up, in summary, clearly, thus, therefore
- All in all, overall, evidently, it is clear that

**Caution:**
- "In conclusion" is TOO FORMAL untuk mid-essay paragraphs
- Use for essay conclusion, not every paragraph conclusion
- Lighter signals better: "thus," "therefore," "clearly"

---

### **Checking Paragraph Unity: Step-by-Step Method**

**When analyzing a paragraph, follow these steps:**

**STEP 1: Identify Topic Sentence**
> ❓ **"What is the main idea of this paragraph?"**
> 
> Find the sentence that states general controlling idea.

**STEP 2: Check Each Supporting Sentence**
> ❓ **"Does this sentence develop/support the main idea?"**
> 
> Apply the 4 tests: Alignment, Removal, Question, Keyword.

**STEP 3: Look for Irrelevant Information**
> ❓ **"Is there anything off-topic, too general, or out of scope?"**
> 
> Identify sentences that don't fit.

**STEP 4: Check Focus Consistency**
> ❓ **"Does the paragraph stay on one topic, or does it shift?"**
> 
> Ensure no mid-paragraph topic changes.

**STEP 5: Evaluate Concluding Sentence (if present)**
> ❓ **"Does it effectively wrap up or transition?"**
> 
> Check if adds value or is redundant.

---

### **Common Unity Violations in SNBT**

**Violation #1: The "Interesting Tangent"**
> Paragraph about climate solutions suddenly includes: "Many people enjoy outdoor activities."

**Why wrong:** Interesting but irrelevant to solutions.

---

**Violation #2: The "Personal Intrusion"**
> Paragraph about general social media impacts includes: "I only use Instagram once a week."

**Why wrong:** Personal habit ≠ general impact discussion.

---

**Violation #3: The "Historical Detour"**
> Paragraph about current problems includes: "This issue began in the 1800s."

**Why wrong:** Unless history is relevant to current discussion, it's off-topic.

---

**Violation #4: The "Definition Dump"**
> Paragraph about benefits includes: "The dictionary defines this as..."

**Why wrong:** Formal definition often unnecessary if context clear.

---

**Violation #5: The "Opposite Topic"**
> Paragraph about advantages suddenly discusses disadvantages.

**Why wrong:** Different controlling idea = different paragraph.

---

### **Practice Technique untuk Soal**

**Saat mengerjakan soal "Which sentence is NOT relevant":**

**1. Read Topic Sentence First**
- Identify main idea dan scope

**2. Predict What Should Be Discussed**
- Based on topic sentence, what topics are relevant?

**3. Check Each Sentence**
- Does it fit prediction?
- Apply the tests

**4. Eliminate Clearly Relevant Ones**
- Focus on questionable ones

**5. Choose Most Irrelevant**
- May be more than one slightly off, choose MOST off-topic

---

### **Remember:**

> **"Unity means every sentence in the paragraph works together to develop ONE main idea. If a sentence doesn't contribute to that idea, it doesn't belong—no matter how interesting or true it is."**

**The Checklist:**
- ✅ **One topic sentence** with clear main idea
- ✅ **Supporting sentences** that develop that idea
- ✅ **No irrelevant** information
- ✅ **Consistent focus** throughout
- ✅ **(Optional) Concluding sentence** that reinforces or transitions

**Master unity, and you'll ace those "irrelevant sentence" questions!** 🎯

## **Materi 2.3.4: Analisis Logical Flow Between Sentences**

### **Apa Itu Logical Flow?**

**Logical flow** adalah "aliran logis" dari satu kalimat ke kalimat berikutnya. Bayangkan seperti aliran sungai: air mengalir smooth dari hulu ke hilir, tanpa tiba-tiba loncat ke tempat lain atau terputus.

Dalam teks, **logical flow** berarti:
- Setiap kalimat **naturally leads** ke kalimat berikutnya
- Hubungan antar kalimat **clear dan masuk akal**
- Tidak ada **sudden jumps** atau **gaps** dalam reasoning
- Reader bisa **easily follow** thought progression

**Why It Matters:**
- Teks dengan good flow = easy dan pleasant to read
- Teks dengan poor flow = confusing dan frustrating
- SNBT sering test kemampuan identify flow problems!

---

### **Prinsip Dasar: Sentence-to-Sentence Connection**

**The Golden Rule:**
> **"Each sentence should connect to the previous sentence AND set up the next sentence."**

Think of it as **relay race**:
- Kalimat 1 passes "baton" (information/idea) to Kalimat 2
- Kalimat 2 takes baton, adds to it, passes to Kalimat 3
- Dan seterusnya...

**Jika ada kalimat yang:**
- ❌ **Tidak menerima baton** dari kalimat sebelumnya = disconnected
- ❌ **Tidak pass baton** ke kalimat selanjutnya = dead end
- ❌ **Nerima baton tapi lempar ke arah lain** = illogical shift

Maka flow **RUSAK**!

---

### **Pattern #1: OLD INFORMATION → NEW INFORMATION**

Ini adalah **THE MOST FUNDAMENTAL PATTERN** untuk logical flow!

**Concept:**
- **Old/Given information** (sudah disebutkan) = di **awal** kalimat baru
- **New information** (belum disebutkan) = di **akhir** kalimat baru

**Why?**
- Pembaca already familiar dengan old info → easy entry point
- New info introduced gradually → easier to process
- Creates **chain** dari kalimat ke kalimat

**Formula:**
```
Sentence 1: [Topic] + [New Information A]
Sentence 2: [Information A - now old] + [New Information B]
Sentence 3: [Information B - now old] + [New Information C]
```

**Contoh BAIK (Good Flow):**

> **Sentence 1:** "Climate change threatens global ecosystems."
> 
> **Sentence 2:** "These **ecosystems** support millions of species." ← (ecosystems = old info dari S1)
> 
> **Sentence 3:** "Many **species** face extinction without urgent action." ← (species = old info dari S2)

**Analysis:**
- S1 introduces: **ecosystems** (new)
- S2 picks up: **ecosystems** (now old) → adds: **species** (new)
- S3 picks up: **species** (now old) → adds: **extinction** (new)

**Chain:** Climate change → ecosystems → species → extinction
**Flow:** SMOOTH! ✅

---

**Contoh BURUK (Poor Flow):**

> **Sentence 1:** "Climate change threatens global ecosystems."
> 
> **Sentence 2:** "Renewable energy can reduce carbon emissions." ← (wait, where did this come from?)
> 
> **Sentence 3:** "Many people prefer solar panels over wind turbines." ← (even more random!)

**Analysis:**
- S1: ecosystems (new)
- S2: renewable energy (TOTALLY NEW, tidak pick up dari S1) ❌
- S3: solar vs wind (TIDAK connect to S2) ❌

**Chain:** BROKEN! Each sentence introduces new topic without connecting.
**Flow:** CHOPPY! ❌

---

**How to Apply This Pattern:**

**Technique #1: Repeat Keywords**
> "**Dogs** are loyal companions. **These animals** form strong bonds..."

**Technique #2: Use Pronouns**
> "The **scientist** made a discovery. **She** announced it..."

**Technique #3: Use Synonyms**
> "**Solar power** is renewable. This **energy source** is also clean..."

**Technique #4: Use Demonstratives**
> "Pollution harms health. **This problem** requires solutions..."

---

### **Pattern #2: GIVEN-NEW CONTRACT**

Ini adalah extension dari old→new pattern, tapi lebih sophisticated.

**The Contract:**
- **Given** (yang sudah known/established) = front-load di sentence
- **New** (yang belum known) = end-load di sentence

**Why "Contract"?**
- It's an **implicit agreement** between writer dan reader
- Reader **expects** familiar info first, new info later
- Violating this contract = confusing reader

**Structure:**
```
[GIVEN: anchor to previous context] + [NEW: advance the discussion]
```

**Contoh:**

**Topic:** Education reform

> **S1:** "Traditional education methods need updating."
> 
> **S2:** "**These methods** rely heavily on rote memorization." 
> - GIVEN: "these methods" (refers to traditional methods dari S1)
> - NEW: "rote memorization" (new information added)
> 
> **S3:** "**Rote memorization**, however, does not develop critical thinking."
> - GIVEN: "rote memorization" (now old, dari S2)
> - NEW: "does not develop critical thinking" (new critique)
> 
> **S4:** "**Critical thinking** is essential for 21st-century skills."
> - GIVEN: "critical thinking" (dari S3)
> - NEW: "essential for 21st-century skills" (new context/importance)

**Analysis:**
- Each sentence anchors to previous via GIVEN
- Each sentence advances via NEW
- Creates **progressive development** of ideas

---

**Violations of Given-New Contract:**

**Violation #1: All New**
> "Traditional methods need updating. Project-based learning improves engagement."

**Problem:** "Project-based learning" is completely new, no bridge from "traditional methods".

**Fix:** Add given element
> "Traditional methods need updating. **To address this**, project-based learning improves engagement."

---

**Violation #2: All Given (Redundant)**
> "Dogs are loyal. Dogs are loyal companions."

**Problem:** Second sentence doesn't add new information, just repeats.

---

**Violation #3: New-Given Order (Backward)**
> "Critical thinking skills are developed through project-based learning. This approach..."

**Better:** Front-load the given
> "Project-based learning develops critical thinking skills. These skills..."

---

### **Pattern #3: CAUSE-EFFECT RELATIONSHIPS**

Logical flow often follows **cause → effect** atau **effect → cause** pattern.

**Type A: Cause → Effect**

**Structure:**
```
[CAUSE sentence] → [EFFECT sentence]
```

**Contoh:**
> "Deforestation destroys natural habitats. **As a result**, many species face extinction."

**Signal words:** therefore, thus, consequently, as a result, for this reason, hence

**Flow:** Clear causal link dari cause (deforestation) ke effect (extinction).

---

**Type B: Effect → Cause**

**Structure:**
```
[EFFECT sentence] → [CAUSE sentence]
```

**Contoh:**
> "Air quality in the city has deteriorated significantly. **This decline** stems from increased vehicle emissions."

**Signal words:** because, since, due to, owing to, caused by, results from

**Flow:** State effect first, then explain cause.

---

**Multiple Cause-Effect Chains:**

Complex topics involve **chains** of causation:

> "Rising temperatures **cause** glaciers to melt. This melting **leads to** rising sea levels. Higher sea levels **result in** coastal flooding. Flooding **forces** communities to relocate."

**Chain:** Temperature rise → glacier melt → sea level rise → flooding → relocation

**Each link** creates smooth flow to next!

---

**Jebakan dalam Soal:**

**False Cause:**
> "Sales increased. The CEO wore a red tie."

Implied causation, tapi logically unrelated! Not legitimate cause-effect.

**Reversed Cause-Effect:**
> "Because the project failed, the team worked carelessly."

**Wait!** Careless work is the **cause**, failure is the **effect**. Order wrong!

**Correct:** "Because the team worked carelessly, the project failed."

---

### **Pattern #4: TIME SEQUENCE MAINTENANCE**

Untuk narrative atau process description, **chronological order** creates natural flow.

**Principle:** Events described in order they occurred (past → present → future).

**Contoh BAIK:**
> "**First**, researchers gathered data. **Then**, they analyzed the results. **Finally**, they published their findings."

**Sequence:** 1→2→3 (logical chronological flow)

---

**Contoh BURUK:**
> "Researchers published findings. They had gathered data earlier. Then analysis occurred."

**Problem:** Jumps around in time (3→1→2). Confusing!

---

**Time Signal Words:**

**Sequential:**
- First, second, third, next, then, after that, subsequently, finally, lastly

**Simultaneous:**
- Meanwhile, at the same time, simultaneously, while, during

**Prior:**
- Before, earlier, previously, formerly, prior to

**Later:**
- Later, afterward, eventually, ultimately, in the future

**Maintaining time sequence:**
- Stick to one direction (usually forward)
- Use appropriate time markers
- Don't jump around randomly

---

### **Pattern #5: GENERAL → SPECIFIC (atau Specific → General)**

Logical flow often moves from **broad concept** to **narrow details** (or reverse).

**Type A: General → Specific (DEDUCTIVE)**

**Contoh:**
> **General:** "Exercise benefits health."
> 
> **More Specific:** "Cardiovascular exercise strengthens the heart."
> 
> **Most Specific:** "Running for 30 minutes daily reduces heart disease risk by 25%."

**Flow:** Broad claim → narrower claim → specific data
**Pattern:** Funnel (wide → narrow)

---

**Type B: Specific → General (INDUCTIVE)**

**Contoh:**
> **Specific:** "In Tokyo, public transport moves 8 million people daily."
> 
> **More General:** "Many Asian cities rely on efficient transit systems."
> 
> **Most General:** "Urban areas worldwide face transportation challenges."

**Flow:** Specific example → broader category → universal principle
**Pattern:** Reverse funnel (narrow → wide)

---

**Why This Creates Flow:**

**General → Specific:**
- Establishes framework first
- Details fit within established context
- Reader knows where details belong

**Specific → General:**
- Builds evidence first
- Conclusion feels earned
- Reader sees reasoning process

---

### **Pattern #6: COMPARISON dan CONTRAST**

When comparing, **parallel structure** dan **consistent focus** create flow.

**Effective Comparison Flow:**

**Option 1: Point-by-Point**
> "Online learning offers flexibility. Traditional classrooms provide structure. Online courses suit self-motivated students. Traditional settings benefit those needing guidance."

**Flow:** Alternates between online dan traditional for each point.

---

**Option 2: Block Style**
> "Online learning offers flexibility and accessibility. Students study anywhere, anytime. Traditional classrooms provide structure and social interaction. Students benefit from face-to-face discussion."

**Flow:** Discusses all aspects of online, then all aspects of traditional.

---

**Signal Words for Comparison:**
- Similarly, likewise, in the same way, equally, just as, like

**Signal Words for Contrast:**
- However, in contrast, on the other hand, whereas, while, unlike, conversely

**Maintaining flow:**
- Use consistent criteria for comparison
- Use appropriate signals
- Don't mix comparison styles within same paragraph

---

### **Common Flow Problems dan How to Fix**

---

#### **Problem #1: SUDDEN TOPIC JUMP**

**Bad:**
> "Pollution harms ecosystems. Many people enjoy hiking."

**Problem:** No connection between pollution dan hiking.

**Fix:** Add bridge
> "Pollution harms ecosystems. **To preserve these natural areas**, many people support hiking trail conservation."

**OR:** Add transition
> "Pollution harms ecosystems. **Despite this threat**, many people enjoy hiking in natural areas."

---

#### **Problem #2: MISSING LINK**

**Bad:**
> "The study involved 500 participants. Results showed significant improvement."

**Problem:** What happened between "involved" dan "results"? Study was conducted!

**Fix:** Fill gap
> "The study involved 500 participants **who followed the program for 6 months**. Results showed significant improvement."

---

#### **Problem #3: UNCLEAR REFERENCE**

**Bad:**
> "Smartphones and tablets are popular. They have many apps."

**Problem:** "They" = smartphones? Tablets? Both?

**Fix:** Clarify
> "Smartphones and tablets are popular. **These devices** have many apps."

---

#### **Problem #4: ILLOGICAL SEQUENCE**

**Bad:**
> "Scientists announced the discovery. Researchers conducted experiments."

**Problem:** Announcement before experiment? Time order wrong!

**Fix:** Reorder
> "Researchers conducted experiments. **After analyzing data**, scientists announced the discovery."

---

#### **Problem #5: CONTRADICTORY FLOW**

**Bad:**
> "Exercise is essential for health. Therefore, many people avoid it."

**Problem:** "Therefore" signals cause-effect, but content shows contradiction!

**Fix:** Change connector
> "Exercise is essential for health. **However**, many people avoid it."

---

### **Analyzing Flow dalam Soal SNBT**

**Strategi Step-by-Step:**

**STEP 1: Read Consecutive Sentences Together**
- Don't read in isolation
- Look for connections

**STEP 2: Identify Connection Type**
- Old→New?
- Cause-effect?
- Time sequence?
- Comparison?
- General-specific?

**STEP 3: Check Transition Words**
- Do they match logical relationship?
- "However" harus show contrast, bukan addition!

**STEP 4: Verify Pronoun/Reference**
- What does "this," "it," "they" refer to?
- Is reference clear?

**STEP 5: Test Removal**
- If sentence removed, does flow break?
- If no, might be irrelevant

### **Practice Exercise Approach**

**When given jumbled sentences:**

**Look for:**
1. **Topic sentence** (most general, sets up topic)
2. **Time markers** (first, then, finally)
3. **Pronouns** (must have antecedent before)
4. **Demonstratives** (this, these, such → refer to previous)
5. **Logical connectors** (however, therefore, thus)

**Build chain:**
1. Start with topic sentence
2. Find sentence that builds on it (picks up info)
3. Continue chain until all sentences placed
4. Verify overall flow makes sense

---

### **Pro Tips untuk Flow Analysis**

**Tip #1: Follow the Keywords**
- Track repeated words/synonyms
- They show thematic connection

**Tip #2: Watch Transitions**
- Each transition word signals specific relationship
- Mismatch = flow problem

**Tip #3: Check Consistency**
- Tense consistent? (past vs present)
- Focus consistent? (same topic)
- Tone consistent? (formal vs informal)

**Tip #4: Read Aloud Mentally**
- Does it sound natural?
- Do you stumble anywhere?
- Stumbling points = flow problems

**Tip #5: Question Everything**
- Why is this sentence here?
- How does it connect to previous?
- Does it set up next?

---

### **Remember the Analogy:**

> **"Logical flow is like a smooth highway. Each sentence is a lane merge. Bad flow is like suddenly hitting a speed bump, or the road splitting in weird directions. Good flow feels like cruising effortlessly from start to finish."**

**Master flow analysis, and you'll spot:**
- ✅ Irrelevant sentences (they disrupt flow)
- ✅ Correct sentence order (flow determines sequence)
- ✅ Missing connections (gaps in logic)
- ✅ Best transitions (match logical relationship)

**Flow = The invisible structure that makes reading effortless!** 🌊✨

## **Materi 2.3.5: Analisis Discourse Markers**

### **Apa Itu Discourse Markers?**

**Discourse markers** (juga disebut **transition words/phrases** atau **connectors**) adalah kata atau frasa yang **explicitly signal** hubungan logis antar ide dalam teks.

Bayangkan discourse markers seperti **rambu-rambu jalan** saat kamu nyetir:
- 🚦 "Turn left" = contrast marker (however)
- ➡️ "Continue straight" = continuation marker (moreover)
- ⚠️ "Caution ahead" = emphasis marker (indeed)
- 🏁 "Destination" = conclusion marker (in conclusion)

Tanpa rambu, kamu bisa **tersesat**. Tanpa discourse markers, pembaca bisa **confused** tentang bagaimana ide-ide saling relate!

---

### **Mengapa Discourse Markers Penting?**

**1. CLARITY (Kejelasan)**
- Make logical relationships **explicit**, not implicit
- Remove ambiguity dalam interpretation

**2. COHESION (Keterkaitan)**
- Create **linguistic bonds** antar kalimat
- Show how ideas fit together

**3. GUIDANCE (Panduan)**
- Direct reader's understanding
- Signal what's coming next

**4. SOPHISTICATION (Kecanggihan)**
- Elevate writing dari simple ke sophisticated
- Show mastery of language

**Dalam SNBT:**
- Soal sering test apakah kamu paham **meaning** discourse markers
- Atau apakah marker yang digunakan **match** dengan logical relationship
- **Jebakan**: wrong marker yang grammatically correct tapi logically wrong!

---

### **Kategori Discourse Markers Lengkap**

---

## **KATEGORI 1: LISTING MARKERS (Penanda Urutan/Daftar)**

**Fungsi:** Menunjukkan sequence dalam list atau enumeration.

**Members:**
- First(ly), second(ly), third(ly), fourth(ly), etc.
- Next, then, after that, afterward, subsequently
- Finally, lastly, last of all, in the end

**Usage Context:** Multi-point arguments, process descriptions, sequential steps.

---

### **A) NUMERICAL SEQUENCE (First, Second, Third...)**

**Formal Style:**
> "**Firstly**, education improves employment prospects. **Secondly**, it enhances critical thinking. **Thirdly**, it promotes social mobility. **Finally**, it contributes to economic growth."

**Less Formal Style:**
> "**First**, gather ingredients. **Second**, mix the dry items. **Third**, add wet ingredients. **Finally**, bake for 30 minutes."

**Key Points:**
- ✅ Use **consistent style**: all -ly OR all without -ly, don't mix
- ✅ **Finally** OR **Lastly** for conclusion, not both
- ✅ Great for **organizing multiple points** clearly

**Typical Number:**
- 2-5 points most common
- More than 5 = too complex untuk one paragraph

---

### **B) TEMPORAL SEQUENCE (Then, Next, After That...)**

**Contoh Process Description:**
> "Heat the oil. **Then**, add chopped onions. **Next**, stir in the spices. **After that**, add tomatoes. **Finally**, simmer for 20 minutes."

**Contoh Narrative:**
> "The team prepared for months. **Then**, they entered the competition. **Next**, they advanced to finals. **Subsequently**, they won the championship."

**Key Points:**
- ✅ Indicates **chronological order**
- ✅ Common in **how-to** guides dan **narratives**
- ✅ Maintains **forward time progression**

---

**When to Use Listing Markers:**

**✅ USE when:**
- Presenting multiple distinct points
- Describing step-by-step process
- Want to emphasize organization dan clarity
- Reader needs to track several items

**❌ DON'T overuse:**
- Not every paragraph needs numerical markers
- Can sound mechanical if used excessively
- Informal writing may prefer smoother transitions

---

**Common Mistakes:**

**Mistake #1: Inconsistent Style**
❌ "Firstly... Second... Third..."
✅ "First... Second... Third..." OR "Firstly... Secondly... Thirdly..."

**Mistake #2: Skipping Numbers**
❌ "First... Third... Finally..." (where's second?)
✅ Include all sequential steps

**Mistake #3: Wrong Final Marker**
❌ "First... Second... Thirdly..." (missing finally/lastly)
✅ "First... Second... Third... Finally..."

---

## **KATEGORI 2: EXEMPLIFICATION MARKERS (Penanda Contoh)**

**Fungsi:** Signal bahwa kamu akan provide concrete example atau illustration.

**Members:**
- For example, for instance
- Such as, like, including
- To illustrate, namely, specifically, in particular
- e.g., i.e. (formal written)

---

### **A) FOR EXAMPLE vs FOR INSTANCE**

**Completely interchangeable!** Kedua nya means exactly the same thing.

**Usage with Full Clause:**
> "**For example**, renewable energy reduces carbon emissions."
> 
> "**For instance**, solar panels can power entire homes."

**Position:**
- Beginning of sentence (most common)
- Middle of sentence: "Renewable energy, **for example**, reduces emissions."

**Formality:** Both acceptable dalam academic writing.

---

### **B) SUCH AS vs LIKE**

**Used untuk introduce list of examples (NOT full clause).**

**SUCH AS (More Formal):**
> "Renewable energy **such as** solar and wind power is becoming affordable."

**LIKE (Less Formal):**
> "Countries **like** Denmark and Germany lead in renewable energy."

**Key Difference:**
- **Such as** = exact member dari category
  - "Fruits **such as** apples..." (apples IS a fruit)
- **Like** = similar to
  - "She sings **like** Adele." (she's NOT Adele, just similar)

**In practice:** Often used interchangeably dalam casual context, but SUCH AS safer untuk formal writing.

---

### **C) INCLUDING**

**Introduces non-exhaustive list** (implies ada yang lain juga).

**Contoh:**
> "Many factors contribute, **including** climate change, deforestation, and pollution."

**Implication:** These are SOME factors, not ALL factors.

**vs "Namely"** (exhaustive - all members listed):
> "Three factors contribute, **namely** climate change, deforestation, and pollution."

**Implication:** These are ALL three factors.

---

### **D) TO ILLUSTRATE**

**Stronger, more intentional** than "for example".

**Contoh:**
> "Communication barriers harm teamwork. **To illustrate**, misunderstandings caused the project's 3-month delay."

**Usage:** When you want to emphasize that example is particularly relevant atau illustrative.

---

### **E) SPECIFICALLY / IN PARTICULAR**

**Narrows focus** to particular case or detail.

**Contoh:**
> "The policy affects all students, **specifically** those from low-income families."
> 
> "Climate change threatens biodiversity, **in particular** in tropical regions."

**Function:** Zoom in from general to specific case.

---

### **F) E.G. vs I.E. (Formal Written Only)**

**E.G. (exempli gratia)** = "for example"
> "Citrus fruits (e.g., oranges, lemons) are rich in vitamin C."

**I.E. (id est)** = "that is" / "in other words"
> "The deadline (i.e., March 15) cannot be extended."

**Key Difference:**
- **E.g.** = some examples dari larger set
- **I.e.** = clarification/restatement dari the same thing

**Formatting:**
- Comma BEFORE: ", e.g.,"
- Comma AFTER (American style): "e.g., apples, oranges"
- Lowercase dengan periods

---

**When to Use Exemplification Markers:**

**✅ USE when:**
- Abstract concept needs concrete illustration
- Making general claim that needs evidence
- Want to make idea relatable/understandable
- Listing members of category

**Pro Tip:**
- **One example** often enough (quality > quantity)
- **Multiple examples** for emphasis or completeness
- Make sure examples actually **illustrate** the point!

---

**Common Mistakes:**

**Mistake #1: No Comma Before "For Example"**
❌ "Many countries for example Denmark..."
✅ "Many countries, for example Denmark,..."

**Mistake #2: Full Clause after "Such As"**
❌ "Energy sources such as solar panels reduce emissions."
✅ "Energy sources such as solar and wind reduce emissions."

**Mistake #3: Confusing E.G. dan I.E.**
❌ "Bring warm clothes, i.e., jacket, sweater." (should be e.g.)
✅ "Bring warm clothes, e.g., jacket, sweater."

---

## **KATEGORI 3: ADDITION/CONTINUATION MARKERS**

**Fungsi:** Add more information in same direction; continue line of thought.

**Members:**

**STRONG Addition:**
- Moreover, furthermore, in addition, additionally, besides
- What's more, beyond that, on top of that

**MILD Addition:**
- Also, as well, too
- Equally, equally important

**SIMILAR Idea:**
- Similarly, likewise, in the same way, by the same token

---

### **A) MOREOVER & FURTHERMORE (Strongest Addition)**

**Highly formal;** adds significant supporting information.

**Contoh:**
> "Exercise improves physical health. **Moreover**, it enhances mental well-being."

**Implication:** Second point is **equally** or **more** important.

**Position:** Usually at beginning of sentence.

**Formality Level:** Academic/formal writing ⭐⭐⭐

---

### **B) IN ADDITION & ADDITIONALLY**

**Slightly less formal** than moreover/furthermore; clear addition.

**Contoh:**
> "The program offers job training. **In addition**, participants receive career counseling."

**Synonymous** dengan moreover/furthermore tapi slightly softer.

**Formality Level:** Academic/professional ⭐⭐

---

### **C) BESIDES**

**Informal addition;** implies "not only that, but also..."

**Contoh:**
> "I don't want to go. **Besides**, I have too much work."

**Tone:** Casual, conversational.

**Usage:** Rare dalam formal academic writing; common dalam speaking.

---

### **D) ALSO & AS WELL & TOO**

**Mild, neutral addition.**

**ALSO:**
- Position: **Mid-sentence** (most common)
- "The policy **also** benefits small businesses."

**AS WELL:**
- Position: **End of sentence**
- "The policy benefits small businesses **as well**."

**TOO:**
- Position: **End of sentence**
- "The policy benefits small businesses, **too**."

**Key Point:** These are **less formal** than moreover/furthermore. Perfect untuk softer transitions.

---

### **E) SIMILARLY & LIKEWISE**

**Shows similarity** between two situations.

**SIMILARLY:**
> "Japan invested in technology. **Similarly**, South Korea prioritized innovation."

**LIKEWISE:**
> "Urban areas face pollution. **Likewise**, rural regions experience environmental challenges."

**Function:** Compare parallel situations or actions.

**vs "Also":**
- **Also** = simple addition
- **Similarly/Likewise** = addition WITH comparison

---

**When to Use Addition Markers:**

**✅ USE when:**
- Building cumulative argument (point after point)
- Each sentence adds new supporting evidence
- Want to show multiple benefits/reasons/examples

**❌ AVOID when:**
- Points are contrasting (use "however" instead)
- Repeating same idea (redundant)
- Already have smooth flow without marker

---

**Choosing Right Addition Marker:**

**Formal Context** (Academic essay, research):
→ Moreover, Furthermore, In addition, Additionally

**Neutral Context** (News article, report):
→ Also, In addition, As well

**Informal Context** (Blog, conversation):
→ Also, Besides, Too, Plus

---

## **KATEGORI 4: CONTRAST/OPPOSITION MARKERS**

**Fungsi:** Signal shift ke contrasting atau opposing idea.

**Members:**

**STRONG Contrast:**
- However, nevertheless, nonetheless, yet, still
- On the other hand, in contrast, conversely

**CONCESSION:**
- Although, though, even though, while, whereas
- Despite, in spite of, regardless of, notwithstanding

---

### **A) HOWEVER (Most Common Contrast Marker)**

**Signals unexpected turn atau contradiction.**

**Contoh:**
> "The plan seemed perfect. **However**, it failed in practice."

**Position:**
- **Beginning** of sentence: "**However**, implementation failed."
- **Mid-sentence** (after subject): "Implementation, **however**, failed."

**Punctuation:**
- After "However" at beginning: "However**,** ..."
- Around "However" in middle: ", however, "

**Strength:** Strong contrast; indicates significant shift.

---

### **B) NEVERTHELESS & NONETHELESS**

**Similar to "however" but STRONGER.** Implies "despite what was just said".

**Contoh:**
> "The challenges were enormous. **Nevertheless**, the team succeeded."

**Implication:** Success happened DESPITE challenges (more emphatic than "however").

**NEVERTHELESS vs NONETHELESS:**
- **Completely synonymous!**
- "Nevertheless" slightly more common
- Both very formal

---

### **C) YET & STILL**

**Milder contrast** than however; often implies "surprisingly" or "continuing despite".

**YET:**
> "She studied hard, **yet** failed the exam."

**Implication:** Failure is surprising given the hard study.

**STILL:**
> "The evidence was clear. **Still**, they refused to believe."

**Implication:** Refusal continues despite clear evidence.

**Note:** Both can also be used as time words (not contrast), context determines meaning!

---

### **D) ON THE OTHER HAND**

**Presents alternative perspective** atau balancing view.

**Contoh:**
> "Online learning offers flexibility. **On the other hand**, it requires self-discipline."

**Function:** Balanced comparison (not necessarily contradiction).

**Often paired with "On the one hand":**
> "**On the one hand**, technology improves efficiency. **On the other hand**, it reduces human interaction."

---

### **E) IN CONTRAST & CONVERSELY**

**Explicit comparison** of two different things.

**IN CONTRAST:**
> "Urban areas have dense populations. **In contrast**, rural areas are sparsely populated."

**CONVERSELY:**
> "Rich countries consume more resources. **Conversely**, poor countries have smaller environmental footprints."

**Difference:**
- **In contrast** = simple comparison
- **Conversely** = opposite relationship atau reverse situation

---

### **F) ALTHOUGH / THOUGH / EVEN THOUGH**

**Subordinating conjunctions;** introduce concessive clause.

**Usage:** Show contrast within single sentence (bukan between sentences).

**ALTHOUGH (Most Formal):**
> "**Although** it was raining, we continued hiking."

**THOUGH (Neutral):**
> "**Though** expensive, the product is worth it."

**EVEN THOUGH (Emphatic):**
> "**Even though** warned repeatedly, they ignored the danger."

**Position:**
- Beginning of sentence: "Although X, Y."
- End of sentence: "Y, although X."

---

### **G) WHILE & WHEREAS**

**Shows contrast** between two simultaneous or related facts.

**WHILE:**
> "**While** some students excel online, others struggle without structure."

**WHEREAS:**
> "Urban schools receive ample funding, **whereas** rural schools face budget cuts."

**Subtle Difference:**
- **While** = concurrent situations
- **Whereas** = more formal, explicit comparison

---

### **H) DESPITE / IN SPITE OF**

**Preposition + noun/gerund** (NOT full clause).

**DESPITE:**
> "**Despite the rain**, the game continued."

**IN SPITE OF:**
> "**In spite of** multiple warnings, accidents occurred."

**Key Rule:** Followed by **NOUN** or **GERUND** (-ing), NOT subject+verb!

❌ "Despite it was raining..."
✅ "Despite the rain..." OR "Despite raining..."

---

**When to Use Contrast Markers:**

**✅ USE when:**
- Presenting opposing viewpoint
- Showing unexpected outcome
- Balancing argument (pros and cons)
- Acknowledging limitations

**Important:** Contrast marker **must match** actual logical relationship!

**Common Mistake:**
❌ "Exercise is healthy. **However**, it also improves mood."

**Problem:** Both sentences positive (not contrasting). Should be "Moreover"!

✅ "Exercise is healthy. **However**, many people avoid it."

**Correct:** Real contrast between healthy (positive) and avoid (negative).

---

## **KATEGORI 5: CAUSE-EFFECT/RESULT MARKERS**

**Fungsi:** Show causal relationship; indicate result atau consequence.

**Members:**

**RESULT/CONSEQUENCE:**
- Therefore, thus, hence, consequently, as a result, accordingly
- For this reason, because of this, due to this

**CAUSE:**
- Because, since, as, due to, owing to, thanks to

---

### **A) THEREFORE & THUS (Strong Result)**

**Indicates logical conclusion** dari previous statement.

**THEREFORE (More Common):**
> "Air pollution reached dangerous levels. **Therefore**, schools were closed."

**THUS (Slightly More Formal):**
> "The evidence was inconclusive. **Thus**, further research is needed."

**Implication:** Result is **logical consequence** dari cause.

**Position:** Usually beginning of sentence (can be mid-sentence).

---

### **B) HENCE**

**Very formal;** similar to "therefore" tapi more archaic/literary.

**Contoh:**
> "Resources are limited. **Hence**, careful planning is essential."

**Usage:** Academic/formal writing; rare dalam casual context.

---

### **C) CONSEQUENTLY**

**Emphasizes consequence** (especially negative outcome).

**Contoh:**
> "The team missed the deadline. **Consequently**, they lost the client."

**Connotation:** Often (but not always) negative consequence.

---

### **D) AS A RESULT**

**Clearly marks result;** very explicit causal link.

**Contoh:**
> "Funding was cut. **As a result**, the program ended."

**Advantage:** Unmistakably clear cause-effect relationship.

---

### **E) FOR THIS REASON**

**Explains reason** for action or decision.

**Contoh:**
> "Data privacy is crucial. **For this reason**, we implement strict protocols."

**Function:** Justifies or explains based on preceding statement.

---

### **F) BECAUSE / SINCE / AS (Cause)**

**Introduce reason/cause** (subordinating conjunctions).

**BECAUSE (Most Common, Neutral):**
> "Schools closed **because** air quality was hazardous."

**SINCE (Implies Already-Known Reason):**
> "**Since** the evidence is clear, action is needed."

**AS (Weakest, Sometimes Ambiguous with Time):**
> "**As** resources are limited, we must prioritize."

**Position:**
- Beginning: "Because X, Y."
- End: "Y because X."

---

### **G) DUE TO / OWING TO**

**Preposition phrases** (NOT conjunctions); followed by NOUN.

**DUE TO:**
> "The delay was **due to** bad weather."

**OWING TO (More Formal):**
> "The cancellation was **owing to** safety concerns."

**Rule:** Use after BE verb or follows noun.

❌ "Due to it rained..." (WRONG - can't use with clause)
✅ "The cancellation was due to rain." (RIGHT - followed by noun)

---

### **H) THANKS TO (Positive Cause)**

**Shows cause** with positive outcome.

**Contoh:**
> "**Thanks to** early intervention, the patient recovered fully."

**Connotation:** Cause is beneficial/positive.

**vs "Due to"** (neutral, often negative):
- "The success was **thanks to** teamwork." ✅ (positive)
- "The failure was **due to** poor planning." ✅ (negative)

---

**Choosing Right Cause-Effect Marker:**

**For RESULT/CONSEQUENCE:**
- **Strong conclusion:** Therefore, Thus
- **Emphasize outcome:** Consequently, As a result
- **Explain decision:** For this reason

**For CAUSE:**
- **Direct cause:** Because
- **Known reason:** Since
- **Positive cause:** Thanks to
- **Neutral/negative cause:** Due to

---

**Common Mistake:**

**Using Cause Marker untuk Result (or vice versa):**

❌ "It rained. **Because**, the game was cancelled."

**Problem:** "Because" introduces CAUSE, not result!

✅ "It rained. **Therefore**, the game was cancelled."

OR

✅ "The game was cancelled **because** it rained."

---

## **KATEGORI 6: EMPHASIS/CLARIFICATION MARKERS**

**Fungsi:** Strengthen atau clarify a point; add emphasis.

**Members:**

**EMPHASIS:**
- Indeed, in fact, actually, certainly, surely, definitely
- Clearly, obviously, undoubtedly, without doubt

**CLARIFICATION:**
- That is (to say), in other words, namely, specifically, to put it simply
- To clarify, to be specific, more precisely

---

### **A) INDEED & IN FACT (Strengthen Point)**

**INDEED (Confirms/Reinforces):**
> "The project was challenging. **Indeed**, it was the most difficult we'd faced."

**Implication:** Confirms dan strengthens previous statement.

**IN FACT (Often Introduces Stronger/Surprising Info):**
> "The plan didn't just succeed. **In fact**, it exceeded all expectations."

**Function:** Escalates dari good to even better (or bad to worse).

---

### **B) ACTUALLY**

**Corrects misconception** atau presents surprising truth.

**Contoh:**
> "Many believe exercise requires hours daily. **Actually**, 30 minutes is sufficient."

**Function:** Contrasts common belief with reality.

**Position:** Can be beginning, mid, or end of sentence.

---

### **C) CLEARLY & OBVIOUSLY**

**Indicates something is self-evident.**

**CLEARLY:**
> "**Clearly**, climate change poses serious risks."

**OBVIOUSLY:**
> "**Obviously**, education is essential for development."

**Caution:** Can sound condescending if overused! Use when truly evident.

---

### **D) THAT IS (TO SAY) & IN OTHER WORDS**

**Restates** idea untuk clarity atau simplification.

**THAT IS:**
> "The deadline is imminent, **that is**, in three days."

**IN OTHER WORDS:**
> "Revenue declined. **In other words**, the company lost money."

**Function:** Rephrases sama idea dengan simpler atau different terms.

---

### **E) NAMELY & SPECIFICALLY**

**Provides exact specification.**

**NAMELY (Lists All Members):**
> "Three factors contributed, **namely** cost, time, and quality."

**Implication:** Exhaustive list (ALL factors mentioned).

**SPECIFICALLY (Narrows Focus):**
> "The policy helps students, **specifically** those from disadvantaged backgrounds."

**Implication:** Particular subset highlighted.

---

**When to Use Emphasis/Clarification:**

**✅ USE when:**
- Want to strengthen important point
- Clarify potentially confusing idea
- Correct common misconception
- Specify exact details

**❌ AVOID when:**
- Overusing makes writing feel pushy
- Point is already crystal clear (redundant)
- In objective/neutral reporting (can bias)

---

## **KATEGORI 7: SUMMARY/CONCLUSION MARKERS**

**Fungsi:** Signal bahwa you're wrapping up atau concluding.

**Members:**
- In conclusion, to conclude, to sum up, in summary, in short
- Overall, all in all, on the whole, ultimately, in the final analysis

---

### **A) IN CONCLUSION (Strongest Signal)**

**Clearly marks final paragraph** or overall conclusion.

**Contoh:**
> "**In conclusion**, renewable energy is essential for sustainable development."

**Usage:** Typically for ESSAY conclusion, not mid-essay paragraphs.

**Formality:** Very formal (academic essays, speeches).

---

### **B) TO SUM UP & IN SUMMARY**

**Recaps main points** briefly.

**TO SUM UP:**
> "**To sum up**, the project succeeded due to teamwork, innovation, and perseverance."

**IN SUMMARY:**
> "**In summary**, the data supports our hypothesis."

**Function:** Condenses discussion into brief recap.

---

### **C) IN SHORT & IN BRIEF**

**Very concise summary.**

**Contoh:**
> "The process is complex. **In short**, it takes years."

**Usage:** When condensing complicated explanation into simple statement.

---

### **D) OVERALL & ALL IN ALL**

**Considering everything** (balanced judgment).

**OVERALL:**
> "**Overall**, the strategy was successful despite minor setbacks."

**ALL IN ALL:**
> "**All in all**, the benefits outweigh the costs."

**Implication:** Takes all factors into account.

---

### **E) ULTIMATELY**

**Final outcome** atau most important conclusion.

**Contoh:**
> "**Ultimately**, education determines a nation's future."

**Function:** Points to most fundamental or final truth.

---

**When to Use Conclusion Markers:**

**✅ USE when:**
- Ending essay or major section
- Want to summarize key points
- Providing final judgment/verdict
- Wrapping up complex discussion

**❌ DON'T use:**
- In middle paragraphs (save for actual conclusions)
- Multiple times (once per essay/section is enough)
- If haven't actually provided comprehensive discussion

---

**Typical Essay Structure:**
- **Intro:** No conclusion marker
- **Body Paragraphs:** Transition markers (however, moreover, etc.)
- **Conclusion Paragraph:** IN CONCLUSION / TO SUM UP / OVERALL

---

### **Quick Reference: Choosing Right Discourse Marker**

**Ask yourself:**

1. **What's the relationship between ideas?**
   - Same direction → Addition (moreover)
   - Opposite direction → Contrast (however)
   - Cause-result → Causation (therefore)
   - Example → Exemplification (for example)

2. **What's the formality level?**
   - Very formal → furthermore, nevertheless, consequently
   - Neutral → however, also, therefore
   - Informal → besides, plus, anyway

3. **What's the strength of connection?**
   - Strong → moreover, however, consequently
   - Mild → also, yet, so

4. **What's the position preference?**
   - Beginning → However, Therefore, Moreover
   - Mid → also, however, too
   - End → as well, too

---

### **Common Mistakes dengan Discourse Markers**

**Mistake #1: Wrong Marker for Relationship**
❌ "I love pizza. However, I also love pasta."

**Problem:** No contrast (both loves!). Should be "Moreover" or "Also".

---

**Mistake #2: Overusing Markers**
❌ "Firstly, pollution harms health. Moreover, it also damages ecosystems. Furthermore, additionally, it affects climate..."

**Problem:** Too many! Sounds mechanical.

✅ "Pollution harms health. It also damages ecosystems and affects climate."

---

**Mistake #3: Punctuation Errors**
❌ "However the plan failed."
✅ "However, the plan failed."

❌ "The plan however failed."
✅ "The plan, however, failed."

---

**Mistake #4: Using Contradictory Markers**
❌ "The study failed. Therefore, it provided valuable insights."

**Problem:** Failure doesn't logically lead to insights (contradiction). Should be "However" or "Nevertheless".

---

### **Practice Strategy for Soal**

**When facing discourse marker questions:**

**STEP 1:** Read sentences before AND after blank
**STEP 2:** Determine logical relationship
**STEP 3:** Eliminate wrong categories
**STEP 4:** Choose marker that fits relationship AND tone

**Example:**

> "Exercise improves health. _____, many people remain sedentary."

**Analysis:**
- Relationship: CONTRAST (improves vs remain sedentary)
- Eliminate: addition, cause-effect, example markers
- Choose from: However, Nevertheless, Yet, Still
- **Answer: However** (most natural untuk this context)
