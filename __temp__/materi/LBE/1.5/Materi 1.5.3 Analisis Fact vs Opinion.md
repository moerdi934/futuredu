## TOPIK 1.5: AUTHOR'S ATTITUDE (OPINION DAN TONE)

---


## **Materi 1.5.3: Analisis Fact vs Opinion**

### **Deep Dive: Perbedaan Fundamental**

Sekarang kita akan masuk lebih dalam ke **teknik membedakan** fakta dan opini—skill yang super krusial untuk SNBT!

---

### **Karakteristik FACT (Fakta)**

#### **1. Verifiable (Dapat Diverifikasi)**

Fakta bisa **dicek kebenarannya** melalui:
- Data statistik
- Penelitian ilmiah
- Dokumen resmi
- Observasi langsung

**Contoh:**
> *"Water boils at 100°C at sea level."*

↑ Ini fakta karena bisa diverifikasi melalui eksperimen.

---

#### **2. Objective (Objektif)**

Fakta **tidak tergantung** pada sudut pandang atau belief personal:
- True regardless of who observes it
- Sama untuk semua orang

**Contoh:**
> *"The earth revolves around the sun."*

↑ Fakta astronomi yang objektif.

---

#### **3. Based on Evidence (Berbasis Bukti)**

Fakta didukung oleh:
- ✅ Numbers/statistics
- ✅ Dates/historical records
- ✅ Scientific findings
- ✅ Direct quotations (dari sumber kredibel)

**Contoh:**
> *"According to WHO data, COVID-19 cases reached 700 million globally by 2023."*

↑ Fakta karena berdasarkan data WHO yang bisa dicek.

---

#### **4. Language Indicators untuk Fakta**

**Verbs faktual:**
- is, are, was, were (state of being)
- occurred, happened (past events)
- shows, indicates, demonstrates (data/research)
- found, discovered, observed (scientific findings)

**Contoh:**
> *"The study **found** that 60% of participants improved their scores."*

↑ Reporting research results = fakta.

---

### **Karakteristik OPINION (Opini)**

Kita sudah bahas di Materi 1.5.2, tapi mari kita recap dengan kontras langsung:

#### **1. Not Verifiable (Tidak Dapat Diverifikasi)**

Opini **tidak bisa dibuktikan benar/salah** secara objektif:
- Based on personal belief
- Matter of perspective

**Contoh:**
> *"Chocolate ice cream is the best flavor."*

↑ Opini karena "best" adalah subjective judgment.

---

#### **2. Subjective (Subjektif)**

Opini **tergantung** pada who's expressing it:
- Different people, different opinions
- Influenced by values, experiences, biases

**Contoh:**
> *"Remote work is more productive than office work."*

↑ Opini karena productivity bisa berbeda untuk tiap orang.

---

#### **3. Based on Interpretation/Judgment**

Opini melibatkan:
- ✅ Personal evaluation
- ✅ Interpretation of facts
- ✅ Predictions/speculations
- ✅ Recommendations

**Contoh:**
> *"The government **should** increase funding for education."*

↑ Opini karena "should" = rekomendasi based on personal judgment.

---

### **Mixed Statements: Fakta + Opini**

Ini yang paling tricky! Banyak kalimat mengandung **keduanya**:

#### **Contoh 1:**
> *"The temperature reached 40°C yesterday (FAKTA), which was **unbearably** hot (OPINI)."*

**Analisis:**
- "reached 40°C" → verifiable data = FAKTA
- "unbearably hot" → personal judgment = OPINI

---

#### **Contoh 2:**
> *"Sales increased by 25% (FAKTA), an **impressive** achievement (OPINI)."*

**Analisis:**
- "increased by 25%" → measurable = FAKTA
- "impressive achievement" → evaluation = OPINI

---

#### **Contoh 3:**
> *"According to the report, deforestation rates have doubled (FAKTA), which is **alarming** (OPINI) and **requires immediate action** (OPINI)."*

**Analisis:**
- Report data → FAKTA
- "Alarming" → emotional judgment = OPINI
- "Requires immediate action" → recommendation = OPINI

---

### **Strategi POWER untuk Membedakan**

Gunakan akronim **POWER**:

#### **P = Provable?**
- Bisakah statement ini dibuktikan dengan data/evidence?
- ✅ Yes → Likely FACT
- ❌ No → Likely OPINION

#### **O = Objective or Subjective?**
- Apakah pernyataan ini sama untuk semua orang?
- ✅ Objective → FACT
- ❌ Subjective (tergantung perspektif) → OPINION

#### **W = Words (Language Clues)**
- Ada evaluative adjectives? (good, bad, important)
- Ada modal verbs? (should, must, may)
- Ada first-person? (I believe, in my view)
- ✅ Ada → Likely OPINION
- ❌ Tidak ada → Check further

#### **E = Evidence-based?**
- Didukung data/research/dokumentasi?
- ✅ Yes → FACT
- ❌ Based on interpretation → OPINION

#### **R = Refutable?**
- Bisakah someone reasonably disagree?
- ✅ Yes (debatable) → OPINION
- ❌ No (universally accepted) → FACT

---

### **Jebakan-Jebakan Umum**

#### **Jebakan 1: Expert Opinion Dikira Pure Fact**

**Contoh:**
> *"Dr. Smith believes that this treatment is effective."*

**Analisis:**
- Ini **fakta tentang opini** Dr. Smith
- Tapi pernyataan "treatment is effective" sendiri adalah OPINI (dari Dr. Smith)

**Dalam soal:**
- Jika pertanyaan: "Which is a fact?" → Kalimat ini bisa jadi jawaban (fakta bahwa Dr. Smith believe something)
- Jika pertanyaan: "What is Dr. Smith's opinion?" → "Treatment is effective" adalah opininya

---

#### **Jebakan 2: Statistical Interpretation**

**Contoh:**
> *"70% of respondents supported the policy."* (FAKTA)  
> *"The overwhelming support shows the policy is right."* (OPINI)

**Analisis:**
- Data 70% → verifiable = FAKTA
- "Overwhelming" → judgment = OPINI
- "Right" → value judgment = OPINI

---

#### **Jebakan 3: Predictive Statements**

**Contoh:**
> *"Global temperatures will continue to rise."*

**Analisis:**
- Ini OPINI (prediction/speculation tentang future)
- Meskipun based on scientific models, tetap prediction = not verifiable fact yet

**Bandingkan dengan:**
> *"Global temperatures have risen by 1.2°C since pre-industrial times."*

↑ Ini FAKTA (historical data yang verifiable)

---

#### **Jebakan 4: "Studies Show" ≠ Automatic Fact**

**Contoh yang Tricky:**
> *"Studies suggest that meditation may improve mental health."*

**Analisis:**
- "Studies suggest" → reporting research
- **Tapi**: "may improve" → masih spekulatif
- **Conclusion**: Ini **FAKTA tentang apa yang studies suggest**, tapi conclusion-nya sendiri masih spekulatif/opini

**Bandingkan:**
> *"Studies have found a correlation between meditation and improved mental health."*

↑ Ini lebih ke FAKTA karena "found" (completed research) dan "correlation" (measurable relationship)

---

### **Praktik Analisis Kompleks**

**Bacaan Contoh:**

> *Climate change is **undeniably** one of the greatest threats facing humanity. **According to NASA**, global temperatures have risen by approximately 1.1°C since 1880. **I believe** we must take **immediate** action to reduce carbon emissions. The Paris Agreement aims to limit warming to 1.5°C, but **critics argue** that the targets are **insufficient**. Renewable energy **should** be prioritized over fossil fuels. **Data shows** that solar and wind power have become increasingly cost-effective.*

**Mari kita breakdown:**

1. "Climate change is undeniably one of the greatest threats"
   - **OPINI**: "Greatest threats" = value judgment; "undeniably" = framing opinion as if it's universal truth

2. "According to NASA, global temperatures have risen by approximately 1.1°C since 1880"
   - **FAKTA**: Specific data dari sumber kredibel (NASA), measurable, verifiable

3. "I believe we must take immediate action"
   - **OPINI**: "I believe" = explicit opinion marker; "must" = strong recommendation

4. "The Paris Agreement aims to limit warming to 1.5°C"
   - **FAKTA**: Stated goal dari treaty (dokumentasi resmi)

5. "Critics argue that the targets are insufficient"
   - **FAKTA tentang OPINI**: Fakta bahwa critics have this opinion; tapi "insufficient" itself adalah judgment

6. "Renewable energy should be prioritized"
   - **OPINI**: "Should" = recommendation/prescription

7. "Data shows that solar and wind power have become increasingly cost-effective"
   - **FAKTA**: "Data shows" = evidence-based; "cost-effective" bisa diukur dengan economic metrics

### **Tips Menghadapi Soal Fact vs Opinion**

✅ **Jangan terburu-buru!**  
Baca kalimat lengkap, jangan cuma lihat sekilas.

✅ **Fokus pada structure, bukan content agreement**  
Kamu bisa strongly agree dengan sebuah opini, tapi tetap harus recognize it as opinion.

✅ **Check attribution**  
- "The report states..." → likely FACT (jika report credible)
- "Experts believe..." → OPINION (belief dari experts)

✅ **Modal verbs adalah red flag**  
Should, must, ought to, may, might → hampir always OPINION

✅ **Numbers aren't always facts**  
- "70% agreed" → FACT
- "A disappointing 70% agreed" → Mixed (fact + opinion)

✅ **Comparative/superlative language**  
Better, best, worse, worst, most important → usually OPINION (judgment)

---

### **Common Patterns dalam Soal SNBT**

#### **Pattern 1: "Which statement is a FACT?"**

**Strategi:**
- Eliminasi yang clearly opini (ada should, good/bad, I think)
- Pilih yang specific, measurable, verifiable

#### **Pattern 2: "Which is the author's OPINION?"**

**Strategi:**
- Cari subjective language
- Cari recommendations (should, must)
- Cari evaluative words (important, necessary, harmful)

#### **Pattern 3: "All are facts EXCEPT..."**

**Strategi:**
- Cari yang paling subjective/judgmental
- Satu yang beda (opinion) dari yang lain (facts)

### **Latihan Mini: Fact or Opinion?**

Coba identifikasi sendiri (jawaban di akhir):

1. "The company's revenue increased by 15% last quarter."
2. "This represents an impressive turnaround for the struggling business."
3. "According to the survey, 85% of customers reported satisfaction."
4. "Customer satisfaction should be the company's top priority."
5. "The CEO announced plans to expand into Asian markets."
6. "This expansion is a risky but necessary move."

**Jawaban:**
1. FAKTA (specific data)
2. OPINI ("impressive" = judgment; "struggling" = evaluative)
3. FAKTA (survey result, verifiable)
4. OPINI ("should" = recommendation)
5. FAKTA (announced plan = verifiable statement)
6. OPINI ("risky but necessary" = judgment call)
