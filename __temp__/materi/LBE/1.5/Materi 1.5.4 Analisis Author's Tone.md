## TOPIK 1.5: AUTHOR'S ATTITUDE (OPINION DAN TONE)

---


## **Materi 1.5.4: Analisis Author's Tone**

### **Apa Itu Tone dalam Teks?**

Kalau **opinion** adalah *what the author thinks*, maka **tone** adalah *how the author feels* dan *how it comes across* dalam tulisan.

**Analogi seru:**
Bayangkan kamu kirim pesan ke teman:
- "Oke, terserah kamu." → Tone bisa netral, bisa sinis (tergantung konteks!)
- "Oke! Terserah kamu! 😊" → Tone lebih supportive
- "Oke... terserah kamu..." → Tone skeptical/reluctant

Nah, dalam teks tertulis, kita gak punya emoji atau nada suara, jadi kita harus **baca tone dari pilihan kata dan struktur kalimat**.

---

### **Kenapa Tone Penting?**

1. **Mengungkap sikap penulis** yang tidak eksplisit
2. **Mempengaruhi credibility** → Tone yang terlalu emosional bisa mengurangi objektivitas
3. **Membantu understand purpose** → Persuasive tone → likely trying to convince
4. **Membedakan genre** → Objective tone (science) vs Subjective tone (editorial)

---

### **Spektrum Tone: Dari Positif ke Negatif**

Tone bisa berada di spektrum:

```
POSITIF ←→ NETRAL ←→ NEGATIF
```

#### **Positive Tones:**
- **Optimistic**: Penuh harapan, melihat sisi baik
- **Enthusiastic**: Bersemangat, antusias
- **Supportive**: Mendukung, membantu
- **Appreciative**: Menghargai, grateful
- **Reverent**: Penuh hormat, respek
- **Sincere**: Tulus, jujur
- **Encouraging**: Memberi semangat

#### **Neutral/Objective Tones:**
- **Objective**: Netral, tidak memihak
- **Detached**: Tidak emosional, distant
- **Informative**: Sekadar memberi info
- **Matter-of-fact**: Straightforward, apa adanya
- **Analytical**: Menganalisis tanpa judgment emosional

#### **Negative Tones:**
- **Critical**: Mengkritik, menunjukkan kelemahan
- **Skeptical**: Meragukan, tidak yakin
- **Cynical**: Sinis, tidak percaya motif baik
- **Pessimistic**: Pesimis, melihat sisi buruk
- **Sarcastic**: Sarkastik, menyindir
- **Contemptuous**: Merendahkan, hina
- **Indignant**: Marah karena ketidakadilan
- **Disapproving**: Tidak setuju, menolak

---

### **Vocabulary untuk Mendeskripsikan Tone**

Berikut daftar lengkap vocabulary yang sering muncul dalam opsi jawaban soal SNBT:

#### **A. Positive/Supportive Tones**

1. **Optimistic** (optimis)
   - Melihat masa depan dengan harapan
   - Fokus pada kemungkinan positif
   - *Example clue*: "This promising development...", "bright future", "hopeful signs"

2. **Enthusiastic** (antusias)
   - Bersemangat dan excited
   - Energetic language
   - *Example clue*: "exciting breakthrough!", "tremendous opportunity", exclamation marks

3. **Reverent** (penuh hormat)
   - Menunjukkan respek dan admirasi
   - Formal, careful language
   - *Example clue*: "the esteemed scholar", "remarkable achievement"

4. **Supportive** (mendukung)
   - Backing up an idea atau person
   - Encouraging language
   - *Example clue*: "deserves our support", "commendable effort"

5. **Sincere** (tulus)
   - Genuine, honest, tidak pretentious
   - Straightforward but warm
   - *Example clue*: Simple, honest expression of views

---

#### **B. Neutral/Objective Tones**

1. **Objective** (objektif)
   - No personal bias
   - Factual, balanced
   - *Example clue*: Data-driven, presents multiple perspectives equally

2. **Detached** (tidak terlibat emosi)
   - Emotionally distant
   - Observational, tidak invested
   - *Example clue*: Clinical language, no evaluative words

3. **Informative** (informatif)
   - Focus pada menyampaikan informasi
   - Educational tone
   - *Example clue*: "It is important to note...", clear explanations

4. **Analytical** (analitis)
   - Breaking down ideas logically
   - Examining evidence
   - *Example clue*: "Analysis shows...", "examining the data..."

---

#### **C. Critical/Questioning Tones**

1. **Critical** (kritis)
   - Menunjukkan kelemahan atau masalah
   - Bukan berarti angry, tapi evaluative
   - *Example clue*: "fails to address", "overlooks", "problematic"

2. **Skeptical** (skeptis)
   - Meragukan klaim atau idea
   - Questioning validity
   - *Example clue*: "allegedly", "claims to", "questionable", "dubious"

3. **Cautious** (hati-hati)
   - Careful, tidak commit fully
   - Hedging language
   - *Example clue*: "may", "might", "appears to", "suggests"

4. **Concerned** (prihatin)
   - Worried tapi tidak hostile
   - Shows care about issue
   - *Example clue*: "troubling trend", "raises concerns", "worrying"

---

#### **D. Negative/Hostile Tones**

1. **Cynical** (sinis)
   - Tidak percaya motif baik orang/institusi
   - Believes people are selfish
   - *Example clue*: "so-called experts", quotation marks untuk undermine

2. **Sarcastic** (sarkastik)
   - Saying opposite of what is meant
   - Ironic, mocking
   - *Example clue*: Exaggerated praise yang actually kritik

3. **Contemptuous** (merendahkan)
   - Showing disdain
   - Very negative, dismissive
   - *Example clue*: "pathetic attempt", "laughable claim"

4. **Indignant** (marah karena unfairness)
   - Angry at injustice
   - Moral outrage
   - *Example clue*: "outrageous", "unacceptable", strong moral language

5. **Pessimistic** (pesimis)
   - Expecting negative outcomes
   - Opposite of optimistic
   - *Example clue*: "inevitable decline", "grim future", "little hope"

6. **Disapproving** (tidak setuju)
   - Shows disapproval
   - Negative judgment
   - *Example clue*: "unfortunate decision", "regrettable"

---

### **Cara Mengidentifikasi Tone dari Text**

#### **1. Perhatikan Word Choice (Diction)**

Pilihan kata adalah clue terbesar untuk tone:

**Contoh 1: Neutral Tone**
> *"The policy was implemented in 2020 and affects approximately 2 million citizens."*

↑ Factual words, no evaluative language = **Objective tone**

**Contoh 2: Critical Tone**
> *"The **poorly conceived** policy was **hastily** implemented in 2020, **disrupting** the lives of 2 million citizens."*

↑ Negative evaluative words (poorly conceived, hastily, disrupting) = **Critical tone**

**Contoh 3: Supportive Tone**
> *"The **well-designed** policy was **successfully** implemented in 2020, **benefiting** 2 million citizens."*

↑ Positive evaluative words = **Supportive/Positive tone**

---

#### **2. Perhatikan Intensifiers dan Qualifiers**

**Intensifiers** (memperkuat):
- very, extremely, highly, deeply, absolutely, utterly
- Menunjukkan strong feeling

**Qualifiers** (melemahkan):
- somewhat, rather, fairly, relatively, quite
- Menunjukkan restraint atau hesitation

**Contoh:**
> "The results are **extremely disappointing**." → Strong negative tone  
> "The results are **somewhat disappointing**." → Mild negative tone

---

#### **3. Perhatikan Sentence Structure**

**Short, declarative sentences:**
- Bisa indicate confidence atau directness
- *Example*: "This must stop. Now." → Firm/Indignant

**Long, complex sentences:**
- Bisa indicate careful analysis atau tentative reasoning
- *Example*: "While it is possible that..., one must also consider..." → Cautious/Analytical

**Rhetorical questions:**
- Often indicate skepticism atau sarcasm
- *Example*: "Is anyone truly surprised by these results?" → Cynical/Sarcastic

---

#### **4. Perhatikan Connotation (Makna Konotasi)**

Kata dengan denotasi sama bisa punya connotation berbeda:

| Neutral | Positive | Negative |
|---------|----------|----------|
| thin | slender, slim | skinny, scrawny |
| confident | self-assured | arrogant, cocky |
| cheap | affordable, economical | shoddy, inferior |
| persistent | determined, tenacious | stubborn, obstinate |

**Dalam analisis tone:**
> "He is a **determined** leader." → Positive tone  
> "He is a **stubborn** leader." → Negative tone

### **Jebakan dalam Soal Tone**

#### **Jebakan 1: Confusing Critical with Hostile**

**Critical** ≠ **Hostile**

- **Critical tone**: Menganalisis kelemahan secara objektif, constructive criticism
- **Hostile tone**: Angry, attacking, very negative

**Contoh Critical (bukan Hostile):**
> *"While the study provides valuable insights, it **fails to account for** several confounding variables."*

↑ Ini critical (menunjukkan kelemahan) tapi tidak hostile (tidak attacking atau angry).

---

#### **Jebakan 2: Confusing Objective with Detached**

Keduanya netral tapi ada nuansa beda:

- **Objective**: Presenting facts without bias, balanced
- **Detached**: Emotionally distant, tidak invested in topic

**Contoh Objective:**
> *"The policy has both advantages and disadvantages. On one hand... On the other hand..."*

**Contoh Detached:**
> *"The policy exists. Some support it, others don't."*

↑ Detached terkesan lebih "cold" atau uninterested.

---

#### **Jebakan 3: Sarcasm yang Tidak Kentara**

Sarcasm bisa subtle, terutama dalam teks tertulis:

**Contoh Sarcasm:**
> *"Of course, the government's decision was **brilliant**—if destroying the economy was the goal."*

↑ "Brilliant" di sini sarkastik (actually means the opposite).

**Clues untuk sarcasm:**
- Exaggerated praise dalam konteks negatif
- Quotation marks around words ("brilliant" plan)
- Irony atau contradiction

---

### **Strategi Menentukan Tone**

#### **Step-by-Step Approach:**

**Step 1: Identify Subject Matter**
Apa topik yang dibahas? Controversial issue? Scientific fact?

**Step 2: Look for Evaluative Language**
Ada kata-kata judgment? Positive or negative?

**Step 3: Check Emotional Intensity**
Strong words (extremely, utterly) atau restrained (somewhat, rather)?

**Step 4: Analyze Overall Pattern**
Apakah consistently positive, negative, atau mixed?

**Step 5: Match dengan Vocabulary Tone**
Cocokkan dengan daftar tone words yang kamu tahu.

---

### **Contoh Analisis Tone Lengkap**

**Bacaan 1:**
> *"Recent advancements in renewable energy technology represent a **promising** step toward addressing climate change. Solar and wind power have become increasingly **efficient** and **cost-effective**, offering **hope** for a sustainable future. While challenges remain, the **progress** made thus far is **encouraging**."*

**Analisis:**
- **Evaluative words**: promising, efficient, cost-effective, hope, progress, encouraging
- **Semua positive**
- **No hedging**, fairly confident
- **Conclusion**: **Optimistic tone**

---

**Bacaan 2:**
> *"The government's so-called 'reform' policy has **predictably** resulted in chaos. Despite **warnings** from experts, officials **stubbornly** proceeded with implementation. The **disastrous** consequences were **entirely avoidable**."*

**Analisis:**
- **Evaluative words**: so-called (dismissive), predictably (cynical), warnings (ignored), stubbornly (negative), disastrous, avoidable
- **Heavy negative judgment**
- **Blame implied** (officials ignored warnings)
- **Conclusion**: **Critical/Indignant tone**

---

**Bacaan 3:**
> *"The study examined 500 participants over a six-month period. Results indicated a 15% improvement in test group compared to control. Data was collected using standardized protocols and analyzed using statistical methods."*

**Analisis:**
- **Factual language**: examined, indicated, collected, analyzed
- **No evaluative words**
- **No emotional language**
- **Conclusion**: **Objective/Informative tone**

### **Tips Menghadapi Soal Tone**

✅ **Baca keseluruhan passage, jangan cuma satu kalimat**  
Tone sering terbangun dari akumulasi, bukan single word.

✅ **Fokus pada adjectives dan adverbs**  
Ini word classes yang paling sering membawa tone.

✅ **Perhatikan proportion**  
Jika 80% positive words dan 20% negative, overall tone likely positive.

✅ **Context matters**  
Kata "interesting" bisa genuine atau sarcastic tergantung context.

✅ **Eliminasi ekstrem dulu**  
Jika tone clearly not "contemptuous" (very negative), eliminate that option early.

✅ **Bedakan tone penulis vs tone yang dijelaskan**  
Penulis bisa menjelaskan view yang hostile tanpa adopting that tone themselves.

---

### **Common Tone Patterns dalam Berbagai Genre**

#### **Scientific/Academic Texts:**
- Usually: **Objective, Analytical, Informative**
- Jarang: Emotional atau strongly opinionated

#### **Editorials/Opinion Pieces:**
- Bisa: **Critical, Persuasive, Passionate**
- Range: Dari supportive sampai hostile

#### **News Reports:**
- Ideal: **Objective, Detached**
- Realita: Sometimes subtle bias

#### **Literary Texts:**
- Wide range: Bisa anything dari **nostalgic** sampai **satirical**
