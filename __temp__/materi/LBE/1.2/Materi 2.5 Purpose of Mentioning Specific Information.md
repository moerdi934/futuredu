## **Materi 2.5: Purpose of Mentioning Specific Information**

### **Apa Itu "Purpose of Mentioning"?**

Okay, sekarang kita masuk ke level yang lebih detail: **Purpose of Mentioning Specific Information**. Ini adalah jenis soal Purpose yang nanyain: **"Kenapa penulis nyebutin informasi ini?"**

Berbeda dengan Purpose keseluruhan teks atau paragraf, soal ini fokus pada **satu detail spesifik**—bisa berupa:
- **Data atau statistik** (angka, persentase, hasil penelitian)
- **Nama orang** (expert, tokoh, ilmuwan)
- **Contoh** (kasus, ilustrasi, anekdot)
- **Kutipan** (quote dari seseorang)
- **Fakta spesifik** (tanggal, tempat, kejadian)

Pertanyaannya biasanya kayak:
- "Why does the author mention '...' in the passage?"
- "The author refers to '...' to..."
- "What is the purpose of mentioning the statistic in line...?"
- "The author includes the example of '...' in order to..."

---

### **Kenapa Penulis Menyebutkan Informasi Spesifik?**

Penulis ga pernah nyebutin sesuatu tanpa alasan. Setiap detail yang disebutkan pasti punya **fungsi** dalam konteks teks. Fungsi-fungsi umum dari mentioning specific information antara lain:

1. **To support an argument** (mendukung argumen)
2. **To provide evidence** (kasih bukti)
3. **To give an example** (kasih contoh)
4. **To illustrate a concept** (mengilustrasikan konsep abstrak)
5. **To add credibility** (menambah kredibilitas)
6. **To clarify** (memperjelas)
7. **To emphasize** (menekankan)
8. **To contrast** (membandingkan/mempertentangkan)

Mari kita bahas satu per satu!

---

### **Fungsi 1: To Support an Argument (Mendukung Argumen)**

Kalau penulis lagi **berargumen** atau punya **stance** tertentu, dia biasanya perlu **support** argumen itu dengan sesuatu yang konkret. Nah, di sinilah specific information masuk.

**Ciri-ciri:**
- Teks punya **clear stance** (opini atau posisi)
- Informasi spesifik muncul **setelah** atau **bersamaan** dengan klaim/argumen
- Biasanya ada transition words seperti "for instance," "for example," "such as," "in fact"

**Contoh:**

> "Social media has a negative impact on mental health. **For instance, a study by the University of Pennsylvania found that limiting social media use to 30 minutes per day significantly reduced symptoms of depression and loneliness.**"

**Analisis:**
- **Informasi spesifik:** "a study by the University of Pennsylvania..."
- **Fungsi:** **To support the argument** that social media has a negative impact on mental health.

**Soal yang mungkin muncul:**
"Why does the author mention the study by the University of Pennsylvania?"
- **Jawaban:** To support the claim that social media negatively affects mental health.

---

### **Fungsi 2: To Provide Evidence (Kasih Bukti)**

Similar dengan "support," tapi **evidence** lebih fokus pada **proof** atau **data konkret** yang membuktikan sesuatu. Biasanya berupa angka, hasil penelitian, atau fakta yang bisa diverifikasi.

**Ciri-ciri:**
- Informasi berupa **data**, **statistik**, **hasil penelitian**
- Digunakan untuk **validate** atau **prove** suatu klaim
- Tone cenderung **faktual** dan **objektif**

**Contoh:**

> "Air pollution is a serious health hazard. **According to WHO, 7 million people die each year from air pollution-related diseases.**"

**Analisis:**
- **Informasi spesifik:** "7 million people die each year..."
- **Fungsi:** **To provide evidence** that air pollution is a serious health hazard.

**Soal yang mungkin muncul:**
"The author mentions the WHO statistic to..."
- **Jawaban:** To provide evidence of the severity of air pollution.

---

### **Fungsi 3: To Give an Example (Kasih Contoh)**

Kadang, penulis ngasih **contoh konkret** untuk **mempermudah pemahaman** pembaca tentang konsep yang abstrak atau general.

**Ciri-ciri:**
- Informasi berupa **kasus spesifik**, **ilustrasi**, atau **anekdot**
- Biasanya preceded by "for example," "for instance," "such as," "like"
- Fungsi: membuat abstract concept jadi **more relatable** atau **easier to understand**

**Contoh:**

> "Many cities are adopting green transportation policies. **For example, Amsterdam has invested heavily in cycling infrastructure, making bicycles the primary mode of transport for many residents.**"

**Analisis:**
- **Informasi spesifik:** "Amsterdam has invested heavily in cycling infrastructure..."
- **Fungsi:** **To give an example** of a city adopting green transportation policies.

**Soal yang mungkin muncul:**
"The author mentions Amsterdam to..."
- **Jawaban:** To provide an example of a city with successful green transportation policies.

---

### **Fungsi 4: To Illustrate a Concept (Mengilustrasikan Konsep Abstrak)**

Similar dengan "give an example," tapi **illustrate** lebih fokus pada **visualizing** atau **concretizing** suatu ide yang abstrak atau kompleks.

**Ciri-ciri:**
- Konsep yang dijelaskan **abstract** atau **theoretical**
- Informasi spesifik membuat konsep jadi **more concrete** atau **tangible**
- Membantu pembaca **visualize** atau **understand better**

**Contoh:**

> "The concept of supply and demand is fundamental in economics. **To illustrate, when a new iPhone is released, high demand and limited supply cause prices to remain high.**"

**Analisis:**
- **Informasi spesifik:** "when a new iPhone is released..."
- **Fungsi:** **To illustrate** the concept of supply and demand.

**Soal yang mungkin muncul:**
"The author mentions the iPhone example to..."
- **Jawaban:** To illustrate the concept of supply and demand.

---

### **Fungsi 5: To Add Credibility (Menambah Kredibilitas)**

Kalau penulis mau **strengthen** argumen atau klaim dia, salah satu caranya adalah dengan **cite** expert atau otoritas di bidang itu. Ini bikin argumen lebih **credible** dan **trustworthy**.

**Ciri-ciri:**
- Mention **nama expert**, **institusi**, atau **organisasi terpercaya**
- Biasanya berupa **kutipan** atau **referensi** ke penelitian
- Purpose: make argument **more authoritative**

**Contoh:**

> "Vaccines are safe and effective. **Dr. Anthony Fauci, director of the National Institute of Allergy and Infectious Diseases, stated that vaccines have saved millions of lives.**"

**Analisis:**
- **Informasi spesifik:** "Dr. Anthony Fauci, director of..."
- **Fungsi:** **To add credibility** to the claim that vaccines are safe and effective.

**Soal yang mungkin muncul:**
"Why does the author mention Dr. Anthony Fauci?"
- **Jawaban:** To add credibility to the argument about vaccine safety.

---

### **Fungsi 6: To Clarify (Memperjelas)**

Kadang, konsep yang dijelaskan **complex** atau **potentially confusing**. Penulis menyebutkan informasi spesifik untuk **clarify** atau **make it clearer**.

**Ciri-ciri:**
- Ada konsep yang mungkin **misunderstood** atau **ambiguous**
- Informasi spesifik help **disambiguate** atau **explain further**
- Biasanya preceded by "in other words," "that is," "specifically," "to clarify"

**Contoh:**

> "Photosynthesis is the process by which plants make food. **Specifically, plants use sunlight, water, and carbon dioxide to produce glucose and oxygen.**"

**Analisis:**
- **Informasi spesifik:** "plants use sunlight, water, and carbon dioxide..."
- **Fungsi:** **To clarify** what photosynthesis is.

**Soal yang mungkin muncul:**
"The author mentions the components of photosynthesis to..."
- **Jawaban:** To clarify the process of photosynthesis.

---

### **Fungsi 7: To Emphasize (Menekankan)**

Kalau penulis mau **highlight** atau **stress** suatu poin, dia bisa menyebutkan informasi spesifik yang **dramatic**, **striking**, atau **significant** untuk **emphasize** pentingnya isu itu.

**Ciri-ciri:**
- Informasi biasanya **shocking**, **surprising**, atau **significant**
- Purpose: make readers **pay attention** atau **realize the importance**
- Kadang ada emotif words atau strong language

**Contoh:**

> "Climate change is an urgent crisis. **In fact, the past decade has been the hottest on record, with unprecedented wildfires, floods, and droughts occurring worldwide.**"

**Analisis:**
- **Informasi spesifik:** "the past decade has been the hottest on record..."
- **Fungsi:** **To emphasize** the urgency of climate change.

**Soal yang mungkin muncul:**
"The author mentions the record-breaking temperatures to..."
- **Jawaban:** To emphasize the seriousness of climate change.

---

### **Fungsi 8: To Contrast (Membandingkan/Mempertentangkan)**

Kalau penulis lagi **compare** atau **contrast** dua hal, dia bisa menyebutkan informasi spesifik tentang **masing-masing** untuk **highlight** perbedaan atau persamaan.

**Ciri-ciri:**
- Ada dua atau lebih hal yang dibandingkan
- Informasi spesifik untuk **each side**
- Biasanya ada contrast words: "however," "on the other hand," "while," "whereas"

**Contoh:**

> "Traditional education requires students to attend physical classes. **In contrast, online learning allows students to study from anywhere with internet access.**"

**Analisis:**
- **Informasi spesifik:** "online learning allows students to study from anywhere..."
- **Fungsi:** **To contrast** online learning with traditional education.

**Soal yang mungkin muncul:**
"The author mentions online learning to..."
- **Jawaban:** To contrast it with traditional education.

---

### **Cara Menjawab Soal "Purpose of Mentioning"**

#### **Step 1: Identify informasi spesifik yang ditanyakan**

Baca soal dengan cermat. Cari tahu **apa** yang ditanyakan—data? nama orang? contoh?

**Contoh soal:**
"Why does the author mention the study by Harvard University?"

→ Informasi spesifik: "study by Harvard University"

---

#### **Step 2: Locate informasi itu di teks**

Cari di mana informasi itu muncul. Baca kalimat yang mengandung informasi itu, **PLUS** kalimat sebelum dan sesudahnya.

**Contoh teks:**
> "Regular exercise is beneficial for mental health. **A study by Harvard University found that people who exercise regularly have lower rates of depression.** This suggests that physical activity can improve mood."

**Lokasi:** Kalimat kedua (middle).

---

#### **Step 3: Pahami konteks sekitar**

Tanya diri sendiri:
- **Apa yang dibahas sebelum informasi ini?** (Main point atau klaim)
- **Apa yang dibahas setelah informasi ini?** (Penjelasan lebih lanjut atau conclusion)

**Analisis konteks:**
- **Sebelum:** "Regular exercise is beneficial for mental health." (Klaim)
- **Informasi:** "A study by Harvard University..." (Bukti)
- **Sesudah:** "This suggests that physical activity can improve mood." (Conclusion dari bukti)

---

#### **Step 4: Tentukan fungsi**

Dari konteks, tentukan **kenapa** penulis nyebutin informasi itu. Apakah untuk:
- Support argument?
- Provide evidence?
- Give example?
- Illustrate concept?
- Add credibility?
- Clarify?
- Emphasize?
- Contrast?

**Analisis:**
- Fungsi: **To provide evidence** atau **to support the claim** that exercise is beneficial for mental health.

---

#### **Step 5: Match dengan opsi jawaban**

Pilih opsi yang paling sesuai dengan fungsi yang kamu identify.

**Opsi jawaban:**
A. To provide evidence that exercise improves mental health. ✅
B. To describe the methodology of the study. ❌
C. To compare different types of exercise. ❌
D. To introduce a new topic. ❌

**Jawaban:** A

---

### **Jebakan Umum dalam Soal "Purpose of Mentioning"**

#### **Jebakan 1: Confusing fungsi dengan isi**

Opsi jawaban yang cuma **restate** isi informasi, tapi **bukan** fungsinya.

**Contoh:**
Soal: "Why does the author mention that 60% of people prefer online shopping?"

**Jebakan:**
- "To state that online shopping is popular." ❌ (Ini cuma restate isi, bukan fungsi)

**Jawaban yang benar:**
- "To support the argument that e-commerce is growing." ✅ (Ini fungsinya)

---

#### **Jebakan 2: Opsi yang terlalu spesifik atau terlalu umum**

**Terlalu spesifik:** Fokus ke detail kecil yang ga represent fungsi utama.

**Contoh:**
Soal: "Why does the author mention Amsterdam's cycling infrastructure?"

**Jebakan:**
- "To discuss the design of bike lanes in Amsterdam." ❌ (Terlalu spesifik)

**Jawaban yang benar:**
- "To provide an example of green transportation." ✅

**Terlalu umum:** Opsi yang bisa apply ke banyak informasi lain.

**Jebakan:**
- "To provide information to readers." ❌ (Terlalu umum)

---

#### **Jebakan 3: Salah mengidentifikasi konteks**

Kalau kamu ga baca kalimat sebelum/sesudah, kamu bisa salah tangkep fungsinya.

**Contoh:**
> "Many people believe that social media is harmful. **However, a study shows that social media can help people stay connected.** This suggests that the effects of social media are complex."

Soal: "Why does the author mention the study?"

**Jebakan:**
- "To support the claim that social media is harmful." ❌ (Salah! Ini actually **counter** klaim itu)

**Jawaban yang benar:**
- "To present a contrasting view." ✅

### **Latihan Analisis (Tanpa Soal)**

**Paragraf:**
> "Renewable energy is the future of power generation. **According to the International Energy Agency, renewable energy sources now account for nearly 30% of global electricity production.** This marks a significant shift from fossil fuels."

**Pertanyaan:** "Why does the author mention the statistic from the International Energy Agency?"

**Analisis:**
- **Informasi spesifik:** "renewable energy sources now account for nearly 30%..."
- **Konteks sebelum:** "Renewable energy is the future..." (Klaim)
- **Konteks sesudah:** "This marks a significant shift..." (Conclusion)
- **Fungsi:** **To provide evidence** atau **to support the claim** that renewable energy is the future.

---

**Paragraf:**
> "Urban farming is becoming popular in many cities. **For example, in Singapore, vertical farms are being used to grow vegetables in limited spaces.** This innovative approach helps reduce food transportation costs."

**Pertanyaan:** "Why does the author mention Singapore?"

**Analisis:**
- **Informasi spesifik:** "in Singapore, vertical farms..."
- **Konteks sebelum:** "Urban farming is becoming popular..." (General statement)
- **Konteks sesudah:** "This innovative approach..." (Benefit)
- **Fungsi:** **To give an example** of urban farming.

---

**Paragraf:**
> "Vaccines are crucial for public health. **Dr. Jane Smith, a leading immunologist, states that vaccines have eradicated diseases like smallpox and polio.** Without vaccines, these diseases would still be widespread."

**Pertanyaan:** "Why does the author mention Dr. Jane Smith?"

**Analisis:**
- **Informasi spesifik:** "Dr. Jane Smith, a leading immunologist..."
- **Konteks sebelum:** "Vaccines are crucial..." (Klaim)
- **Konteks sesudah:** "Without vaccines..." (Further emphasis)
- **Fungsi:** **To add credibility** to the claim about vaccines.

---

### **Tips Menguasai "Purpose of Mentioning"**

1. **Always read the context:**
   Jangan cuma baca informasi yang disebutin. Baca **kalimat sebelum** dan **kalimat sesudah** untuk understand fungsinya.

2. **Identify the main claim first:**
   Cari tahu apa **main point** atau **argument** yang lagi dikembangin penulis. Informasi spesifik biasanya **support** main point itu.

3. **Ask "Why this? Why here?":**
   Tanya kenapa penulis pilih informasi **ini** dan kenapa di **sini**. Apa hubungannya dengan argument?

4. **Match fungsi dengan vocabulary:**
   Kalau fungsinya "kasih bukti," opsi jawaban kemungkinan pake kata **"to provide evidence"** atau **"to support"**.

5. **Eliminate jebakan:**
   - Jangan pilih opsi yang cuma **restate isi** tanpa explain fungsi
   - Avoid opsi yang terlalu spesifik atau terlalu umum

---

### **Rangkuman Materi 2.5**

✅ **Purpose of Mentioning:** Kenapa penulis nyebutin informasi spesifik  
✅ **Fungsi umum:** Support argument, provide evidence, give example, illustrate, add credibility, clarify, emphasize, contrast  
✅ **Cara menjawab:** Identify → Locate → Understand context → Determine function → Match dengan opsi  
✅ **Jebakan:** Confusing isi dengan fungsi, terlalu spesifik/umum, salah identify konteks  
✅ **Tips:** Read context (before + after), identify main claim, ask "why this?", match fungsi dengan vocabulary
