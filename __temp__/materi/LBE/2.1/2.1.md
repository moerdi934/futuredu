# Section 2: Struktur dan Organisasi Teks

## Topik 2.1: Organization of Ideas

---

### **Materi 2.1.1: Pengenalan Organization of Ideas**

Bayangin kamu lagi baca novel thriller yang seru banget. Kalau ceritanya loncat-loncat nggak jelas, kadang di chapter 1, tiba-tiba chapter 3, terus balik lagi ke chapter 2... pasti bikin bingung kan? Nah, itulah pentingnya **organization** atau **pengorganisasian ide** dalam sebuah teks!

**Organization of Ideas** adalah cara penulis menyusun dan mengurutkan informasi dalam sebuah teks supaya mudah dipahami dan mengalir dengan logis. Bayangin ini kayak kamu lagi ngasih direction ke temen yang mau ke rumahmu—kalau urutannya acak-acakan, temenmu bisa nyasar. Tapi kalau kamu jelasin dengan urutan yang jelas (belok kanan di perempatan pertama, lurus terus sampai ada alfamart, terus belok kiri), pasti sampai deh!

#### **Kenapa Organization Penting?**

1. **Coherence (Kepaduan)**: Ide-ide dalam teks saling terhubung dengan logis. Nggak ada lompatan pemikiran yang bikin pembaca mikir "Lho, kok tiba-tiba ngomongin ini?"

2. **Cohesion (Keterkaitan)**: Kalimat-kalimat dan paragraf-paragraf nempel satu sama lain kayak puzzle yang pas. Ada "lem" berupa transition words dan reference words yang bikin semuanya menyatu.

3. **Comprehension**: Pembaca bisa mengikuti alur pemikiran penulis dengan mudah. Bayangkan kamu lagi ikutin resep masak—kalau urutannya jelas, masakanmu jadi. Kalau acak-acakan, bisa gosong atau malah mentah!

#### **Variasi Pertanyaan dalam Soal SNBT**

Soal tentang organization biasanya muncul dalam bentuk:
- "How is the information in the passage organized?"
- "How does paragraph 2 relate to paragraph 1?"
- "How does the author organize the ideas in the passage?"
- "Which of the following best describes the organization of the passage?"
- "In organizing the ideas, the author starts by..."

**Trik Jitu**: Kalau nemu soal kayak gini, jangan langsung baca seluruh teks dulu! Coba lihat **kalimat pertama setiap paragraf**—ini kayak "map" yang ngasih kamu gambaran besar tentang gimana teks disusun.

#### **Pattern Recognition: Kenali Pola Organisasi**

Setiap teks punya "kepribadian" dalam cara penyusunannya. Seperti orang yang suka ngomong kronologis (cerita dari awal sampai akhir), ada juga yang suka bandingin A vs B, atau yang suka jelasin masalah terus kasih solusi. Nah, mengenali pola ini adalah **kunci utama** untuk menjawab soal organization dengan cepat dan akurat.

Bayangkan kamu lagi detektif yang harus cari petunjuk—transition words seperti "first, then, however, therefore" adalah jejak kaki yang bakal ngarahin kamu ke jawaban!

#### **Hubungan dengan Main Idea**

Organization itu kayak **kerangka rumah**, sedangkan Main Idea adalah **pondasi**-nya. Kalau kamu udah paham Main Idea dari setiap paragraf (ingat topik 1.1 kan?), maka memahami organization jadi jauh lebih mudah. Kamu bisa lihat:
- Paragraf 1 ngomongin apa?
- Paragraf 2 ngomongin apa?
- Hubungannya gimana? Melanjutkan? Kontras? Kasih contoh?

**Jebakan Umum**:
- **Terlalu fokus pada detail**: Soal organization tanya tentang struktur BESAR teks, bukan detail kecil di satu paragraf.
- **Nggak perhatiin transition words**: Kata-kata kayak "however, therefore, for example" itu petunjuk EMAS tentang hubungan antar ide!
- **Salah bedain pola**: Misalnya mikir teksnya problem-solution padahal sebenarnya cause-effect. Hati-hati!

### **Materi 2.1.2: Analisis Pola Organisasi Teks**

Oke, sekarang saatnya kita masuk ke "jurus-jurus" pola organisasi teks! Setiap pola punya ciri khas dan "jejak" yang bisa kamu deteksi. Ini kayak kamu belajar bahasa isyarat—setelah tau polanya, kamu langsung paham maksudnya!

---

#### **1. Chronological Order (Urutan Kronologis/Waktu)**

**Karakteristik**: Informasi disusun berdasarkan urutan waktu—dari yang paling awal sampai yang paling akhir. Kayak timeline di Instagram Story yang berurutan!

**Ciri-ciri Signal Words**:
- **Time markers**: first, second, third, then, next, after that, finally, lastly, eventually
- **Specific dates/times**: in 1945, during the 1990s, last year, yesterday, at 3 PM
- **Sequence words**: before, after, while, meanwhile, subsequently, previously

**Contoh Penggunaan**:
Pola ini sering banget muncul di:
- **Historical texts** (sejarah peristiwa)
- **Biography** (perjalanan hidup seseorang)
- **Process description** (langkah-langkah melakukan sesuatu)
- **Narrative texts** (cerita dengan plot kronologis)

**Contoh Bacaan**:

*"The history of smartphones began in 1992 when IBM introduced the first smartphone called Simon. **Then**, in 1996, Nokia released the Nokia 9000 Communicator. **After that**, in 2007, Apple revolutionized the smartphone industry with the first iPhone. **Subsequently**, Android emerged as a competitor in 2008, and **finally**, by 2010, smartphones had become mainstream worldwide."*

Lihat nggak? Ada jelas urutan waktunya dari 1992 → 1996 → 2007 → 2008 → 2010. Plus banyak transition words yang nunjukin urutan waktu!

**Trik Deteksi Cepat**:
- Scan cepat untuk cari **angka tahun** atau **time expressions**
- Lihat apakah ada **sequence dari awal ke akhir** yang jelas
- Check apakah setiap paragraf maju dalam timeline

**Jebakan**:
- **Flashback**: Kadang teks mulai dari tengah, terus flashback ke awal. Ini tetep chronological, tapi butuh extra hati-hati!
- **Multiple timelines**: Ada teks yang ngomongin dua kejadian berbeda dengan timeline masing-masing. Jangan sampai ketuker!

#### **2. Cause and Effect (Sebab-Akibat)**

**Karakteristik**: Teks menjelaskan **penyebab** (cause) dari suatu kejadian dan **akibat** (effect) yang ditimbulkan. Atau sebaliknya—mulai dari akibat, terus jelasin penyebabnya. Kayak main domino—satu jatuh, yang lain ikutan!

**Ciri-ciri Signal Words**:
- **Cause markers**: because, since, due to, owing to, as a result of, caused by, stems from
- **Effect markers**: therefore, thus, consequently, as a result, so, hence, leads to, results in, causes
- **Condition markers**: if...then, when...consequently

**Contoh Penggunaan**:
- **Scientific explanations** (fenomena alam, proses kimia/fisika)
- **Social issues** (masalah sosial dan dampaknya)
- **Health topics** (penyakit dan penyebabnya, atau treatment dan hasilnya)
- **Environmental texts** (polusi → global warming → climate change)

**Contoh Bacaan**:

*"Deforestation has become a critical environmental issue. **Because** large areas of forests are being cleared for agriculture and urban development, wildlife habitats are destroyed. **As a result**, many species face extinction. **Furthermore**, the loss of trees **leads to** increased carbon dioxide in the atmosphere **since** trees absorb CO2 during photosynthesis. **Consequently**, this contributes to global warming and climate change."*

Ada chain of causation di sini:
- Deforestation (cause) → habitat destruction (effect) → extinction (further effect)
- Loss of trees (cause) → increased CO2 (effect) → global warming (further effect)

**Variasi Pola**:
1. **Simple Cause-Effect**: Satu sebab → satu akibat
2. **Multiple Causes**: Beberapa sebab → satu akibat
3. **Chain Reaction**: Sebab → akibat pertama → akibat kedua → akibat ketiga (domino effect!)
4. **Effect-Cause**: Mulai dari akibat dulu, baru jelasin penyebabnya (backward organization)

**Trik Deteksi Cepat**:
- Cari kata "because, therefore, as a result, consequently"—ini golden signals!
- Identifikasi **what happened** vs **why it happened**
- Perhatikan apakah ada **chain of events** (satu kejadian memicu kejadian lain)

**Jebakan**:
- **Bukan causation tapi correlation**: Dua hal terjadi bersamaan bukan berarti yang satu menyebabkan yang lain! Contoh: "Ice cream sales increase in summer, and drowning cases also increase." Bukan ice cream yang bikin orang tenggelam—keduanya disebabkan faktor ketiga (cuaca panas)!
- **Reversed cause-effect**: Kadang effect disebutin duluan, baru cause-nya. Jangan ketipu!

#### **3. Problem and Solution (Masalah-Solusi)**

**Karakteristik**: Teks dimulai dengan **mengidentifikasi masalah**, lalu menawarkan **solusi** atau cara mengatasinya. Ini kayak konsultasi dokter—jelasin sakit apa, terus dikasih obat!

**Ciri-ciri Signal Words**:
- **Problem markers**: problem, issue, challenge, difficulty, dilemma, concern, crisis
- **Solution markers**: solution, answer, resolve, address, tackle, overcome, prevent, remedy, fix
- **Transition**: to solve this, one way to address, in response to

**Contoh Penggunaan**:
- **Social/environmental issues** dan cara mengatasinya
- **Technical problems** dan troubleshooting
- **Business challenges** dan strategies
- **Health problems** dan treatments

**Contoh Bacaan**:

*"Urban traffic congestion has become a major **problem** in big cities worldwide. During rush hours, commuters spend hours stuck in traffic, leading to lost productivity and increased stress. **To address this issue**, many cities have implemented several **solutions**. First, they have expanded public transportation systems to encourage people to leave their cars at home. Second, some cities have introduced congestion pricing, charging fees for driving in crowded areas during peak hours. **These measures have proven effective** in reducing traffic and improving quality of life."*

Structure-nya jelas:
1. **Problem identification**: Traffic congestion + dampaknya
2. **Solutions offered**: Public transport expansion + congestion pricing
3. **Result/effectiveness**: (kadang ada) → measures proven effective

**Variasi Pola**:
1. **Single Problem - Multiple Solutions**: Satu masalah, beberapa solusi ditawarkan
2. **Multiple Problems - Comprehensive Solution**: Beberapa masalah, satu solusi besar
3. **Problem → Failed Solutions → Better Solution**: Ngomongin solusi yang udah dicoba tapi gagal, terus kasih solusi yang lebih baik

**Trik Deteksi Cepat**:
- Paragraf pertama biasanya ngebahas **masalah dan dampaknya**
- Paragraf berikutnya ngebahas **solusi atau cara mengatasi**
- Look for "to address, to solve, to tackle, in response"

**Jebakan**:
- **Problem tanpa solution**: Kadang teks cuma ngebahas problem aja tanpa kasih solusi—ini **problem description**, bukan problem-solution!
- **Implisit vs eksplisit**: Kadang solusi nggak disebutin eksplisit dengan kata "solution"—kamu harus infer dari konteks
- **Evaluasi solusi**: Kadang ditambahin evaluasi apakah solusinya berhasil atau nggak—ini bikin pattern lebih kompleks

#### **4. Compare and Contrast (Perbandingan dan Pertentangan)**

**Karakteristik**: Teks membandingkan **similarities (persamaan)** dan/atau **differences (perbedaan)** antara dua atau lebih hal. Kayak kamu ngebandingin iPhone vs Android—ada yang sama, ada yang beda!

**Ciri-ciri Signal Words**:
- **Comparison (similarities)**: similarly, likewise, in the same way, just as, both, also, too, as well as, comparable to
- **Contrast (differences)**: however, but, on the other hand, whereas, while, in contrast, unlike, conversely, although, nevertheless, despite

**Contoh Penggunaan**:
- **Comparing theories/concepts**
- **Comparing products/technologies**
- **Historical comparisons** (era vs era)
- **Cultural comparisons**

**Contoh Bacaan**:

*"Traditional education and online learning have distinct characteristics. **Both** methods aim to deliver knowledge to students, and **similarly**, they require dedication and time management from learners. **However**, they differ significantly in delivery format. Traditional education involves face-to-face interaction in physical classrooms, **whereas** online learning takes place in virtual environments. **In addition**, traditional classes follow fixed schedules, **while** online courses often offer flexibility. **Despite** these differences, **both** can be effective when properly implemented."*

Structure-nya:
1. Introduction to both subjects
2. Similarities → Both aim to deliver knowledge, both require dedication
3. Differences → Delivery format, schedule flexibility
4. Conclusion → Both can be effective

**Variasi Pola**:
1. **Block Method**: Bahas A lengkap dulu, terus bahas B lengkap → compare
2. **Point-by-Point Method**: Bahas aspek 1 (A vs B), aspek 2 (A vs B), aspek 3 (A vs B)
3. **Similarities First, Then Differences**: Atau sebaliknya
4. **Emphasis on Contrast**: Lebih fokus ke perbedaan daripada persamaan

**Trik Deteksi Cepat**:
- Ada **dua subjek atau lebih** yang dibahas
- Banyak **"however, while, whereas, on the other hand"**
- Struktur **parallel**—aspek yang sama dibahas untuk kedua subjek

**Jebakan**:
- **Comparison aja atau contrast aja**: Kadang teks HANYA compare (similarities) atau HANYA contrast (differences)—bukan keduanya!
- **Implicit comparison**: Kadang comparison nggak eksplisit—kamu harus infer dari deskripsi dua hal yang disandingkan
- **Unbalanced comparison**: Satu subjek dibahas lebih banyak dari yang lain—ini bisa mengecoh!

#### **5. General to Specific (Umum ke Khusus)**

**Karakteristik**: Teks dimulai dengan **pernyataan umum** (general statement), kemudian diikuti oleh **details, examples, atau elaborasi** yang lebih spesifik. Kayak kamu ngomong "Aku suka buah" (general), terus jelasin "Terutama apel, mangga, sama jeruk" (specific).

**Ciri-ciri Signal Words**:
- **General statements**: in general, generally speaking, overall, broadly
- **Specification markers**: specifically, particularly, especially, for example, for instance, such as, including

**Contoh Penggunaan**:
- **Topic introduction** dengan examples
- **Classification texts** (kategori umum → subkategori spesifik)
- **Definition texts** (definisi umum → aplikasi spesifik)

**Contoh Bacaan**:

*"**Generally speaking**, exercise provides numerous health benefits. **Specifically**, regular physical activity strengthens the cardiovascular system, reducing the risk of heart disease. **For example**, a study found that people who exercise for 30 minutes daily have 40% lower risk of heart attacks. **In addition**, exercise improves mental health by releasing endorphins. **For instance**, many people report feeling less stressed and more energized after a workout session."*

Structure-nya:
1. General statement → Exercise has health benefits
2. Specific benefit 1 → Cardiovascular health + example/data
3. Specific benefit 2 → Mental health + example

**Trik Deteksi Cepat**:
- Paragraf pertama atau kalimat pertama biasanya **broad/general**
- Paragraf/kalimat berikutnya **narrow down** dengan details dan examples
- Banyak "for example, for instance, such as"

**Jebakan**:
- **Reversed pattern (Specific to General)**: Kadang dimulai dari contoh spesifik dulu, baru kasih kesimpulan general di akhir! Ini kebalikannya—tapi tetep valid pattern!

---

#### **6. Order of Importance (Urutan Tingkat Kepentingan)**

**Karakteristik**: Ide disusun berdasarkan tingkat kepentingan—bisa dari **paling penting ke kurang penting** (most to least important) atau **sebaliknya** (least to most important, building up ke climax).

**Ciri-ciri Signal Words**:
- **Importance markers**: most importantly, significantly, primarily, above all, first and foremost, critically, essentially
- **Ranking markers**: first, second, third, finally, lastly, last but not least
- **Emphasis markers**: especially, particularly, notably, more importantly

**Contoh Penggunaan**:
- **Argumentative texts** (argumen terkuat duluan atau di akhir)
- **Priority lists** (langkah paling penting duluan)
- **Persuasive texts** (save the best for last)

**Contoh Bacaan**:

*"When preparing for a job interview, several factors contribute to success. **Most importantly**, research the company thoroughly. Understanding the company's values, products, and culture shows genuine interest. **Secondly**, prepare answers to common interview questions. Practice will help you respond confidently. **Additionally**, dress appropriately for the industry and position. **Finally**, while less critical but still important, arrive early to demonstrate punctuality and reduce stress."*

Di sini, penulis arrange dari **most important** (research) ke **less critical** (arriving early).

**Trik Deteksi Cepat**:
- Lihat apakah ada **ranking/numbering** eksplisit (first, second, third)
- Check apakah ada **superlative** (most important, least significant)
- Perhatikan apakah penulis **build up** (climax) atau **start strong**

**Jebakan**:
- **Misleading first/second/third**: Kadang numbering nggak nunjukin importance tapi chronology atau just listing—baca carefully!

---

### **Tips Mengenali Pola Organisasi dengan Cepat:**

1. **Scan transition words** di awal setiap paragraf dan dalam paragraf
2. **Lihat struktur paragraf pertama**—ini sering jadi petunjuk pola keseluruhan
3. **Identifikasi Main Idea tiap paragraf** terus lihat hubungannya
4. **Cari signal words** yang karakteristik untuk setiap pola
5. **Jangan terjebak detail**—fokus pada BIG PICTURE struktur teks

### **Materi 2.1.3: Analisis Hubungan Antar Paragraf**

Nah, sekarang kita masuk ke level yang lebih seru lagi! Kalau tadi kita bahas pola organisasi dalam **keseluruhan teks**, sekarang kita zoom in ke hubungan **antara paragraf yang satu dengan paragraf yang lain**. Ini kayak kamu ngelihat gimana setiap carriage di kereta api terhubung—masing-masing punya fungsi, tapi semua bergerak ke arah yang sama!

Dalam soal SNBT, sering banget muncul pertanyaan kayak:
- "How does paragraph 2 relate to paragraph 1?"
- "The relationship between paragraph 3 and paragraph 4 is..."
- "What is the function of paragraph 5 in relation to the previous paragraphs?"

**Kunci utama**: Pahami **Main Idea setiap paragraf**, terus lihat **hubungan logis** antar main idea tersebut.

---

#### **1. Sequential Relationship (Hubungan Berurutan)**

**Karakteristik**: Paragraf-paragraf mengikuti **urutan logis atau kronologis**. Paragraf berikutnya melanjutkan atau maju dari paragraf sebelumnya. Kayak episode series yang ngelanjutin cerita dari episode sebelumnya!

**Contoh Situasi**:
- **Time sequence**: Paragraf 1 ngomongin events di 1990s, Paragraf 2 ngomongin 2000s, Paragraf 3 ngomongin 2010s
- **Step-by-step process**: Paragraf 1 step 1, Paragraf 2 step 2, dst.
- **Logical progression**: Paragraf 1 introduce concept, Paragraf 2 elaborate, Paragraf 3 provide examples

**Signal Words Antar Paragraf**:
- Next, then, after that, subsequently, following this
- Later, afterward, eventually
- The next stage, the following step

**Contoh Struktur**:

*Paragraf 1*: "In the early stages of smartphone development, devices were bulky and had limited features..."

*Paragraf 2*: "**As technology advanced**, manufacturers began to miniaturize components. **Subsequently**, smartphones in the 2000s became more compact..."

*Paragraf 3*: "**The next major breakthrough came** with the introduction of touchscreen technology..."

Lihat? Ada **clear progression** dari early stages → advances → breakthrough. Masing-masing paragraf maju dari yang sebelumnya dalam timeline.

**Ciri-ciri**:
- **Information builds** dari satu paragraf ke paragraf lain
- Setiap paragraf adalah **next step** dalam narasi atau explanation
- **Chronological markers** atau **step indicators** jelas terlihat

**Trik Identifikasi**:
- Check apakah ada **time advancement** atau **progression dalam process**
- Lihat apakah paragraf berikutnya **continue the story/explanation**
- Baca kalimat pertama paragraf berikutnya—biasanya ada penanda "next, then, following"

---

#### **2. Contrastive Relationship (Hubungan Pertentangan)**

**Karakteristik**: Paragraf menyajikan **ide yang kontras atau berlawanan** dengan paragraf sebelumnya. Ini kayak debat—satu paragraf kasih argumen A, paragraf berikutnya kasih argumen B yang berbeda atau bahkan bertentangan!

**Contoh Situasi**:
- **Different perspectives**: Paragraf 1 pros, Paragraf 2 cons
- **Comparison**: Paragraf 1 describe A, Paragraf 2 describe B (dengan fokus perbedaan)
- **Problem vs solution**: Paragraf 1 masalah, Paragraf 2 solusi yang kontras dengan masalah
- **Traditional vs modern**: Paragraf 1 old ways, Paragraf 2 new ways

**Signal Words Antar Paragraf**:
- However, nevertheless, nonetheless, yet, still
- On the other hand, conversely, in contrast, by contrast
- Despite this, although, whereas, while
- Alternatively, rather

**Contoh Struktur**:

*Paragraf 1*: "Traditional education offers valuable face-to-face interaction. Students benefit from immediate feedback and personal connections with teachers. The structured environment also helps maintain discipline..."

*Paragraf 2*: "**On the other hand**, online learning provides unprecedented flexibility. Students can access materials anytime, anywhere, making education more accessible to those with busy schedules or geographic limitations. **In contrast to** traditional classrooms, online platforms often offer personalized learning paths..."

Ada **clear contrast** di sini:
- Traditional → personal interaction vs Online → flexibility
- Traditional → structured vs Online → personalized

**Ciri-ciri**:
- **Opposing ideas atau perspectives**
- Penggunaan **contrastive transition words** di awal paragraf berikutnya
- **Different aspects** dari same topic dibahas dengan emphasis berbeda

**Trik Identifikasi**:
- Look for "however, on the other hand, in contrast" di awal paragraf
- Check apakah ada **opposing adjectives** (traditional vs modern, positive vs negative)
- Lihat apakah tone atau perspective berubah dari paragraf sebelumnya

**Jebakan**:
- **Subtle contrast**: Kadang kontras nggak eksplisit—nggak ada "however" tapi ide-nya tetep berlawanan
- **Complement vs contrast**: Kadang seperti contrast tapi sebenarnya saling melengkapi (different aspects, not opposing)

---

#### **3. Elaborative Relationship (Hubungan Elaborasi/Pengembangan)**

**Karakteristik**: Paragraf berikutnya **mengembangkan, menjelaskan lebih detail, atau mengekspansi** ide dari paragraf sebelumnya. Kayak kamu nge-zoom suatu foto—paragraf pertama big picture, paragraf berikutnya detail closeup!

**Contoh Situasi**:
- **General → Specific**: Paragraf 1 general statement, Paragraf 2 specific details
- **Claim → Evidence**: Paragraf 1 makes a claim, Paragraf 2 provides supporting evidence
- **Introduction → Explanation**: Paragraf 1 introduce concept, Paragraf 2 explain how it works
- **Overview → Deep dive**: Paragraf 1 overview, Paragraf 2-3 analyze different aspects in depth

**Signal Words Antar Paragraf**:
- Moreover, furthermore, additionally, in addition
- More specifically, to elaborate, to clarify
- In particular, especially, notably
- For instance, for example (kalau kasih example sebagai elaboration)

**Contoh Struktur**:

*Paragraf 1*: "Climate change poses significant threats to global ecosystems. Rising temperatures affect weather patterns, ocean currents, and biodiversity worldwide."

*Paragraf 2*: "**More specifically**, rising sea levels threaten coastal communities. As polar ice melts, low-lying areas face increased flooding. Cities like Jakarta, Miami, and Venice are already experiencing regular inundation. Scientists predict that by 2050, millions of people may need to relocate..."

Paragraf 2 adalah **elaboration** dari "threats to global ecosystems"—specifically focusing on sea level rise dan coastal communities.

**Ciri-ciri**:
- **Same topic**, more detail
- Paragraf berikutnya **dig deeper** ke specific aspect dari paragraf sebelumnya
- Kadang ada **examples, statistics, atau case studies** sebagai elaboration

**Trik Identifikasi**:
- Check apakah paragraf berikutnya **still talking about same main idea** tapi dengan more depth
- Look for "more specifically, in particular, for example"
- Lihat apakah ada **narrowing down** dari general ke specific

**Jebakan**:
- **New topic vs elaboration**: Kadang susah bedain—apakah ini elaboration dari topic yang sama atau new related topic? Check apakah masih directly related ke main idea paragraf sebelumnya!

---

#### **4. Exemplification Relationship (Hubungan Pemberian Contoh)**

**Karakteristik**: Paragraf berikutnya memberikan **contoh konkret, ilustrasi, atau case study** dari konsep abstract atau general statement di paragraf sebelumnya. Ini kayak guru yang jelasin teori, terus kasih contoh soal supaya jelas!

**Contoh Situasi**:
- **Theory → Application**: Paragraf 1 explain theory, Paragraf 2 show real-world example
- **General principle → Specific case**: Paragraf 1 state principle, Paragraf 2 illustrate dengan case study
- **Abstract concept → Concrete illustration**: Paragraf 1 abstract idea, Paragraf 2 tangible example

**Signal Words Antar Paragraf**:
- For example, for instance
- To illustrate, as an illustration
- A case in point, consider the case of
- One example of this, such as

**Contoh Struktur**:

*Paragraf 1*: "Effective leadership requires emotional intelligence. Leaders must understand and manage not only their own emotions but also recognize and respond to the emotions of their team members. This ability fosters trust and collaboration."

*Paragraf 2*: "**A prime example of this** can be seen in the leadership style of Satya Nadella, CEO of Microsoft. When he took over in 2014, Microsoft's culture was competitive and siloed. Nadella emphasized empathy and collaboration, encouraging employees to adopt a 'growth mindset.' **This illustrates** how emotional intelligence in leadership can transform organizational culture..."

Paragraf 2 adalah **concrete example** (Satya Nadella) dari **abstract concept** (emotional intelligence) di paragraf 1.

**Ciri-ciri**:
- **Shift from abstract to concrete**
- Paragraf sebelumnya biasanya **theoretical atau conceptual**
- Paragraf exemplification biasanya **specific, detailed, may mention names, dates, places**

**Trik Identifikasi**:
- Look for "for example, to illustrate, consider the case of"
- Check apakah ada **shift ke specific story atau case**
- Lihat apakah paragraf sebelumnya **general/abstract** dan berikutnya **concrete/specific**

---

#### **5. Conclusive Relationship (Hubungan Kesimpulan)**

**Karakteristik**: Paragraf berikutnya (biasanya paragraf terakhir) **menyimpulkan atau merangkum** ide-ide dari paragraf-paragraf sebelumnya. Atau bisa juga memberikan **final thought, broader implication, atau recommendation** based on discussion sebelumnya.

**Contoh Situasi**:
- **Summary**: Merangkum poin-poin utama
- **Conclusion**: Menarik kesimpulan dari arguments atau evidence sebelumnya
- **Implication**: Mendiskusikan broader implications dari discussion
- **Recommendation**: Memberikan saran atau call to action based on analysis

**Signal Words Antar Paragraf**:
- In conclusion, to conclude, in summary, to sum up
- Therefore, thus, hence, as a result (jika conclusive dalam bentuk logical conclusion)
- Overall, all in all, ultimately
- Given these points, based on this discussion

**Contoh Struktur**:

*Paragraf 1*: "Regular exercise benefits cardiovascular health..."
*Paragraf 2*: "Physical activity also improves mental wellbeing..."
*Paragraf 3*: "Additionally, exercise strengthens the immune system..."

*Paragraf 4*: "**Given these substantial benefits**, it is clear that regular physical activity should be a priority for everyone. **In conclusion**, incorporating just 30 minutes of exercise into daily routines can significantly enhance both physical and mental health, leading to improved quality of life."

Paragraf 4 **wraps up** semua benefits yang disebutkan di paragraf 1-3 dan kasih final recommendation.

**Ciri-ciri**:
- Biasanya **paragraf terakhir** dalam teks
- **Reiterates main points** without introducing new major information
- Might include **final thoughts, implications, atau recommendations**

**Trik Identifikasi**:
- Look for "in conclusion, therefore, thus, overall"
- Check apakah paragraf **summarizes atau wraps up** previous discussion
- Lihat apakah ada **broader perspective atau call to action**

**Jebakan**:
- **Conclusion vs new point**: Kadang paragraf terakhir bukan conclusion tapi introduce new related idea—ini **extension**, bukan conclusive!

---

### **Tips Menganalisis Hubungan Antar Paragraf dengan Cepat:**

1. **Baca kalimat pertama setiap paragraf**—ini biasanya berisi transition atau topic sentence yang nunjukin hubungan dengan paragraf sebelumnya

2. **Identifikasi transition words/phrases** di awal paragraf:
   - "However" → kemungkinan contrast
   - "For example" → kemungkinan exemplification
   - "Furthermore" → kemungkinan elaboration
   - "In conclusion" → conclusive

3. **Compare Main Idea paragraf-paragraf**:
   - Same topic, more detail → elaboration
   - Different perspective → contrast
   - Specific case dari general idea → exemplification
   - Summary dari previous ideas → conclusive

4. **Perhatikan tone dan content shift**:
   - Shift dari general ke specific → elaboration/exemplification
   - Shift dari positive ke negative (atau vice versa) → contrast
   - No shift, continuation → sequential

5. **Check chronology dan logic**:
   - Ada time advancement → sequential
   - Ada logical cause-effect → bisa sequential atau elaboration
   - Ada opposing views → contrast

### **Materi 2.1.4: Analisis Main Idea Tiap Paragraf untuk Mapping**

Oke, sekarang kita masuk ke teknik **mapping** yang super powerful! Ini kayak kamu bikin peta atau mind map dari suatu teks. Dengan mapping, kamu bisa **visualisasikan struktur teks** secara keseluruhan dan lihat hubungan antar bagian dengan jelas. Ini technique favorit banyak students karena bikin organization teks langsung terlihat jelas!

---

#### **Kenapa Mapping Penting?**

Bayangin kamu lagi di mall yang super gede. Kalau nggak ada peta atau directory, kamu bisa nyasar kan? Nah, mapping Main Idea tiap paragraf itu kayak bikin peta mall—kamu tau posisimu di mana, mau ke mana, dan gimana cara sampai ke sana!

**Benefits of Mapping**:
1. **Visual clarity**: Langsung keliatan big picture struktur teks
2. **Quick reference**: Kalau ada soal tentang specific info, kamu langsung tau paragraf mana yang harus dicek
3. **Organization understanding**: Kamu paham gimana ide-ide terhubung
4. **Time-efficient**: Nggak perlu baca ulang seluruh teks untuk jawab soal

---

#### **Teknik Identifikasi Main Idea per Paragraf dengan Cepat**

**Langkah 1: Fokus pada Kalimat Pertama (Topic Sentence)**

Dalam **80-90% paragraf akademik**, Main Idea ada di **kalimat pertama**! Ini karena penulis akademik biasanya use **deductive approach**—mulai dari general statement terus develop dengan details.

**Contoh**:

*Paragraf 1, Kalimat 1*: "Climate change has emerged as one of the most pressing global challenges of the 21st century."

Boom! Ini Main Idea paragraf 1 → Climate change sebagai tantangan global utama.

**Trik**: Baca **kalimat pertama setiap paragraf** dengan seksama—ini usually your main idea!

---

**Langkah 2: Check Kalimat Terakhir (Jika Kalimat Pertama Kurang Jelas)**

Kadang, kalimat pertama cuma **hook** atau **transition** dari paragraf sebelumnya. Kalau gitu, Main Idea biasanya ada di **kalimat terakhir** (inductive approach) atau **harus disimpulkan** dari keseluruhan paragraf.

**Contoh**:

*Paragraf 2*:
- Kalimat 1: "Various factors contribute to this phenomenon."
- Kalimat 2-5: Jelasin industrial emissions, deforestation, fossil fuels, etc.
- Kalimat terakhir: "These human activities have significantly accelerated global warming."

Kalimat pertama ("various factors") terlalu vague. Main Idea sebenarnya ada di kalimat terakhir: **Human activities accelerate global warming.**

---

**Langkah 3: Simpulkan dari Keseluruhan (Jika Implicit)**

Kadang, Main Idea **nggak eksplisit stated** dalam satu kalimat—kamu harus **infer dari keseluruhan paragraf**. Ini lebih challenging tapi tetep doable!

**Contoh**:

*Paragraf 3*:
- Kalimat 1: "Jakarta experiences regular flooding during rainy season."
- Kalimat 2: "Coastal cities in the US face hurricane threats."
- Kalimat 3: "Pacific island nations worry about submersion."
- Kalimat 4: "Arctic communities deal with melting permafrost."

Nggak ada satu kalimat yang eksplisit state Main Idea, tapi dari semua examples ini kamu bisa simpulin: **Climate change affects various regions globally in different ways.** Atau: **Different geographical areas face unique climate-related challenges.**

**Trik**: Kalau semua kalimat kasih examples atau details, coba tanya diri sendiri: "What's the common thread? What unites all these points?"

---

#### **Mapping Struktur: Ide Utama → Ide Pendukung → Detail Spesifik**

Setiap paragraf biasanya punya **hierarchical structure**:

```
MAIN IDEA (What's the paragraph about?)
├── Major Supporting Idea 1
│   ├── Detail/Example
│   └── Detail/Example
├── Major Supporting Idea 2
│   ├── Detail/Example
│   └── Detail/Example
└── Major Supporting Idea 3
    ├── Detail/Example
    └── Detail/Example
```

**Contoh Mapping**:

*Paragraf tentang Benefits of Exercise*:

**Main Idea**: Regular exercise provides multiple health benefits.

**Supporting Idea 1**: Physical benefits
- Details: Strengthens cardiovascular system, improves muscle tone, increases endurance

**Supporting Idea 2**: Mental benefits
- Details: Reduces stress, releases endorphins, improves mood

**Supporting Idea 3**: Social benefits
- Details: Group activities build community, team sports develop cooperation

Dengan mapping kayak gini, kamu **instantly understand** gimana paragraf diorganisir dan apa aja poin pentingnya!

---

#### **Identifikasi Pola Pengembangan Teks**

Setelah kamu **map Main Idea setiap paragraf**, langkah berikutnya adalah **identifikasi pola pengembangan** keseluruhan teks dengan melihat hubungan antar Main Ideas.

**Teknik**:
1. **List Main Idea tiap paragraf** dalam urutan
2. **Lihat hubungan** antar Main Ideas
3. **Identifikasi pattern**

**Contoh Mapping Full Text**:

**Teks tentang Evolution of Communication Technology**

- **Para 1 Main Idea**: Early forms of communication were limited and slow (smoke signals, messenger pigeons)
- **Para 2 Main Idea**: Telegraph revolutionized long-distance communication in the 19th century
- **Para 3 Main Idea**: Telephone enabled real-time voice communication
- **Para 4 Main Idea**: Internet transformed communication into instant, global connectivity
- **Para 5 Main Idea**: Social media platforms now shape how billions communicate daily

**Pattern Identified**: **Chronological Order** (time sequence dari earliest → modern) dengan **Progression theme** (each stage more advanced than previous)

---

**Contoh 2**:

**Teks tentang Benefits and Drawbacks of Remote Work**

- **Para 1 Main Idea**: Remote work offers flexibility and work-life balance
- **Para 2 Main Idea**: Employees save time and money on commuting
- **Para 3 Main Idea**: However, remote work can lead to isolation and loneliness
- **Para 4 Main Idea**: Lack of face-to-face interaction hinders team collaboration
- **Para 5 Main Idea**: Balance between remote and office work may be optimal solution

**Pattern Identified**: **Compare-Contrast** (Para 1-2 advantages, Para 3-4 disadvantages) → **Problem-Solution** (Para 5 proposed solution)

---

#### **Transition Words sebagai Penanda Hubungan Antar Paragraf**

Transition words atau phrases di **awal paragraf** adalah **golden clues** tentang hubungan paragraf itu dengan yang sebelumnya!

**Quick Reference Transition Meanings**:

| Transition | Meaning/Function |
|------------|------------------|
| Moreover, Furthermore, Additionally | **Adding information** (elaboration) |
| However, Nevertheless, On the other hand | **Contrasting** (opposing idea) |
| For example, For instance, To illustrate | **Exemplifying** (giving example) |
| Therefore, Thus, Consequently | **Concluding** (result/conclusion) |
| First, Second, Next, Finally | **Sequencing** (chronological atau order) |
| Similarly, Likewise, In the same way | **Comparing** (similarity) |
| In contrast, Conversely, Unlike | **Contrasting** (difference) |
| Specifically, In particular | **Specifying** (narrowing focus) |

**Contoh Analysis**:

*Paragraf 1*: "Online learning offers flexibility for students..."

*Paragraf 2*: "**Moreover**, digital platforms provide access to diverse resources..."
→ "Moreover" = **Adding more benefits**, so Para 2 **elaborates/continues** the positive aspects from Para 1

*Paragraf 3*: "**However**, technical issues can disrupt the learning process..."
→ "However" = **Introducing contrast**, so Para 3 presents **opposing view/drawbacks**

*Paragraf 4*: "**To illustrate**, consider the experience of university students during the pandemic..."
→ "To illustrate" = **Giving example**, so Para 4 provides **concrete illustration** of points made earlier

---

#### **Logical Progression: Bagaimana Ide Berkembang dari Paragraf ke Paragraf**

Ada beberapa **logical progressions** umum dalam teks akademik:

**1. Funnel Progression (General → Specific)**
```
Para 1: Very broad topic introduction
Para 2: Narrowing down to specific aspect
Para 3: Even more specific detail
Para 4: Detailed case study or example
```

**2. Building Block Progression (Foundation → Complex)**
```
Para 1: Basic concept/definition
Para 2: Add layer of complexity
Para 3: Add more complex layer
Para 4: Full complex understanding
```

**3. Problem-Analysis-Solution Progression**
```
Para 1: Introduce problem
Para 2: Analyze causes of problem
Para 3: Analyze effects of problem
Para 4: Propose solution(s)
Para 5: Evaluate solution effectiveness
```

**4. Thesis-Support Progression (Claim → Evidence)**
```
Para 1: State thesis/main argument
Para 2: Provide evidence/reason 1
Para 3: Provide evidence/reason 2
Para 4: Provide evidence/reason 3
Para 5: Reinforce thesis with synthesis
```

**Trik Identifikasi Progression**:
- **Lihat apakah scope melebar atau menyempit** dari paragraf ke paragraf
- **Check apakah complexity meningkat** atau tetap sama
- **Perhatikan apakah ada clear argument flow** (claim → support → conclusion)

---

#### **Consistency Check: Memastikan Semua Paragraf Mendukung Thesis/Main Idea Keseluruhan**

Setelah map Main Idea tiap paragraf, **verify consistency**:

**Ask yourself**:
1. Apakah semua paragraph Main Ideas **relate back** ke overall thesis/main idea teks?
2. Apakah ada paragraf yang **off-topic** atau **nggak support** main argument?
3. Apakah progression **logical dan coherent**?

**Contoh Consistency Check**:

**Overall Thesis**: Social media has transformed modern communication

**Paragraph Main Ideas**:
- Para 1: Social media enables instant global connectivity ✓ (supports)
- Para 2: Platforms like Facebook connect billions of users ✓ (supports with example)
- Para 3: Social media influences political movements ✓ (supports—transformation aspect)
- Para 4: However, privacy concerns arise from data collection ✓ (supports—another aspect of transformation, negative side)
- Para 5: Recipe for chocolate cake ✗ (OFF TOPIC! Doesn't support thesis)

Paragraf 5 clearly **inconsistent** dan **irrelevant** to thesis.

Dalam teks yang well-organized, **semua paragraf harus contribute** ke overall thesis atau main idea. Kalau ada yang nggak nyambung, itu **red flag** untuk coherence issue (atau bisa jadi **irrelevant sentence** yang harus diidentifikasi—ini kita bahas di materi selanjutnya!)

---

### **Practical Mapping Exercise Approach**

Ketika kamu baca teks SNBT, coba **quick mental mapping** dengan steps ini:

**Step 1**: Baca kalimat pertama every paragraph (max 30 detik untuk 5-paragraph text)

**Step 2**: **Mentally note** atau **tulis singkat** Main Idea each para (misal: P1-intro, P2-benefit, P3-drawback, P4-example, P5-conclusion)

**Step 3**: **Identify pattern** (chronological? Problem-solution? Compare-contrast?)

**Step 4**: **Check transition words** untuk confirm relationships

**Step 5**: Sekarang kamu **siap jawab soal organization** dengan cepat dan akurat!

**Time estimate**: Whole process should take **1-2 minutes max** for typical SNBT passage!

### **Materi 2.1.5: Analisis Jebakan Opsi Jawaban dalam Soal Organization**

Nah, sekarang kita masuk ke part yang super **practical**—gimana cara **avoid traps** dalam opsi jawaban soal organization! Pembuat soal SNBT suka banget bikin **distractor** (opsi pengecoh) yang keliatan bener tapi sebenarnya salah. Kita bakal bongkar semua trik mereka!

---

#### **Jebakan 1: Terlalu Spesifik (Too Narrow/Focused on One Detail)**

**Karakteristik**: Opsi jawaban **hanya menggambarkan satu paragraf** atau **satu detail kecil**, padahal soal tanya tentang **organization keseluruhan teks**.

**Contoh Jebakan**:

**Teks**: 5 paragraf tentang climate change (causes, effects on ecosystems, effects on human societies, mitigation strategies, conclusion)

**Soal**: "How is the information in the passage organized?"

**Opsi Jebakan**: "The passage discusses how rising sea levels affect coastal communities."

**Kenapa Salah**: Ini cuma **one detail dari one paragraph** (effects on human societies). Bukan menggambarkan organization keseluruhan!

**Opsi Benar**: "The passage presents causes and effects of climate change, followed by potential solutions."

**How to Avoid**:
- **Verifikasi** apakah opsi mencakup **seluruh teks** atau cuma sebagian
- **Cross-check** dengan Main Idea tiap paragraf—apakah opsi cover semuanya?
- **Ask yourself**: Does this describe the BIG PICTURE organization?

---

#### **Jebakan 2: Terlalu Umum (Too Broad/Generic)**

**Karakteristik**: Opsi jawaban **terlalu abstrak atau general**, bisa berlaku untuk **banyak teks berbeda**, nggak spesifik untuk teks yang dibahas.

**Contoh Jebakan**:

**Teks**: Specific analysis comparing online vs traditional education dengan focus advantages & disadvantages each

**Soal**: "Which of the following best describes the organization of the passage?"

**Opsi Jebakan**: "The passage discusses educational methods."

**Kenapa Salah**: Too vague! **Nggak describe pattern organization** yang specific (comparison, contrast, etc.). Statement ini bisa apply ke thousands of different texts!

**Opsi Benar**: "The passage compares and contrasts online education with traditional education, highlighting advantages and disadvantages of each."

**How to Avoid**:
- **Check specificity**: Apakah opsi **specifically describe** gimana info diorganisir?
- **Look for pattern words**: compare, contrast, chronological, cause-effect, problem-solution
- **Reject vague statements** yang bisa apply to too many texts

---

#### **Jebakan 3: Menggunakan Kata dari Teks tapi Salah Konteks**

**Karakteristik**: Opsi menggunakan **exact words atau phrases dari teks**, sehingga keliatan familiar dan "benar", tapi **maknanya di-twist** atau **konteksnya salah**.

**Contoh Jebakan**:

**Teks**: Discusses problem of plastic pollution, then analyzes its causes (consumer behavior, industry practices), then effects (marine life, human health), then solutions (regulations, alternatives)

**Soal**: "How does the author organize the ideas?"

**Opsi Jebakan**: "The author explains consumer behavior and industry practices related to plastic."

**Kenapa Salah**: "Consumer behavior" dan "industry practices" **memang ada di teks**, but ini cuma **causes section**, bukan overall organization! Plus, phrasing bikin keliatan seolah fokus utama teks cuma dua hal itu, padahal ada problem-causes-effects-solutions structure yang complete.

**Opsi Benar**: "The author identifies the problem of plastic pollution, analyzes its causes and effects, and proposes potential solutions."

**How to Avoid**:
- **Don't be fooled by familiar words!** Just because words are in the text doesn't mean the statement is correct
- **Check context**: Are those words being used in the right way to describe organization?
- **Verify completeness**: Does the option cover the whole structure?

---

#### **Jebakan 4: Salah Urutan (Incorrect Sequence)**

**Karakteristik**: Opsi describe **elements yang benar** tapi **urutannya salah** atau **sequence-nya tidak accurately reflect** actual text flow.

**Contoh Jebakan**:

**Actual Text Structure**:
1. Para 1: Define renewable energy
2. Para 2: Benefits of renewable energy
3. Para 3: Challenges in implementation
4. Para 4: Future outlook

**Soal**: "How is the passage organized?"

**Opsi Jebakan**: "The passage discusses challenges of renewable energy, then defines it and explains benefits."

**Kenapa Salah**: Elements are correct (challenges, definition, benefits) but **order is wrong**! Actual text starts with definition, then benefits, then challenges.

**Opsi Benar**: "The passage defines renewable energy, explains its benefits, discusses implementation challenges, and presents future outlook."

**How to Avoid**:
- **Check sequence carefully**: Mental recall urutan paragraph Main Ideas
- **Use your mapping**: Refer to your mental map of para 1, 2, 3, etc.
- **Verify chronology**: Especially important for time-sequence texts

---

#### **Jebakan 5: Missing Elements (Incomplete Coverage)**

**Karakteristik**: Opsi **correct but incomplete**—menyebut beberapa elements tapi **skip major parts** of the text.

**Contoh Jebakan**:

**Actual Text Structure**: Problem of obesity → Causes (diet, lifestyle) → Effects (health, economic) → Solutions (education, policy)

**Opsi Jebakan**: "The passage discusses causes and effects of obesity."

**Kenapa Salah**: **True but incomplete!** Text also discusses problem identification di awal dan solutions di akhir. Missing 40% of the content!

**Opsi Benar**: "The passage identifies obesity as a problem, analyzes its causes and effects, and proposes solutions."

**How to Avoid**:
- **Count major sections** in your mapping—does option cover all of them?
- **Watch for omissions**: What's left out?
- **Prefer comprehensive options** over partial ones (unless specifically asked about specific section)

---

#### **Jebakan 6: Confusion Between Pattern Types**

**Karakteristik**: **Mix up different organization patterns**—misalnya bilang "cause-effect" padahal sebenarnya "problem-solution", atau "chronological" padahal sebenarnya "order of importance".

**Contoh Jebakan**:

**Actual Text**: Discusses different types of renewable energy (solar, wind, hydro, geothermal) with pros/cons each → **Classification/Category pattern**

**Opsi Jebakan**: "The passage presents a chronological development of renewable energy technologies."

**Kenapa Salah**: **No time sequence!** Text categorizes by types, not by when they were developed.

**Opsi Benar**: "The passage classifies different types of renewable energy and evaluates advantages and disadvantages of each."

**How to Avoid**:
- **Identify specific pattern markers**: 
  - Chronological → dates, "first, then, next"
  - Cause-effect → "because, therefore, as a result"
  - Problem-solution → "problem, issue" then "solution, address"
  - Compare-contrast → "however, whereas, similarly"
  - Classification → categories, types, "one type...another type"
- **Match pattern dengan actual text structure**

---

#### **Jebakan 7: Confusion About Paragraph Function**

**Karakteristik**: Misinterpret **function of specific paragraph** dalam keseluruhan organization.

**Contoh Jebakan**:

**Actual Structure**:
- Para 1-3: Discuss benefits of meditation (main content)
- Para 4: Give specific example of successful meditation program (example/illustration of previous paragraphs)

**Opsi Jebakan**: "The passage primarily focuses on a specific meditation program case study."

**Kenapa Salah**: Case study is **supporting example**, not primary focus! Main content adalah benefits of meditation in Para 1-3.

**Opsi Benar**: "The passage discusses benefits of meditation and illustrates these with a case study example."

**How to Avoid**:
- **Distinguish main content dari supporting elements** (examples, case studies, anecdotes)
- **Check proportions**: How many paragraphs on main discussion vs. how many on examples?
- **Identify hierarchy**: Main ideas > supporting examples

---

#### **Jebakan 8: Overemphasis on Introduction/Conclusion**

**Karakteristik**: Opsi **overemphasize** intro or conclusion paragraph yang sebenarnya cuma **framing**, bukan main content.

**Contoh Jebakan**:

**Actual Structure**:
- Para 1: Brief intro stating social media is influential (1 paragraph)
- Para 2-5: Detailed analysis of how social media affects politics, business, culture, personal relationships (4 paragraphs)
- Para 6: Brief conclusion (1 paragraph)

**Opsi Jebakan**: "The passage argues that social media is influential and concludes by summarizing main points."

**Kenapa Salah**: This describes intro + conclusion but **completely ignores** the main content body (4 paragraphs of detailed analysis)!

**Opsi Benar**: "The passage introduces social media's influence, then analyzes its effects on politics, business, culture, and relationships."

**How to Avoid**:
- **Focus on body paragraphs** where main content lives
- **Intro/conclusion are framing**, not main organization
- **Count paragraphs**: Where's the bulk of content?

---

### **Strategi Ultimate untuk Menghadapi Soal Organization**

**Sebelum Lihat Opsi**:
1. **Quick mapping** (1-2 minutes): Main idea each paragraph
2. **Identify pattern**: Chronological? Compare-contrast? Problem-solution? Cause-effect?
3. **Note major sections**: Intro, body (with subsections), conclusion
4. **Check transitions**: What words connect paragraphs?

**Saat Evaluasi Opsi**:
1. **Eliminate obviously wrong** (too narrow, too broad, uses wrong pattern)
2. **Verify sequence** if option lists order
3. **Check completeness**: Does it cover all major sections?
4. **Match pattern terminology**: Does it use right pattern words?
5. **Verify dengan text**: Quick cross-check dengan paragraph Main Ideas

**Red Flags to Avoid**:
- ❌ Opsi yang cuma mention 1-2 paragraphs
- ❌ Opsi yang too vague ("discusses topic X")
- ❌ Opsi dengan sequence salah
- ❌ Opsi yang skip major sections
- ❌ Opsi yang confuse pattern types
- ❌ Opsi yang emphasize minor elements (examples, anecdotes) as if they're main content

**Green Lights to Choose**:
- ✅ Opsi yang cover ALL major sections
- ✅ Opsi dengan correct sequence
- ✅ Opsi yang use appropriate pattern terminology
- ✅ Opsi yang balanced (not overemphasizing intro/conclusion)
- ✅ Opsi yang specific enough tapi comprehensive
