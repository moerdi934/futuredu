### **Materi 3.1.5: Analisis Academic Vocabulary dan Register**

#### **Welcome to the World of Academic Vocabulary!**

Pernah nggak sih kamu baca teks bahasa Inggris casual (kayak chat, sosmed, blog personal) dan rasanya gampang banget? Tapi begitu baca journal article atau teks academic, langsung berasa kayak baca bahasa alien? 

Nah, itu karena **register**-nya beda!

**Register** itu level formalitas bahasa yang dipakai. Dalam SNBT, teks-teksnya mostly academic/formal register, jadi kamu harus familiar dengan **academic vocabulary**—kata-kata formal yang sering muncul di teks ilmiah, penelitian, dan bacaan akademis.

---

#### **Apa Itu Academic Vocabulary?**

Academic vocabulary adalah kata-kata yang:
1. **Formal** dan sophisticated
2. **Sering muncul** di teks akademik (journal, textbook, research paper)
3. **Lintas disiplin** (nggak spesifik satu subject, bisa dipake di berbagai field)
4. **Presisi tinggi** (makna lebih spesifik dibanding kata casual)

Contoh simpel:

| Casual/Informal | Academic/Formal |
|----------------|-----------------|
| get | obtain, acquire, receive |
| show | demonstrate, illustrate, indicate |
| use | utilize, employ, apply |
| think about | consider, contemplate, examine |
| help | assist, facilitate, aid |
| start | commence, initiate, begin |
| end | conclude, terminate, finalize |
| buy | purchase, acquire, procure |
| make | create, produce, construct, fabricate |

#### **Kenapa Academic Vocabulary Penting untuk SNBT?**

**Alasan #1: Teks SNBT itu Academic!**

Semua bacaan di SNBT—baik teks saintek, soshum, maupun umum—ditulis dengan academic register. Kalau kamu cuma familiar dengan casual vocabulary, bakal struggle parah.

**Alasan #2: Synonym Options Mostly Academic**

Lihat contoh soal:

> *"The study **demonstrates** a clear correlation."*
> 
> The word "demonstrates" is closest in meaning to:
> A. shows ✅ (casual tapi accepted)
> B. proves ✅ (semi-formal)
> C. illustrates ✅ (academic)
> D. tells ❌ (too casual untuk konteks ini)

Opsi yang benar biasanya yang maintain academic register!

**Alasan #3: Comprehension Speed**

Kalau kamu familiar dengan academic vocab, reading jadi lebih cepat. Nggak perlu ngos-ngosan translate tiap kata.

#### **Register Levels: From Casual to Ultra-Formal**

Mari kita lihat satu concept expressed in different registers:

**Concept:** Menunjukkan informasi/bukti

| Register Level | Vocabulary Choice |
|---------------|-------------------|
| **Casual/Slang** | "This stuff shows that..." |
| **Informal** | "This shows that..." |
| **Neutral** | "This indicates that..." |
| **Formal** | "This demonstrates that..." |
| **Very Formal/Academic** | "This illustrates/reveals that..." |
| **Ultra Formal/Archaic** | "This evinces that..." |

Dalam SNBT, expect level **Neutral** sampai **Formal/Academic**.

#### **Denotation vs Connotation: Subtle but Important**

**Denotation** = literal dictionary meaning
**Connotation** = emotional/cultural associations with a word

Contoh:

**"Cheap" vs "Inexpensive"**
- Denotation: kedua-duanya = low price
- Connotation:
  - **Cheap** → negative (poor quality, murahan)
  - **Inexpensive** → neutral/positive (affordable, terjangkau)

**"Skinny" vs "Slender" vs "Thin"**
- Denotation: semua = not fat
- Connotation:
  - **Skinny** → negative (terlalu kurus, unhealthy)
  - **Thin** → neutral (kurus secara objektif)
  - **Slender** → positive (langsing dengan kesan graceful)

**Dalam SNBT:** Academic texts prefer words dengan **neutral atau positive connotation**. Jadi kalau ada pilihan antara "cheap" dan "inexpensive" untuk formal context, choose "inexpensive"!

#### **Precision: Academic Words Are More Specific**

Academic vocabulary itu nggak cuma formal, tapi juga lebih **precise** (tepat/spesifik).

**Example 1:**

Casual: *"The weather affected the crops."*
Academic: *"The drought **adversely affected** crop yields."*

"Adversely affected" lebih specific daripada just "affected"—jelas menunjukkan negative impact.

**Example 2:**

Casual: *"Many people think this way."*
Academic: *"A **substantial proportion** of respondents **subscribe to** this viewpoint."*

"Substantial proportion" lebih precise dari "many people", dan "subscribe to this viewpoint" lebih formal dari "think this way".

#### **Latin & Greek Roots: The Secret Weapon**

Banyak academic vocabulary berasal dari Latin dan Greek. Kalau kamu recognize roots-nya, kamu bisa infer meaning!

**Common Latin Roots:**

| Root | Meaning | Examples |
|------|---------|----------|
| **port** | carry | transport, import, export, portable |
| **dict** | say | predict, dictate, contradict, indicate |
| **scrib/script** | write | describe, prescribe, manuscript, transcribe |
| **spect** | see/look | inspect, respect, spectator, perspective |
| **mit/miss** | send | transmit, submit, admission, dismiss |
| **fact/fect** | make/do | manufacture, effect, defect, perfect |
| **duc/duct** | lead | produce, reduce, conduct, educate |

**Common Greek Roots:**

| Root | Meaning | Examples |
|------|---------|----------|
| **bio** | life | biology, biography, biodegradable |
| **geo** | earth | geography, geology, geometry |
| **graph** | write | graphic, photograph, paragraph |
| **phone** | sound | telephone, microphone, phonetics |
| **psych** | mind | psychology, psychiatry, psychic |
| **tech** | skill/art | technology, technical, technique |
| **log** | word/study | logic, dialogue, biology (study of life) |

**Pro Strategy:** Kalau ketemu unfamiliar academic word, try to break it down by roots!

Example: **"Biodegradable"**
- **bio** (life) + **de** (down) + **grad** (step) + **able** (can be)
- = can be broken down by living organisms

#### **Collocation: Words That Go Together**

Academic words punya **preferred partners**—kata-kata yang naturally sering dipasangkan.

**Strong Collocations:**

**With "conduct":**
- conduct **research** ✅
- conduct **a study** ✅
- conduct **an experiment** ✅
- conduct **an investigation** ✅
- ❌ (jarang:) conduct a test, conduct work

**With "significant":**
- **significant** impact/effect/difference/increase ✅
- **significant** role/contribution/progress ✅

**With "demonstrate":**
- demonstrate **ability/skill** ✅
- demonstrate **evidence/proof** ✅
- demonstrate **effectiveness** ✅

**Kenapa penting?** Dalam soal synonym, opsi yang punya collocation match dengan kata lain dalam kalimat lebih likely correct!

#### **Strategy untuk Master Academic Vocabulary**

**Strategy #1: Read, Read,Read Academic Texts**

The more you expose yourself to academic writing, the more familiar the vocabulary becomes.

**Recommended sources:**
- Scientific American (simplified science articles)
- The Economist (economy, social issues)
- National Geographic (science, environment)
- TED Talk transcripts (various academic topics)

**Strategy #2: Create Personal Academic Word Bank**

Bikin daftar kata-kata academic yang kamu encounter:
- Kata + definisi
- Synonym formal dan informal
- Example sentence
- Collocations

**Strategy #3: Practice Word Transformation**

Latihan ubah informal → formal:

Informal: *"A lot of people get sick from bad food."*
Formal: *"Numerous individuals contract illnesses from contaminated food."*

**Strategy #4: Use Academic Vocabulary in Writing**

Force yourself to use academic words when writing. Practice makes permanent!

#### **Common Pitfalls dengan Academic Vocabulary**

**Pitfall #1: Overusing Fancy Words (Thesaurus Syndrome)**

Jangan asal ganti semua kata dengan yang fancy! Harus natural dan appropriate.

❌ TOO MUCH: *"The individual commenced to ambulate toward the domicile."*
✅ BETTER: *"The person started to walk toward the house."*

**Pitfall #2: Mixing Registers**

Don't mix casual dan formal words awkwardly:

❌ MIXED: *"The research kinda demonstrates that..."* (kinda = casual!)
✅ CONSISTENT: *"The research demonstrates that..."*

**Pitfall #3: False Friends**

Beberapa academic words mirip dengan kata lain tapi beda arti:

- **Affect** (verb: mempengaruhi) ≠ **Effect** (noun: hasil)
- **Complement** (melengkapi) ≠ **Compliment** (pujian)
- **Principle** (prinsip) ≠ **Principal** (kepala sekolah/utama)

---

#### **Quick Recognition Checklist**

Untuk identify apakah suatu text academic or not:

✅ Uses formal vocabulary (obtain vs get, demonstrate vs show)
✅ Avoids contractions (do not vs don't, cannot vs can't)
✅ Uses passive voice frequently (it is believed that...)
✅ Uses hedging language (may, might, possibly, potentially)
✅ Cites sources and evidence (according to, research shows)
✅ Uses technical/discipline-specific terms
✅ Longer, complex sentences dengan multiple clauses
