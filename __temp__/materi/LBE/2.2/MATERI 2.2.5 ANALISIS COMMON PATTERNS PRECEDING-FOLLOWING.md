## MATERI 2.2.5: ANALISIS COMMON PATTERNS PRECEDING-FOLLOWING

Setelah paham clues dan transition words, sekarang kita lihat **POLA-POLA UMUM** yang sering muncul dalam hubungan preceding-following dengan current text!

Mengenali patterns ini akan bikin kamu lebih cepat dan akurat dalam menjawab!

### Pattern 1: Background/Context → Definition/Main Topic

**The Flow:**
PRECEDING: Background information, context, atau introduction to a topic
CURRENT: Definition atau detailed introduction of the main topic
FOLLOWING: Elaboration, applications, atau examples

**Karakteristik:**
- Current text often starts dengan defining key terms atau introducing main concept
- Ada shift dari general background ke specific topic

**Contoh Structure:**
PRECEDING: Historical development of psychology
CURRENT: Definition of cognitive psychology (starts here)
FOLLOWING: Key principles of cognitive psychology

**How to Recognize:**
- Current text dimulai dengan definition atau introduction of a specific concept
- Ada reference ke broader field atau earlier developments
- Tone shifts dari contextual ke explanatory

**Typical Opening Sentences (Current Text):**
> "Cognitive psychology is defined as..."
> "This field emerged from..."
> "The concept of X refers to..."

**Preceding Would Discuss:**
- Historical background
- Related fields
- Why this concept is important
- General context

**Following Would Discuss:**
- Detailed explanation
- Applications
- Examples
- Further subdivisions

---

### Pattern 2: General Concept → Specific Example/Case Study

**The Flow:**
PRECEDING: General principles, theory, atau overview
CURRENT: Specific example, case study, atau application (starts here)
FOLLOWING: Analysis of the example, lessons learned, atau additional examples

**Karakteristik:**
- Current text suddenly focuses on a specific case tanpa introducing the general concept
- Ada demonstrative pronouns ("This example," "Such cases")

**Contoh Structure:**
PRECEDING: General principles of economics
CURRENT: "The 2008 financial crisis illustrates..." (starts here)
FOLLOWING: Lessons learned from the crisis

**How to Recognize:**
- Specific names, dates, places mentioned immediately
- Language shifts dari theoretical ke concrete
- Phrases like "for example," "for instance," "a case in point"

**Typical Opening Sentences:**
> "The Amazon rainforest provides a clear example..."
> "Consider the case of Nokia..."
> "This phenomenon can be observed in..."

**Preceding Would Discuss:**
- The general concept being exemplified
- Theoretical framework
- Why examples are important

**Following Would Discuss:**
- Detailed analysis of the example
- What we can learn
- How it relates back to theory
- Other similar examples

---

### Pattern 3: Problem Statement → Detailed Exploration → Solutions

**The Flow:**
PRECEDING: Introduction to a problem atau issue
CURRENT: Detailed exploration of the problem (starts here)
FOLLOWING: Proposed solutions atau approaches

**Karakteristik:**
- Current text discusses complexity, aspects, atau impacts of a problem
- Problem assumed to be known (mentioned in preceding)

**Contoh Structure:**
PRECEDING: Climate change is a pressing issue
CURRENT: "The effects of climate change on biodiversity are profound..." (starts here)
FOLLOWING: Strategies to mitigate these effects

**How to Recognize:**
- Problem-focused language tanpa initial problem statement
- Reference to "these challenges," "this issue," "such problems"
- Analytical tone

**Typical Opening Sentences:**
> "This problem has multiple dimensions..."
> "The complexity of this issue becomes apparent..."
> "These challenges affect various sectors..."

**Preceding Would Discuss:**
- What the problem is
- Why it's important
- Who is affected

**Following Would Discuss:**
- Solutions
- Recommendations
- Future directions
- How to address the issues discussed

---

### Pattern 4: Current Situation → Future Implications/Predictions

**The Flow:**
PRECEDING: Past developments atau how we got here
CURRENT: Current situation atau present state (starts here)
FOLLOWING: Future implications, predictions, atau trends

**Karakteristik:**
- Current text describes present state in detail
- Often uses present tense
- May end dengan hints about future

**Contoh Structure:**
PRECEDING: Historical development of AI
CURRENT: "Today, AI systems can perform..." (starts here)
FOLLOWING: Future capabilities and ethical concerns

**How to Recognize:**
- Present tense dominance
- Current data, statistics
- "Today," "currently," "at present," "nowadays"
- Ending hints at implications atau "what's next"

**Typical Opening Sentences:**
> "Currently, the technology allows..."
> "Modern approaches to this problem involve..."
> "Today's consumers expect..."

**Preceding Would Discuss:**
- How we arrived at current situation
- Historical development
- Past approaches vs current

**Following Would Discuss:**
- Future projections
- Implications
- Challenges ahead
- Recommendations for moving forward

---

### Pattern 5: Theory/Principle → Application/Implementation

**The Flow:**
PRECEDING: Abstract theory atau principles
CURRENT: How theory is applied (starts here)
FOLLOWING: Results, effectiveness, atau further applications

**Karakteristik:**
- Shift dari theoretical ke practical
- Reference to "applying," "implementing," "using"

**Contoh Structure:**
PRECEDING: Principles of adult learning theory
CURRENT: "When these principles are applied in corporate training..." (starts here)
FOLLOWING: Outcomes and best practices

**How to Recognize:**
- Practical, implementation-focused language
- Real-world contexts mentioned
- Phrases like "in practice," "when applied," "implementation of"

**Typical Opening Sentences:**
> "In practice, these theories translate to..."
> "The application of this principle in..."
> "When implemented in real-world settings..."

**Preceding Would Discuss:**
- The theory itself
- Principles
- Theoretical background

**Following Would Discuss:**
- Effectiveness
- Challenges in application
- Case studies
- Refinements needed

---

### Pattern 6: Introduction of Multiple Aspects → Detailed Discussion of One Aspect

**The Flow:**
PRECEDING: Overview mentioning multiple aspects
CURRENT: Detailed discussion of ONE specific aspect (starts here)
FOLLOWING: Either discussion of another aspect OR implications of the discussed aspect

**Karakteristik:**
- Focus on subset of broader topic
- Reference to "one aspect," "first," "among these"

**Contoh Structure:**
PRECEDING: Social media affects society in multiple ways
CURRENT: "The impact on mental health, particularly among adolescents..." (starts here)
FOLLOWING: Other impacts (on privacy, democracy, etc.) OR implications of mental health impact

**How to Recognize:**
- Selective focus indicated
- Phrases like "particularly," "specifically," "one key area"
- Deep dive into one facet

**Typical Opening Sentences:**
> "Among these factors, X is particularly significant..."
> "One crucial aspect is..."
> "The first consideration involves..."

**Preceding Would Discuss:**
- Overview of all aspects
- Introduction to the broader topic
- Why these aspects matter

**Following Would Discuss:**
- Other aspects not yet covered
- OR synthesis of the discussed aspect
- Implications
- Comparisons between aspects

---

### Pattern 7: Chronological Sequence

**The Flow:**
PRECEDING: Earlier time period atau phase
CURRENT: Subsequent time period atau phase (starts here)
FOLLOWING: Next time period atau current/future state

**Karakteristik:**
- Clear temporal progression
- Time markers throughout
- Historical atau developmental focus

**Contoh Structure:**
PRECEDING: Early stages of the Industrial Revolution
CURRENT: "By the mid-19th century..." (starts here)
FOLLOWING: Late 19th century developments

**How to Recognize:**
- Dates, time periods, era references
- Chronological transition words
- Past tense (for historical) atau progressive development

**Typical Opening Sentences:**
> "During the second phase..."
> "By the 1990s..."
> "As the technology matured..."

**Preceding Would Discuss:**
- Earlier time period
- Initial stages
- Origins

**Following Would Discuss:**
- Subsequent developments
- Later stages
- Current state atau future

---

### Pattern 8: Cause → Effect → Further Consequences/Solutions

**The Flow:**
PRECEDING: Causes atau triggering factors
CURRENT: Direct effects atau consequences (starts here)
FOLLOWING: Secondary effects, chain reactions, OR solutions to address effects

**Karakteristik:**
- Causal chain clearly structured
- Effect-focused language in current text

**Contoh Structure:**
PRECEDING: Causes of deforestation
CURRENT: "As a result, biodiversity loss has accelerated..." (starts here)
FOLLOWING: Impact on climate OR conservation strategies

**How to Recognize:**
- Result/effect language
- "As a result," "consequently," "therefore"
- Description of outcomes

**Typical Opening Sentences:**
> "These factors have led to..."
> "The consequences include..."
> "As a result of these changes..."

**Preceding Would Discuss:**
- What caused this
- Contributing factors
- Context

**Following Would Discuss:**
- Further implications
- Chain effects
- Solutions atau responses
- Long-term consequences

---

### Pattern 9: Comparison → Contrast → Conclusion/Preference

**The Flow:**
PRECEDING: Introduction of things being compared
CURRENT: Similarities (comparison) atau differences (contrast) (starts here)
FOLLOWING: Other side (if current is comparison, following is contrast, or vice versa) OR conclusions drawn

**Karakteristik:**
- Comparative/contrastive structure
- Balanced presentation atau evaluative

**Contoh Structure:**
PRECEDING: Introduction of Traditional vs Digital Marketing
CURRENT: "While traditional marketing relies on..." (starts here - showing contrast)
FOLLOWING: Advantages of digital OR synthesis of both approaches

**How to Recognize:**
- Comparison/contrast language
- "While," "whereas," "similarly," "in contrast"
- Side-by-side analysis

**Typical Opening Sentences:**
> "In contrast to X, Y demonstrates..."
> "While both approaches share..."
> "Unlike traditional methods..."

**Preceding Would Discuss:**
- Introduction of both items
- Why comparison is relevant
- Context

**Following Would Discuss:**
- Other side of comparison
- Synthesis
- Which is better atau when to use each
- Implications

---

### Pattern 10: Evidence Presentation → Interpretation/Analysis

**The Flow:**
PRECEDING: Research question atau hypothesis
CURRENT: Data, findings, evidence (starts here)
FOLLOWING: Interpretation, analysis, implications

**Karakteristik:**
- Objective, data-focused current text
- Minimal interpretation in current text
- Sets up for analysis

**Contoh Structure:**
PRECEDING: Study methodology and hypothesis
CURRENT: "The results showed that..." (starts here)
FOLLOWING: "These findings suggest that..."

**How to Recognize:**
- Presentation of results, data, statistics
- Neutral, objective tone
- "The data show," "findings indicate," "results reveal"

**Typical Opening Sentences:**
> "The survey results indicated..."
> "Data analysis revealed..."
> "Observations showed that..."

**Preceding Would Discuss:**
- Research question
- Methodology
- What was being investigated

**Following Would Discuss:**
- What results mean
- Interpretation
- Implications for theory atau practice
- Recommendations

### Pattern Recognition Strategy

Untuk cepat recognize pattern:

**STEP 1: Identify the NATURE of current text**
- Is it theoretical atau practical?
- Is it general atau specific?
- Is it cause atau effect?
- Is it past, present, atau future-focused?

**STEP 2: Determine POSITION in logical sequence**
- Beginning, middle, atau end of discussion?
- Introduction, development, atau conclusion?

**STEP 3: Look for MISSING pieces**
- What's needed BEFORE to make current text make sense?
- What's needed AFTER to complete the discussion?

**STEP 4: Match to PATTERN**
- Which pattern fits the flow?
- What would logically come before/after based on the pattern?

### Common Traps in Pattern-Based Questions

⚠️ **TRAP 1: Assuming Linear Progression**
Tidak semua text follow strict linear pattern. Kadang ada recursive loops atau parallel discussions.

⚠️ **TRAP 2: Confusing Cause with Effect**
Hati-hati: yang terlihat seperti cause bisa jadi effect dari sesuatu yang di preceding, dan yang terlihat seperti effect bisa menjadi cause untuk following.

⚠️ **TRAP 3: Over-predicting**
Preceding/following harus reasonable next step, bukan melompat 5 langkah ke depan.

⚠️ **TRAP 4: Missing the Scope**
Preceding/following harus match scope dari current text. Kalau current text very specific, preceding biasanya slightly more general, bukan completely different broad topic.
