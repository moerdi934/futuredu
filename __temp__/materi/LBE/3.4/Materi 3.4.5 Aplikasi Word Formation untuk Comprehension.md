#### **🔍 PART 1: THE BREAKDOWN SYSTEM**

Ini adalah sistem step-by-step untuk attack SETIAP unfamiliar word yang kamu temui di SNBT!

**THE 5-STEP BREAKDOWN METHOD:**

**STEP 1: IDENTIFY Word Parts**
Pisahkan kata kompleks menjadi components:
- Prefix (jika ada)
- Root word
- Suffix (jika ada)

**STEP 2: ANALYZE Each Component**
Tentukan meaning dari tiap part:
- Prefix meaning
- Root meaning
- Suffix meaning (dan word class yang dihasilkan)

**STEP 3: COMBINE Meanings**
Gabungkan logical dari semua parts

**STEP 4: VERIFY with Context**
Check apakah meaning yang kamu infer cocok dengan kalimat/paragraf

**STEP 5: CROSS-CHECK Grammar**
Pastikan word class sesuai dengan posisi dalam kalimat

---

**🎬 CONTOH APLIKASI LENGKAP:**

**Scenario:** Kamu menemukan kalimat ini di bacaan SNBT:

*"The **interconnectedness** of global economies makes **unilateral** action increasingly **ineffective**."*

Kamu tidak familiar dengan ketiga kata yang di-bold. Let's break them down!

---

**KATA 1: INTERCONNECTEDNESS**

**STEP 1: Identify Parts**
- inter- + connect + -ed + -ness

**STEP 2: Analyze Components**
- **inter-** = between, among (prefix)
- **connect** = join, link (root)
- **-ed** = past participle (suffix)
- **-ness** = state/quality (noun suffix)

**STEP 3: Combine**
- inter (between) + connect (link) + -ed (form) + -ness (state)
- = "the state of being linked between/among things"
- = **keterhubungan/keterkaitan**

**STEP 4: Context Verification**
- "...of global economies" → economies are linked together
- "makes unilateral action..." → because things connected, one action affects others
- ✅ Makes perfect sense!

**STEP 5: Grammar Check**
- Position: subject of sentence (butuh noun)
- -ness suffix → creates NOUN ✅
- Grammar correct!

**Final Understanding:** Keterhubungan ekonomi global membuat tindakan sepihak semakin tidak efektif.

---

**KATA 2: UNILATERAL**

**STEP 1: Identify Parts**
- uni- + later + -al

**STEP 2: Analyze**
- **uni-** = one (prefix)
- **later** = side (Latin root: *latus*)
- **-al** = relating to (adjective suffix)

**STEP 3: Combine**
- uni (one) + lateral (side) + -al (relating to)
- = "relating to one side"
- = **sepihak**

**STEP 4: Context**
- "unilateral action" → action by one side/party alone
- Contrast dengan interconnectedness → makes sense, can't act alone when everything connected!
- ✅ Perfect fit!

**STEP 5: Grammar**
- Position: modifier before noun "action" (butuh adjective)
- -al suffix → creates ADJECTIVE ✅

---

**KATA 3: INEFFECTIVE**

**STEP 1: Parts**
- in- + effect + -ive

**STEP 2: Analyze**
- **in-** = not (negative prefix)
- **effect** = result, outcome (root)
- **-ive** = having quality of (adjective suffix)

**STEP 3: Combine**
- in (not) + effect (result) + -ive (having)
- = "not having the desired result"
- = **tidak efektif**

**STEP 4: Context**
- Why ineffective? Because interconnected! One country can't solve alone
- ✅ Logical connection!

**STEP 5: Grammar**
- Position: predicate adjective after "makes"
- -ive suffix → ADJECTIVE ✅

**FULL SENTENCE UNDERSTANDING:**
"Keterhubungan ekonomi global membuat tindakan sepihak semakin tidak efektif."

Boom! 💥 Dari completely unfamiliar, sekarang fully understood—tanpa kamus!

#### **📊 PART 2: PATTERN RECOGNITION dalam Academic Vocabulary**

Academic texts (yang SERING muncul di SNBT) punya patterns tertentu dalam word formation. Recognize patterns ini = speed boost!

---

**PATTERN #1: The "-tion/-sion" Noun Pattern**

Hampir SEMUA verb bisa jadi noun dengan -tion/-sion:

| Verb | Noun Form | Meaning |
|------|-----------|---------|
| educate | education | pendidikan |
| communicate | communication | komunikasi |
| investigate | investigation | investigasi |
| organize | organization | organisasi |
| conclude | conclusion | kesimpulan |

🎯 **SNBT Hack:** 
Kalau nemu kata ending -tion/-sion, itu 99% NOUN yang berasal dari VERB. Helpful untuk:
- Grammar questions
- Paraphrase recognition (verb form ↔ noun form)

**Example in SNBT:**
- Text: "The researchers **investigated** the phenomenon."
- Question option: "The **investigation** revealed..."
- Same info, different form! ✅

---

**PATTERN #2: The "-able/-ible" Adjective Pattern**

Verb + able/ible = "can be [verb]ed"

| Verb | Adjective | Meaning |
|------|-----------|---------|
| read | readable | dapat dibaca |
| predict | predictable | dapat diprediksi |
| access | accessible | dapat diakses |
| reverse | reversible | dapat dibalik |
| believe | believable | dapat dipercaya |

🎯 **Usage:**
Passive meaning! "readable" = can be read (oleh orang lain)

---

**PATTERN #3: The "-ful/-less" Opposite Pair**

| Base Noun | -ful (with) | -less (without) |
|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| care | careful | careless |
| hope | hopeful | hopeless |
| pain | painful | painless |
| harm | harmful | harmless |
| meaning | meaningful | meaningless |

🎯 **Antonym Detector:**
Kalau soal tanya "opposite of X", cek apakah ada -ful/-less pair!

---

**PATTERN #4: The "-ology" Family (Study Of...)**

Super common di teks saintek!

| Root + -ology | Meaning |
|---------------|---------|
| bio**logy** | study of life |
| geo**logy** | study of earth |
| psycho**logy** | study of mind |
| socio**logy** | study of society |
| archaeo**logy** | study of ancient artifacts |
| zoo**logy** | study of animals |

🎯 **Quick ID:**
Anything ending -ology = field of study!

---

**PATTERN #5: The "-ist" Person Pattern**

Root + -ist = person who practices/believes

| Root + -ist | Meaning |
|-------------|---------|
| art**ist** | person who creates art |
| scient**ist** | person who does science |
| psycholog**ist** | person who practices psychology |
| capital**ist** | person who believes in capitalism |
| journal**ist** | person who does journalism |

#### **🎓 PART 3: EXPANDING Vocabulary from One Root**

Ini adalah the MOST EFFICIENT vocabulary building technique! Dari ONE root word, generate DOZENS of related words!

---

**EXAMPLE: From Root "FORM" (shape, bentuk)**

1. **Basic Forms:**
   - form (bentuk)
   - formal (resmi, bentuk yang tepat)
   - inform (memberi bentuk/tahu)
   - reform (bentuk ulang)

2. **Add More Prefixes:**
   - **trans**form (mengubah bentuk)
   - **con**form (menyesuaikan bentuk)
   - **de**form (merusak bentuk)
   - **per**form (melakukan, membentuk aksi)

3. **Add Suffixes:**
   - form**ation** (pembentukan)
   - form**ative** (yang membentuk)
   - in**form**ation (informasi)
   - re**form**ation (reformasi)
   - trans**form**ation (transformasi)

4. **Combine Multiple Affixes:**
   - **uni**form (satu bentuk)
   - **in**form**ative** (memberikan informasi)
   - **con**form**ity** (kesesuaian)
   - **trans**form**ation**al (bersifat transformasi)

**Result:** Dari ONE root "form", kamu just learned 15+ words! 🚀

---

**ANOTHER EXAMPLE: Root "PRESS" (push, tekan)**

1. **de**press (menekan ke bawah) → depression
2. **ex**press (menekan keluar) → expression
3. **im**press (menekan masuk) → impression
4. **op**press (menekan berlawanan) → oppression
5. **sup**press (menekan di bawah) → suppression
6. **com**press (menekan bersama) → compression
7. **re**press (menekan kembali) → repression

Plus all their related forms with suffixes (-ion, -ive, -ed, -ing, etc.)!

#### **⚠️ PART 4: VERIFIKASI dengan Context—The Critical Step!**

Word formation gives you CLUES, but context gives CONFIRMATION. Never skip context verification!

---

**⚠️ WARNING CASE #1: Multiple Meanings**

Word: **"COMPANY"**
- Break down: com- (together) + pan- (bread) + -y
- Literal: "people who break bread together"
- But modern meanings:
  1. Business organization (perusahaan)
  2. Companionship (teman)
  3. Group of actors (rombongan)
  4. Military unit (kompi)

→ **Context determines which meaning!**

*"The company launched a new product."* → business
*"I enjoy your company."* → companionship

---

**⚠️ WARNING CASE #2: False Friends**

Word: **"TERRIBLE"**
- Looks like: terr- (earth) + -ible?
- WRONG! Actually from Latin *terribilis* = causing terror
- Meaning: very bad, awful (sangat buruk)

→ **Not all parts are what they seem! Context + common usage matters!**

---

**⚠️ WARNING CASE #3: Figurative Extensions**

Word: **"BROADCAST"**
- Literal breakdown: broad (luas) + cast (lempar)
- Original: menyebar benih secara luas (agriculture)
- Modern meaning: menyiarkan (radio/TV)

→ **Meanings evolve! Current usage > literal parts**

#### **💡 PART 5: PRAKTIS Tips untuk SNBT Day**

**TIP #1: Pre-Reading Scan**
Sebelum mulai baca intensively, scan text untuk unfamiliar words. Quick mental note kata mana yang might need breakdown.

**TIP #2: Prioritize by Question Relevance**
Kalau kata tidak related dengan soal, skip the deep analysis! Focus energy on words yang muncul di pertanyaan.

**TIP #3: Use Elimination First**
Dalam soal synonym/meaning:
1. Eliminate obviously wrong answers FIRST
2. THEN breakdown unfamiliar word
3. Match dengan remaining options

Ini saves time!

**TIP #4: Mark Familiar Parts**
Saat breakdown kata, underline/highlight mentally part yang kamu YAKIN:
- un-**predict**-able
- **Predict** kamu tahu = ramal
- Un- = not, -able = can be
- → not predictable = tidak bisa diprediksi

**TIP #5: Build Pre-Test Vocabulary**
Minggu-minggu sebelum SNBT, focus on high-frequency academic words dengan useful roots. Buat flashcard dari:
- Top 20 prefixes
- Top 30 roots (Latin + Greek)
- Top 15 suffixes

Ini covers 70-80% of academic vocabulary!

#### **🎯 PART 6: REAL SNBT-STYLE Application**

Mari simulate actual SNBT scenario!

---

**PASSAGE EXCERPT:**

*"The **unprecedented** global pandemic **necessitated** a **comprehensive** **reorganization** of healthcare **infrastructure**. Medical professionals had to rapidly **adapt** their **methodologies** to address the **unpredictable** nature of the virus, while governments **implemented** various **containment** strategies."*

**QUESTION:** What does "unprecedented" in the passage mean?

**(A) Expected**
**(B) Never happened before**
**(C) Frequent**
**(D) Controllable**
**(E) Predictable**

---

**YOUR BREAKDOWN:**

**Word: UNPRECEDENTED**

Parts: un- + pre- + cedent + -ed

- **un-** = not (negative prefix)
- **pre-** = before (time prefix)  
- **cedent** = going (dari Latin *cedere* = to go)
- **-ed** = past form

Combine: not + before + going/happened
→ "not happened before"
→ **never happened before**

Context check: "global pandemic" + "necessitated reorganization"
→ Something new and significant happened!

**Answer: (B) Never happened before** ✅

---

**Let's Try More Words from Same Passage:**

**1. NECESSITATED**
- **ne-** (variant of "necessary")
- **cess** = go, yield
- **-itate** (verb suffix)
- **-ed** (past)
→ "made necessary"

**2. COMPREHENSIVE**
- **com-** = together, completely
- **pre-** = before, forward
- **hens** = grasp (dari *hendere*)
- **-ive** = having quality
→ "grasping everything together" = complete, thorough

**3. REORGANIZATION**
- **re-** = again
- **organ** = arrange, structure
- **-ize** = make into
- **-ation** = noun of action
→ "action of arranging again"

**4. INFRASTRUCTURE**
- **infra-** = below, beneath
- **structure** = building, arrangement
→ "underlying structure/foundation"

**5. METHODOLOGIES**
- **method** = way, system
- **-ology** = study of, system of
- **-ies** = plural
→ "systems of methods"

**6. UNPREDICTABLE**
- **un-** = not
- **pre-** = before
- **dict** = say
- **-able** = can be
→ "cannot be said/known before" = unpredictable

**7. CONTAINMENT**
- **con-** = together
- **tain** = hold (dari Latin *tenere*)
- **-ment** = action/result
→ "action of holding together/controlling"

See how powerful this is? Dari satu paragraf dengan 8 "difficult" words, kamu bisa understand SEMUANYA dengan word formation knowledge! 🔥
