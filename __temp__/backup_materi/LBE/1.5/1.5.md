# SECTION 1: PEMAHAMAN IDE DAN TUJUAN TEKS

## TOPIK 1.5: AUTHOR'S ATTITUDE (OPINION DAN TONE)

---

## **Materi 1.5.1: Pengenalan Author's Attitude** *(Materi Dibuka)*

### **Apa Sih Author's Attitude Itu?**

Bayangkan kamu lagi ngobrol sama teman, terus dia cerita sesuatu. Dari cara dia cerita—nada suaranya, pilihan katanya, ekspresinya—kamu bisa tahu kan dia seneng, kesel, sinis, atau netral aja? Nah, dalam teks tertulis pun sama! **Author's Attitude** adalah sikap, perasaan, atau pandangan penulis terhadap topik yang dia bahas.

Bedanya, karena ini teks (bukan ngobrol langsung), kita harus jadi **detektif kata-kata**. Kita gali petunjuk dari:
- **Pilihan kata** (word choice/diction)
- **Struktur kalimat**
- **Nada tulisan** (tone)
- **Opini yang diselipkan**

Author's Attitude ini penting banget di SNBT karena sering muncul dalam berbagai bentuk pertanyaan. Dan yang seru, jawabannya nggak selalu eksplisit—kita harus **baca di antara baris** (read between the lines).

---

### **Dua Komponen Utama: Opinion vs Tone**

Author's Attitude punya dua aspek yang saling terkait tapi berbeda:

#### **1. OPINION (Pendapat/Pandangan)**
- **Sifat**: Subjektif, personal belief penulis
- **Ciri khas**: Ada keberpihakan (bias), penilaian personal
- **Contoh**: "Social media is ruining face-to-face communication." ← Ini opini karena ada judgement value

#### **2. TONE (Nada/Emosi)**
- **Sifat**: Emosi atau sikap yang terpancar dari tulisan
- **Ciri khas**: Bisa objektif atau subjektif, tergantung genre teks
- **Contoh**: Tone bisa *critical* (kritis), *optimistic* (optimis), *sarcastic* (sarkastik), *objective* (objektif)

**Analogi sederhana:**
- **Opinion** = *Apa yang penulis pikirkan/yakini* tentang topik
- **Tone** = *Bagaimana perasaan penulis* saat membahas topik itu

---

### **Kenapa Author's Attitude Penting?**

1. **Memahami perspektif penulis** → Apakah dia mendukung, menentang, atau netral terhadap topik?
2. **Menilai kredibilitas** → Teks yang terlalu bias mungkin kurang objektif
3. **Memprediksi purpose** → Attitude sering terkait dengan purpose (Topik 1.2)
4. **Membedakan fakta vs opini** → Skill krusial untuk critical reading

**Fun fact:** Di SNBT, soal tentang attitude sering jadi "pembunuh waktu" karena kamu harus membaca dengan hati-hati. Tapi tenang, dengan strategi yang tepat, kamu bisa jawab cepat dan akurat! 🎯

---

### **Variasi Pertanyaan dalam Soal SNBT**

Pertanyaan tentang Author's Attitude bisa muncul dalam berbagai bentuk:

**Tentang Opinion:**
1. "The author's opinion about X is..."
2. "The writer assumes that..."
3. "The author would most likely agree with..."
4. "Which of the following best reflects the author's view?"
5. "The author holds the assumption that..."

**Tentang Tone:**
1. "The tone of the passage is best described as..."
2. "Which of the following best describes the author's tone?"
3. "The attitude of the author toward X is..."
4. "The author's tone can be characterized as..."

**Tentang Opinion vs Fact:**
1. "Which of the following is an opinion from the text?"
2. "Which statement represents a fact?"
3. "The author's belief is expressed in..."

---

### **Hubungan dengan Topik Lain**

Author's Attitude nggak berdiri sendiri—dia connected dengan beberapa topik:

- **Purpose (Topik 1.2)**: Sikap penulis mempengaruhi tujuan penulisan
  - *Contoh*: Tone kritis → Purpose biasanya to criticize/to argue
  
- **Main Idea (Topik 1.1)**: Attitude bisa tersirat dari main idea
  - *Contoh*: Main idea tentang "bahaya media sosial" → likely penulis punya sikap negatif
  
- **Inference (Topik 1.4)**: Kita sering infer attitude dari clues tersirat

---

### **Tips Awal Sebelum Masuk Materi Mendalam**

✅ **Jangan terburu-buru judge!** Baca seluruh konteks dulu sebelum menentukan attitude
✅ **Cari kata kunci emosional** (loaded words) yang menunjukkan bias
✅ **Perhatikan struktur argumentasi**: apakah penulis satu sisi atau balanced?
✅ **Hindari proyeksi pribadi**: jangan asumsikan penulis setuju dengan kamu 😄

## **Materi 1.5.2: Analisis Author's Opinion**

### **Apa Bedanya Opini dengan Fakta?**

Sebelum kita bahas lebih dalam tentang opinion, mari kita bedakan dulu dengan **fakta**:

| **FAKTA** | **OPINI** |
|-----------|-----------|
| Dapat diverifikasi (dicek kebenarannya) | Tidak dapat diverifikasi objektif |
| Objektif (tidak tergantung sudut pandang) | Subjektif (tergantung belief pribadi) |
| Berbasis data/bukti konkret | Berbasis penilaian/interpretasi personal |
| *Contoh*: "Jakarta adalah ibu kota Indonesia." | *Contoh*: "Jakarta adalah kota terbaik di Indonesia." |

**Trik cepat membedakan:**
- Fakta → bisa dijawab dengan "Ya" atau "Tidak" secara pasti
- Opini → bisa dijawab dengan "Setuju" atau "Tidak setuju"

---

### **Karakteristik Opinion dalam Teks**

#### **1. Bahasa Subjektif**

Opini biasanya menggunakan kata-kata yang menunjukkan **personal belief** atau **judgment**:

**Frasa penanda opinion:**
- "I believe that..."
- "In my opinion..."
- "It seems that..."
- "I think..."
- "From my perspective..."
- "It appears that..."

**Contoh dalam teks:**
> *"I believe that remote learning, while convenient, cannot fully replace the experience of in-person education."*

↑ Ini jelas opini karena ada "I believe" dan judgment value tentang remote learning.

---

#### **2. Ekspresi Ketidakpastian (Hedging)**

Penulis sering menggunakan **modal verbs** atau **adverbs** untuk menunjukkan ketidakpastian, yang mengindikasikan opini:

**Modal verbs:**
- may, might, could, would, should

**Adverbs:**
- possibly, probably, likely, perhaps, arguably, presumably

**Contoh:**
> *"This policy **may** lead to better economic outcomes."*

↑ Kata "may" menunjukkan ini spekulasi/opini, bukan fakta pasti.

**Contoh lain:**
> *"It is **arguably** the most significant discovery of the decade."*

↑ "Arguably" = bisa diperdebatkan = opini, bukan fakta mutlak.

---

#### **3. Evaluative/Judgmental Words**

Kata-kata yang memberi **penilaian** (baik/buruk, benar/salah) adalah indikator kuat opini:

**Adjectives evaluatif:**
- good, bad, excellent, terrible, wonderful, awful
- important, insignificant, essential, unnecessary
- effective, ineffective, successful, failed
- beautiful, ugly, impressive, disappointing

**Verbs evaluatif:**
- should/ought to (menunjukkan rekomendasi)
- deserve, merit (layak/pantas)
- condemn, praise (mengecam/memuji)

**Contoh:**
> *"The government **should** invest more in renewable energy."*

↑ "Should" = rekomendasi = opini tentang apa yang seharusnya dilakukan.

---

#### **4. Interpretasi Personal**

Kadang penulis menginterpretasi fakta dengan cara tertentu berdasarkan sudut pandangnya:

**Contoh:**
> **Fakta**: "Unemployment rate increased by 2% last year."  
> **Opini**: "The alarming rise in unemployment demonstrates the government's failed economic policies."

↑ "Alarming" dan "failed" adalah interpretasi subjektif dari fakta objektif.

---

### **Jenis-Jenis Opinion dalam Teks Akademik**

#### **1. Direct Opinion (Opini Langsung)**

Penulis secara eksplisit menyatakan pendapatnya:

**Contoh:**
> *"In my view, artificial intelligence poses more risks than benefits to society."*

**Ciri khas:**
- Ada first-person pronouns (I, we, my, our)
- Pernyataan belief/stance jelas

---

#### **2. Implied Opinion (Opini Tersirat)**

Penulis tidak langsung bilang "saya pikir", tapi opini tersirat dari pilihan kata dan framing:

**Contoh:**
> *"Despite **so-called** progress in gender equality, women still face **significant** barriers in the workplace."*

↑ "So-called" (yang katanya) menunjukkan skeptisisme; "significant" adalah judgment. Ini opini tersirat bahwa progress gender equality belum cukup.

---

#### **3. Disguised Opinion (Opini Terselubung)**

Ini yang tricky! Opini dibungkus dengan bahasa yang terlihat objektif:

**Contoh:**
> *"It is **clear** that climate change **requires immediate action**."*

↑ "Clear" dan "requires immediate action" terdengar faktual, tapi ini sebenarnya interpretasi/judgment.

**Perhatikan frasa seperti:**
- "It is obvious/evident/clear that..."
- "There is no doubt that..."
- "Everyone knows that..."

→ Ini sering dipakai untuk membuat opini terlihat seperti fakta universal.

---

### **Jebakan dalam Soal Opinion**

#### **Jebakan 1: Fakta yang Dikira Opini**

**Contoh soal:**
> "Which of the following is an opinion?"
> A. "The study found that 70% of participants preferred option A."
> B. "This is a concerning trend that needs attention."

**Jawaban:** B (opini dengan "concerning" = judgment)  
**Jebakan:** A terlihat subjektif karena ada "preferred", tapi ini fakta tentang hasil studi.

---

#### **Jebakan 2: Opini yang Dikira Fakta**

**Contoh:**
> "Climate change is **undeniably** the most pressing issue of our time."

↑ Meskipun terdengar meyakinkan, "most pressing" adalah comparative judgment = opini!

---

#### **Jebakan 3: Mixed Statements (Fakta + Opini)**

Banyak kalimat mengandung **keduanya**:

**Contoh:**
> *"The unemployment rate rose to 15% last year (FAKTA), which is **unacceptable** (OPINI)."*

**Dalam soal SNBT:**
- Jika pertanyaannya "Which is an opinion?", pilih yang clearly subjective
- Jika "Which is a fact?", pilih yang purely verifiable

---

### **Strategi Identifikasi Opinion**

#### **Step 1: Scan untuk Subjective Markers**

Cari signal words:
- ✅ I believe, in my opinion, it seems
- ✅ should, ought to, must (obligation/recommendation)
- ✅ may, might, could (speculation)
- ✅ arguably, possibly, probably

#### **Step 2: Identifikasi Evaluative Language**

Cari adjectives/verbs yang memberi **value judgment**:
- ✅ good, bad, important, harmful, beneficial
- ✅ improve, worsen, deserve, fail

#### **Step 3: Cek Verifiability**

Tanya diri sendiri: "Bisakah pernyataan ini dibuktikan dengan data objektif?"
- **Bisa** → Kemungkinan fakta
- **Tidak bisa** → Kemungkinan opini

#### **Step 4: Perhatikan Tone**

Apakah penulis terdengar:
- ✅ Neutral dan objektif? → Lebih likely fakta
- ✅ Emosional atau persuasif? → Lebih likely opini

---

### **Contoh Analisis dalam Bacaan**

**Bacaan:**
> *According to recent studies, social media usage has increased by 40% among teenagers. **I believe** this trend is **concerning** because it **may** lead to mental health issues. Young people **should** limit their screen time. Experts suggest that excessive social media use is **linked** to anxiety and depression.*

**Analisis:**
1. "social media usage has increased by 40%" → **FAKTA** (data verifiable)
2. "I believe this trend is concerning" → **OPINI** (personal judgment dengan "I believe" dan "concerning")
3. "it may lead to mental health issues" → **OPINI** (speculation dengan "may")
4. "Young people should limit their screen time" → **OPINI** (rekomendasi dengan "should")
5. "excessive social media use is linked to anxiety" → **FAKTA** (berdasarkan expert research)

---

### **Tips Pro untuk Soal Opinion**

✅ **Jangan terpengaruh agreement pribadi!**  
Kamu mungkin setuju dengan statement, tapi tetap bisa jadi opini. Fokus pada struktur dan language markers, bukan apakah kamu agree or not.

✅ **Hati-hati dengan "universal statements"**  
"Everyone knows...", "It is obvious..." → Ini sering opini yang dipaksa jadi terlihat universal.

✅ **Perhatikan attribution**  
"Experts say..." → Bisa fakta (jika benar-benar ada experts)  
"Some people think..." → Biasanya lead ke opini

✅ **Modal verbs adalah clue besar**  
Should, must, ought to → hampir selalu opini (rekomendasi)

## **Materi 1.5.3: Analisis Fact vs Opinion**

### **Deep Dive: Perbedaan Fundamental**

Sekarang kita akan masuk lebih dalam ke **teknik membedakan** fakta dan opini—skill yang super krusial untuk SNBT!

---

### **Karakteristik FACT (Fakta)**

#### **1. Verifiable (Dapat Diverifikasi)**

Fakta bisa **dicek kebenarannya** melalui:
- Data statistik
- Penelitian ilmiah
- Dokumen resmi
- Observasi langsung

**Contoh:**
> *"Water boils at 100°C at sea level."*

↑ Ini fakta karena bisa diverifikasi melalui eksperimen.

---

#### **2. Objective (Objektif)**

Fakta **tidak tergantung** pada sudut pandang atau belief personal:
- True regardless of who observes it
- Sama untuk semua orang

**Contoh:**
> *"The earth revolves around the sun."*

↑ Fakta astronomi yang objektif.

---

#### **3. Based on Evidence (Berbasis Bukti)**

Fakta didukung oleh:
- ✅ Numbers/statistics
- ✅ Dates/historical records
- ✅ Scientific findings
- ✅ Direct quotations (dari sumber kredibel)

**Contoh:**
> *"According to WHO data, COVID-19 cases reached 700 million globally by 2023."*

↑ Fakta karena berdasarkan data WHO yang bisa dicek.

---

#### **4. Language Indicators untuk Fakta**

**Verbs faktual:**
- is, are, was, were (state of being)
- occurred, happened (past events)
- shows, indicates, demonstrates (data/research)
- found, discovered, observed (scientific findings)

**Contoh:**
> *"The study **found** that 60% of participants improved their scores."*

↑ Reporting research results = fakta.

---

### **Karakteristik OPINION (Opini)**

Kita sudah bahas di Materi 1.5.2, tapi mari kita recap dengan kontras langsung:

#### **1. Not Verifiable (Tidak Dapat Diverifikasi)**

Opini **tidak bisa dibuktikan benar/salah** secara objektif:
- Based on personal belief
- Matter of perspective

**Contoh:**
> *"Chocolate ice cream is the best flavor."*

↑ Opini karena "best" adalah subjective judgment.

---

#### **2. Subjective (Subjektif)**

Opini **tergantung** pada who's expressing it:
- Different people, different opinions
- Influenced by values, experiences, biases

**Contoh:**
> *"Remote work is more productive than office work."*

↑ Opini karena productivity bisa berbeda untuk tiap orang.

---

#### **3. Based on Interpretation/Judgment**

Opini melibatkan:
- ✅ Personal evaluation
- ✅ Interpretation of facts
- ✅ Predictions/speculations
- ✅ Recommendations

**Contoh:**
> *"The government **should** increase funding for education."*

↑ Opini karena "should" = rekomendasi based on personal judgment.

---

### **Mixed Statements: Fakta + Opini**

Ini yang paling tricky! Banyak kalimat mengandung **keduanya**:

#### **Contoh 1:**
> *"The temperature reached 40°C yesterday (FAKTA), which was **unbearably** hot (OPINI)."*

**Analisis:**
- "reached 40°C" → verifiable data = FAKTA
- "unbearably hot" → personal judgment = OPINI

---

#### **Contoh 2:**
> *"Sales increased by 25% (FAKTA), an **impressive** achievement (OPINI)."*

**Analisis:**
- "increased by 25%" → measurable = FAKTA
- "impressive achievement" → evaluation = OPINI

---

#### **Contoh 3:**
> *"According to the report, deforestation rates have doubled (FAKTA), which is **alarming** (OPINI) and **requires immediate action** (OPINI)."*

**Analisis:**
- Report data → FAKTA
- "Alarming" → emotional judgment = OPINI
- "Requires immediate action" → recommendation = OPINI

---

### **Strategi POWER untuk Membedakan**

Gunakan akronim **POWER**:

#### **P = Provable?**
- Bisakah statement ini dibuktikan dengan data/evidence?
- ✅ Yes → Likely FACT
- ❌ No → Likely OPINION

#### **O = Objective or Subjective?**
- Apakah pernyataan ini sama untuk semua orang?
- ✅ Objective → FACT
- ❌ Subjective (tergantung perspektif) → OPINION

#### **W = Words (Language Clues)**
- Ada evaluative adjectives? (good, bad, important)
- Ada modal verbs? (should, must, may)
- Ada first-person? (I believe, in my view)
- ✅ Ada → Likely OPINION
- ❌ Tidak ada → Check further

#### **E = Evidence-based?**
- Didukung data/research/dokumentasi?
- ✅ Yes → FACT
- ❌ Based on interpretation → OPINION

#### **R = Refutable?**
- Bisakah someone reasonably disagree?
- ✅ Yes (debatable) → OPINION
- ❌ No (universally accepted) → FACT

---

### **Jebakan-Jebakan Umum**

#### **Jebakan 1: Expert Opinion Dikira Pure Fact**

**Contoh:**
> *"Dr. Smith believes that this treatment is effective."*

**Analisis:**
- Ini **fakta tentang opini** Dr. Smith
- Tapi pernyataan "treatment is effective" sendiri adalah OPINI (dari Dr. Smith)

**Dalam soal:**
- Jika pertanyaan: "Which is a fact?" → Kalimat ini bisa jadi jawaban (fakta bahwa Dr. Smith believe something)
- Jika pertanyaan: "What is Dr. Smith's opinion?" → "Treatment is effective" adalah opininya

---

#### **Jebakan 2: Statistical Interpretation**

**Contoh:**
> *"70% of respondents supported the policy."* (FAKTA)  
> *"The overwhelming support shows the policy is right."* (OPINI)

**Analisis:**
- Data 70% → verifiable = FAKTA
- "Overwhelming" → judgment = OPINI
- "Right" → value judgment = OPINI

---

#### **Jebakan 3: Predictive Statements**

**Contoh:**
> *"Global temperatures will continue to rise."*

**Analisis:**
- Ini OPINI (prediction/speculation tentang future)
- Meskipun based on scientific models, tetap prediction = not verifiable fact yet

**Bandingkan dengan:**
> *"Global temperatures have risen by 1.2°C since pre-industrial times."*

↑ Ini FAKTA (historical data yang verifiable)

---

#### **Jebakan 4: "Studies Show" ≠ Automatic Fact**

**Contoh yang Tricky:**
> *"Studies suggest that meditation may improve mental health."*

**Analisis:**
- "Studies suggest" → reporting research
- **Tapi**: "may improve" → masih spekulatif
- **Conclusion**: Ini **FAKTA tentang apa yang studies suggest**, tapi conclusion-nya sendiri masih spekulatif/opini

**Bandingkan:**
> *"Studies have found a correlation between meditation and improved mental health."*

↑ Ini lebih ke FAKTA karena "found" (completed research) dan "correlation" (measurable relationship)

---

### **Praktik Analisis Kompleks**

**Bacaan Contoh:**

> *Climate change is **undeniably** one of the greatest threats facing humanity. **According to NASA**, global temperatures have risen by approximately 1.1°C since 1880. **I believe** we must take **immediate** action to reduce carbon emissions. The Paris Agreement aims to limit warming to 1.5°C, but **critics argue** that the targets are **insufficient**. Renewable energy **should** be prioritized over fossil fuels. **Data shows** that solar and wind power have become increasingly cost-effective.*

**Mari kita breakdown:**

1. "Climate change is undeniably one of the greatest threats"
   - **OPINI**: "Greatest threats" = value judgment; "undeniably" = framing opinion as if it's universal truth

2. "According to NASA, global temperatures have risen by approximately 1.1°C since 1880"
   - **FAKTA**: Specific data dari sumber kredibel (NASA), measurable, verifiable

3. "I believe we must take immediate action"
   - **OPINI**: "I believe" = explicit opinion marker; "must" = strong recommendation

4. "The Paris Agreement aims to limit warming to 1.5°C"
   - **FAKTA**: Stated goal dari treaty (dokumentasi resmi)

5. "Critics argue that the targets are insufficient"
   - **FAKTA tentang OPINI**: Fakta bahwa critics have this opinion; tapi "insufficient" itself adalah judgment

6. "Renewable energy should be prioritized"
   - **OPINI**: "Should" = recommendation/prescription

7. "Data shows that solar and wind power have become increasingly cost-effective"
   - **FAKTA**: "Data shows" = evidence-based; "cost-effective" bisa diukur dengan economic metrics

---

### **Tips Menghadapi Soal Fact vs Opinion**

✅ **Jangan terburu-buru!**  
Baca kalimat lengkap, jangan cuma lihat sekilas.

✅ **Fokus pada structure, bukan content agreement**  
Kamu bisa strongly agree dengan sebuah opini, tapi tetap harus recognize it as opinion.

✅ **Check attribution**  
- "The report states..." → likely FACT (jika report credible)
- "Experts believe..." → OPINION (belief dari experts)

✅ **Modal verbs adalah red flag**  
Should, must, ought to, may, might → hampir always OPINION

✅ **Numbers aren't always facts**  
- "70% agreed" → FACT
- "A disappointing 70% agreed" → Mixed (fact + opinion)

✅ **Comparative/superlative language**  
Better, best, worse, worst, most important → usually OPINION (judgment)

---

### **Common Patterns dalam Soal SNBT**

#### **Pattern 1: "Which statement is a FACT?"**

**Strategi:**
- Eliminasi yang clearly opini (ada should, good/bad, I think)
- Pilih yang specific, measurable, verifiable

#### **Pattern 2: "Which is the author's OPINION?"**

**Strategi:**
- Cari subjective language
- Cari recommendations (should, must)
- Cari evaluative words (important, necessary, harmful)

#### **Pattern 3: "All are facts EXCEPT..."**

**Strategi:**
- Cari yang paling subjective/judgmental
- Satu yang beda (opinion) dari yang lain (facts)

---

### **Latihan Mini: Fact or Opinion?**

Coba identifikasi sendiri (jawaban di akhir):

1. "The company's revenue increased by 15% last quarter."
2. "This represents an impressive turnaround for the struggling business."
3. "According to the survey, 85% of customers reported satisfaction."
4. "Customer satisfaction should be the company's top priority."
5. "The CEO announced plans to expand into Asian markets."
6. "This expansion is a risky but necessary move."

**Jawaban:**
1. FAKTA (specific data)
2. OPINI ("impressive" = judgment; "struggling" = evaluative)
3. FAKTA (survey result, verifiable)
4. OPINI ("should" = recommendation)
5. FAKTA (announced plan = verifiable statement)
6. OPINI ("risky but necessary" = judgment call)

## **Materi 1.5.4: Analisis Author's Tone**

### **Apa Itu Tone dalam Teks?**

Kalau **opinion** adalah *what the author thinks*, maka **tone** adalah *how the author feels* dan *how it comes across* dalam tulisan.

**Analogi seru:**
Bayangkan kamu kirim pesan ke teman:
- "Oke, terserah kamu." → Tone bisa netral, bisa sinis (tergantung konteks!)
- "Oke! Terserah kamu! 😊" → Tone lebih supportive
- "Oke... terserah kamu..." → Tone skeptical/reluctant

Nah, dalam teks tertulis, kita gak punya emoji atau nada suara, jadi kita harus **baca tone dari pilihan kata dan struktur kalimat**.

---

### **Kenapa Tone Penting?**

1. **Mengungkap sikap penulis** yang tidak eksplisit
2. **Mempengaruhi credibility** → Tone yang terlalu emosional bisa mengurangi objektivitas
3. **Membantu understand purpose** → Persuasive tone → likely trying to convince
4. **Membedakan genre** → Objective tone (science) vs Subjective tone (editorial)

---

### **Spektrum Tone: Dari Positif ke Negatif**

Tone bisa berada di spektrum:

```
POSITIF ←→ NETRAL ←→ NEGATIF
```

#### **Positive Tones:**
- **Optimistic**: Penuh harapan, melihat sisi baik
- **Enthusiastic**: Bersemangat, antusias
- **Supportive**: Mendukung, membantu
- **Appreciative**: Menghargai, grateful
- **Reverent**: Penuh hormat, respek
- **Sincere**: Tulus, jujur
- **Encouraging**: Memberi semangat

#### **Neutral/Objective Tones:**
- **Objective**: Netral, tidak memihak
- **Detached**: Tidak emosional, distant
- **Informative**: Sekadar memberi info
- **Matter-of-fact**: Straightforward, apa adanya
- **Analytical**: Menganalisis tanpa judgment emosional

#### **Negative Tones:**
- **Critical**: Mengkritik, menunjukkan kelemahan
- **Skeptical**: Meragukan, tidak yakin
- **Cynical**: Sinis, tidak percaya motif baik
- **Pessimistic**: Pesimis, melihat sisi buruk
- **Sarcastic**: Sarkastik, menyindir
- **Contemptuous**: Merendahkan, hina
- **Indignant**: Marah karena ketidakadilan
- **Disapproving**: Tidak setuju, menolak

---

### **Vocabulary untuk Mendeskripsikan Tone**

Berikut daftar lengkap vocabulary yang sering muncul dalam opsi jawaban soal SNBT:

#### **A. Positive/Supportive Tones**

1. **Optimistic** (optimis)
   - Melihat masa depan dengan harapan
   - Fokus pada kemungkinan positif
   - *Example clue*: "This promising development...", "bright future", "hopeful signs"

2. **Enthusiastic** (antusias)
   - Bersemangat dan excited
   - Energetic language
   - *Example clue*: "exciting breakthrough!", "tremendous opportunity", exclamation marks

3. **Reverent** (penuh hormat)
   - Menunjukkan respek dan admirasi
   - Formal, careful language
   - *Example clue*: "the esteemed scholar", "remarkable achievement"

4. **Supportive** (mendukung)
   - Backing up an idea atau person
   - Encouraging language
   - *Example clue*: "deserves our support", "commendable effort"

5. **Sincere** (tulus)
   - Genuine, honest, tidak pretentious
   - Straightforward but warm
   - *Example clue*: Simple, honest expression of views

---

#### **B. Neutral/Objective Tones**

1. **Objective** (objektif)
   - No personal bias
   - Factual, balanced
   - *Example clue*: Data-driven, presents multiple perspectives equally

2. **Detached** (tidak terlibat emosi)
   - Emotionally distant
   - Observational, tidak invested
   - *Example clue*: Clinical language, no evaluative words

3. **Informative** (informatif)
   - Focus pada menyampaikan informasi
   - Educational tone
   - *Example clue*: "It is important to note...", clear explanations

4. **Analytical** (analitis)
   - Breaking down ideas logically
   - Examining evidence
   - *Example clue*: "Analysis shows...", "examining the data..."

---

#### **C. Critical/Questioning Tones**

1. **Critical** (kritis)
   - Menunjukkan kelemahan atau masalah
   - Bukan berarti angry, tapi evaluative
   - *Example clue*: "fails to address", "overlooks", "problematic"

2. **Skeptical** (skeptis)
   - Meragukan klaim atau idea
   - Questioning validity
   - *Example clue*: "allegedly", "claims to", "questionable", "dubious"

3. **Cautious** (hati-hati)
   - Careful, tidak commit fully
   - Hedging language
   - *Example clue*: "may", "might", "appears to", "suggests"

4. **Concerned** (prihatin)
   - Worried tapi tidak hostile
   - Shows care about issue
   - *Example clue*: "troubling trend", "raises concerns", "worrying"

---

#### **D. Negative/Hostile Tones**

1. **Cynical** (sinis)
   - Tidak percaya motif baik orang/institusi
   - Believes people are selfish
   - *Example clue*: "so-called experts", quotation marks untuk undermine

2. **Sarcastic** (sarkastik)
   - Saying opposite of what is meant
   - Ironic, mocking
   - *Example clue*: Exaggerated praise yang actually kritik

3. **Contemptuous** (merendahkan)
   - Showing disdain
   - Very negative, dismissive
   - *Example clue*: "pathetic attempt", "laughable claim"

4. **Indignant** (marah karena unfairness)
   - Angry at injustice
   - Moral outrage
   - *Example clue*: "outrageous", "unacceptable", strong moral language

5. **Pessimistic** (pesimis)
   - Expecting negative outcomes
   - Opposite of optimistic
   - *Example clue*: "inevitable decline", "grim future", "little hope"

6. **Disapproving** (tidak setuju)
   - Shows disapproval
   - Negative judgment
   - *Example clue*: "unfortunate decision", "regrettable"

---

### **Cara Mengidentifikasi Tone dari Text**

#### **1. Perhatikan Word Choice (Diction)**

Pilihan kata adalah clue terbesar untuk tone:

**Contoh 1: Neutral Tone**
> *"The policy was implemented in 2020 and affects approximately 2 million citizens."*

↑ Factual words, no evaluative language = **Objective tone**

**Contoh 2: Critical Tone**
> *"The **poorly conceived** policy was **hastily** implemented in 2020, **disrupting** the lives of 2 million citizens."*

↑ Negative evaluative words (poorly conceived, hastily, disrupting) = **Critical tone**

**Contoh 3: Supportive Tone**
> *"The **well-designed** policy was **successfully** implemented in 2020, **benefiting** 2 million citizens."*

↑ Positive evaluative words = **Supportive/Positive tone**

---

#### **2. Perhatikan Intensifiers dan Qualifiers**

**Intensifiers** (memperkuat):
- very, extremely, highly, deeply, absolutely, utterly
- Menunjukkan strong feeling

**Qualifiers** (melemahkan):
- somewhat, rather, fairly, relatively, quite
- Menunjukkan restraint atau hesitation

**Contoh:**
> "The results are **extremely disappointing**." → Strong negative tone  
> "The results are **somewhat disappointing**." → Mild negative tone

---

#### **3. Perhatikan Sentence Structure**

**Short, declarative sentences:**
- Bisa indicate confidence atau directness
- *Example*: "This must stop. Now." → Firm/Indignant

**Long, complex sentences:**
- Bisa indicate careful analysis atau tentative reasoning
- *Example*: "While it is possible that..., one must also consider..." → Cautious/Analytical

**Rhetorical questions:**
- Often indicate skepticism atau sarcasm
- *Example*: "Is anyone truly surprised by these results?" → Cynical/Sarcastic

---

#### **4. Perhatikan Connotation (Makna Konotasi)**

Kata dengan denotasi sama bisa punya connotation berbeda:

| Neutral | Positive | Negative |
|---------|----------|----------|
| thin | slender, slim | skinny, scrawny |
| confident | self-assured | arrogant, cocky |
| cheap | affordable, economical | shoddy, inferior |
| persistent | determined, tenacious | stubborn, obstinate |

**Dalam analisis tone:**
> "He is a **determined** leader." → Positive tone  
> "He is a **stubborn** leader." → Negative tone

---

### **Jebakan dalam Soal Tone**

#### **Jebakan 1: Confusing Critical with Hostile**

**Critical** ≠ **Hostile**

- **Critical tone**: Menganalisis kelemahan secara objektif, constructive criticism
- **Hostile tone**: Angry, attacking, very negative

**Contoh Critical (bukan Hostile):**
> *"While the study provides valuable insights, it **fails to account for** several confounding variables."*

↑ Ini critical (menunjukkan kelemahan) tapi tidak hostile (tidak attacking atau angry).

---

#### **Jebakan 2: Confusing Objective with Detached**

Keduanya netral tapi ada nuansa beda:

- **Objective**: Presenting facts without bias, balanced
- **Detached**: Emotionally distant, tidak invested in topic

**Contoh Objective:**
> *"The policy has both advantages and disadvantages. On one hand... On the other hand..."*

**Contoh Detached:**
> *"The policy exists. Some support it, others don't."*

↑ Detached terkesan lebih "cold" atau uninterested.

---

#### **Jebakan 3: Sarcasm yang Tidak Kentara**

Sarcasm bisa subtle, terutama dalam teks tertulis:

**Contoh Sarcasm:**
> *"Of course, the government's decision was **brilliant**—if destroying the economy was the goal."*

↑ "Brilliant" di sini sarkastik (actually means the opposite).

**Clues untuk sarcasm:**
- Exaggerated praise dalam konteks negatif
- Quotation marks around words ("brilliant" plan)
- Irony atau contradiction

---

### **Strategi Menentukan Tone**

#### **Step-by-Step Approach:**

**Step 1: Identify Subject Matter**
Apa topik yang dibahas? Controversial issue? Scientific fact?

**Step 2: Look for Evaluative Language**
Ada kata-kata judgment? Positive or negative?

**Step 3: Check Emotional Intensity**
Strong words (extremely, utterly) atau restrained (somewhat, rather)?

**Step 4: Analyze Overall Pattern**
Apakah consistently positive, negative, atau mixed?

**Step 5: Match dengan Vocabulary Tone**
Cocokkan dengan daftar tone words yang kamu tahu.

---

### **Contoh Analisis Tone Lengkap**

**Bacaan 1:**
> *"Recent advancements in renewable energy technology represent a **promising** step toward addressing climate change. Solar and wind power have become increasingly **efficient** and **cost-effective**, offering **hope** for a sustainable future. While challenges remain, the **progress** made thus far is **encouraging**."*

**Analisis:**
- **Evaluative words**: promising, efficient, cost-effective, hope, progress, encouraging
- **Semua positive**
- **No hedging**, fairly confident
- **Conclusion**: **Optimistic tone**

---

**Bacaan 2:**
> *"The government's so-called 'reform' policy has **predictably** resulted in chaos. Despite **warnings** from experts, officials **stubbornly** proceeded with implementation. The **disastrous** consequences were **entirely avoidable**."*

**Analisis:**
- **Evaluative words**: so-called (dismissive), predictably (cynical), warnings (ignored), stubbornly (negative), disastrous, avoidable
- **Heavy negative judgment**
- **Blame implied** (officials ignored warnings)
- **Conclusion**: **Critical/Indignant tone**

---

**Bacaan 3:**
> *"The study examined 500 participants over a six-month period. Results indicated a 15% improvement in test group compared to control. Data was collected using standardized protocols and analyzed using statistical methods."*

**Analisis:**
- **Factual language**: examined, indicated, collected, analyzed
- **No evaluative words**
- **No emotional language**
- **Conclusion**: **Objective/Informative tone**

---

### **Tips Menghadapi Soal Tone**

✅ **Baca keseluruhan passage, jangan cuma satu kalimat**  
Tone sering terbangun dari akumulasi, bukan single word.

✅ **Fokus pada adjectives dan adverbs**  
Ini word classes yang paling sering membawa tone.

✅ **Perhatikan proportion**  
Jika 80% positive words dan 20% negative, overall tone likely positive.

✅ **Context matters**  
Kata "interesting" bisa genuine atau sarcastic tergantung context.

✅ **Eliminasi ekstrem dulu**  
Jika tone clearly not "contemptuous" (very negative), eliminate that option early.

✅ **Bedakan tone penulis vs tone yang dijelaskan**  
Penulis bisa menjelaskan view yang hostile tanpa adopting that tone themselves.

### **Common Tone Patterns dalam Berbagai Genre**

#### **Scientific/Academic Texts:**
- Usually: **Objective, Analytical, Informative**
- Jarang: Emotional atau strongly opinionated

#### **Editorials/Opinion Pieces:**
- Bisa: **Critical, Persuasive, Passionate**
- Range: Dari supportive sampai hostile

#### **News Reports:**
- Ideal: **Objective, Detached**
- Realita: Sometimes subtle bias

#### **Literary Texts:**
- Wide range: Bisa anything dari **nostalgic** sampai **satirical**

## **Materi 1.5.5: Analisis Emotional Language dan Bias**

### **Apa Itu Emotional Language?**

**Emotional language** adalah penggunaan kata-kata yang dirancang untuk **memicu respons emosional** dari pembaca, bukan sekadar menyampaikan informasi objektif.

**Contoh perbedaan:**
- **Netral**: "The man entered the room."
- **Emotional**: "The man **burst into** the room."

↑ "Burst into" membawa emotional charge (dramatic, forceful) yang tidak ada di "entered".

---

### **Kenapa Penting untuk SNBT?**

1. **Mengidentifikasi bias** penulis
2. **Menilai credibility** dan objectivity teks
3. **Membedakan informasi vs persuasi**
4. **Critical reading skill** untuk berbagai tipe teks

**Real-world relevance:**
Di era informasi/misinformasi, kemampuan detect emotional manipulation super penting!

---

### **Types of Emotional Language**

#### **1. Loaded Words (Kata Bermuatan)**

Kata dengan **strong connotation** (positive atau negative):

**Positive Loaded Words:**
- freedom, justice, progress, innovation, hero, courageous, triumph
- *Example*: "The **brave** activists **fought** for **justice**."

**Negative Loaded Words:**
- oppression, corruption, failure, destruction, villain, cowardly, disaster
- *Example*: "The **corrupt** officials **destroyed** public trust."

**Compare Neutral Alternatives:**
| Loaded | Neutral |
|--------|---------|
| regime | government |
| propaganda | information |
| mob | crowd |
| exploit | use |
| manipulate | influence |

---

#### **2. Emotive Adjectives dan Adverbs**

**Emotive adjectives:**
- devastating, horrific, wonderful, terrible, magnificent, appalling
- Add emotional color beyond factual description

**Emotive adverbs:**
- shockingly, tragically, fortunately, unfortunately, alarmingly
- Frame facts dengan emotional angle

**Contoh:**
> **Neutral**: "The accident resulted in five deaths."  
> **Emotional**: "The **tragic** accident **devastated** the community, resulting in five **senseless** deaths."

↑ Emotional version memicu sympathy dan sadness.

---

#### **3. Hyperbole (Exaggeration)**

Exaggeration untuk dramatic effect:

**Examples:**
- "This is the **worst** decision **ever** made."
- "**Everyone** knows this policy is **completely** ineffective."
- "The situation is a **total disaster**."

**Red flags:**
- Superlatives: best, worst, greatest, most terrible
- Absolutes: always, never, everyone, no one, completely, totally
- Exaggerated language: disaster, catastrophe, crisis (untuk hal yang mungkin less severe)

---

#### **4. Euphemism vs Dysphemism**

**Euphemism**: Softening negative reality
- "Passed away" instead of "died"
- "Let go" instead of "fired"
- "Collateral damage" instead of "civilian deaths"

**Dysphemism**: Making something sound worse
- "Regime" instead of "government" (when wanting to sound negative)
- "Propaganda" instead of "public information"

**Both manipulate perception!**

---

### **Identifying Bias dalam Teks**

**Bias** adalah keberpihakan penulis yang **mempengaruhi objectivity**.

#### **Types of Bias:**

**1. Selection Bias**
- Memilih informasi yang support viewpoint, mengabaikan yang contradict
- *Example*: Hanya menyebutkan benefits dari policy tanpa mention drawbacks

**2. Language Bias**
- Using loaded words untuk satu sisi, neutral untuk lainnya
- *Example*: "Pro-life advocates" vs "Anti-choice extremists" → bias in framing

**3. Source Bias**
- Hanya cite sources yang agree dengan viewpoint
- Ignore atau dismiss opposing experts

**4. Framing Bias**
- Presenting information dengan cara yang influence perception
- *Example*: "50% success rate" vs "50% failure rate" → same data, different framing

---

### **Techniques for Detecting Bias**

#### **Technique 1: Look for Imbalance**

**Questions to ask:**
- Apakah both sides of issue presented?
- Apakah one perspective diberi more space/emphasis?
- Apakah opposing views fairly represented atau just dismissed?

**Contoh Biased:**
> *"While supporters claim the policy helps the economy, in reality it has caused widespread suffering."*

↑ Dismisses supporters dengan "claim" (implying not true), while presenting opposing view as "reality".

**Contoh Balanced:**
> *"Supporters argue the policy helps the economy by creating jobs, while critics contend it has negative environmental impacts."*

↑ Both views presented with neutral framing ("argue" vs "contend").

---

#### **Technique 2: Check Word Choice Consistency**

Apakah penulis use different language for similar actions tergantung who does it?

**Contoh Bias:**
- Our side: "freedom fighters" / Their side: "terrorists"
- Our policy: "strategic initiative" / Their policy: "reckless gamble"
- We: "carefully consider" / They: "stubbornly refuse"

**Red flag**: Inconsistent language untuk similar actions/groups.

---

#### **Technique 3: Identify Emotional Appeals**

Apakah penulis relying pada:
- **Logic dan evidence** (rational appeal) → Less bias
- **Emotion dan fear** (emotional appeal) → Potentially biased

**Contoh Emotional Appeal:**
> *"If we don't act now, our children will inherit a **dying** planet. The **catastrophic** consequences are **unimaginable**."*

↑ Heavy emotional language (dying, catastrophic, unimaginable) untuk manipulate through fear.

---

### **Connotation Analysis**

Connotation adalah **associated meanings** beyond literal definition.

#### **Positive vs Negative Connotations:**

| Concept | Positive | Neutral | Negative |
|---------|----------|---------|----------|
| Thin | slender, svelte | slim | skinny, scrawny |
| Confident | self-assured, poised | confident | arrogant, cocky |
| Thrifty | economical, prudent | careful with money | cheap, stingy |
| Unique | distinctive, special | unusual | weird, odd |

**Dalam teks:**
> "The CEO is **confident** in her vision." → Positive  
> "The CEO is **arrogant** about her vision." → Negative

↑ Same basic idea (high self-confidence), different emotional coloring.

---

### **Impact pada Credibility**

#### **High Bias = Low Credibility?**

**Not always!**

- **Opinion pieces/editorials**: Expected to have strong viewpoint
- **Scientific papers**: Expected to be objective
- **News reports**: Should strive for balance

**Red flags for problematic bias:**
✅ Presenting opinion as fact  
✅ Ignoring contradictory evidence  
✅ Using manipulative emotional language  
✅ Ad hominem attacks (attacking person, not argument)  
✅ False dichotomies (only two extreme options presented)

---

### **Contoh Analisis: Neutral vs Biased Text**

**Version 1: Neutral/Objective**
> *"The new education policy was implemented in September. Under the policy, class sizes will be reduced from 30 to 25 students. Proponents argue this will improve individual attention, while critics note the increased costs. Initial data shows mixed results across different districts."*

**Analysis:**
- ✅ Factual information
- ✅ Both perspectives presented
- ✅ Neutral language ("proponents argue", "critics note")
- ✅ Acknowledgment of complexity ("mixed results")

---

**Version 2: Positively Biased**
> *"The **groundbreaking** education policy was **successfully** launched in September. The **innovative** approach reduces class sizes, **ensuring** students receive the attention they **deserve**. While some **short-sighted** critics **complain** about costs, the **undeniable benefits** are already evident across **thriving** districts."*

**Analysis:**
- ❌ Loaded words: groundbreaking, innovative, deserve, short-sighted, undeniable, thriving
- ❌ Dismisses opposition: "short-sighted critics complain"
- ❌ Overstates success: "undeniable benefits", "thriving"
- ❌ Presents opinion as fact: "ensuring" students get what they "deserve"

---

**Version 3: Negatively Biased**
> *"The **controversial** education policy was **hastily** imposed in September. The **costly** scheme reduces class sizes at **enormous** expense to taxpayers. Supporters **naively claim** this will help students, but **disastrous** implementation has **predictably** led to **chaos** in struggling districts."*

**Analysis:**
- ❌ Loaded words: controversial, hastily, costly, enormous, naively, disastrous, chaos
- ❌ Dismisses supporters: "naively claim"
- ❌ Exaggerates problems: "enormous expense", "disastrous", "chaos"
- ❌ Assumes bad outcome: "predictably led to chaos"

---

### **Strategi Menganalisis Emotional Language dan Bias**

#### **Step 1: Identify Evaluative Words**

Highlight atau mark semua:
- Adjectives dengan strong connotation
- Adverbs yang add emotional coloring
- Verbs dengan emotional charge

#### **Step 2: Check for Balance**

- Apakah semua perspectives diberi equal treatment?
- Apakah one side described with loaded language while other neutral?

#### **Step 3: Separate Fact from Interpretation**

**Fact**: "The policy costs $10 million."  
**Interpretation**: "The policy is **expensive**." (value judgment)

#### **Step 4: Assess Overall Impact**

- Setelah membaca, apakah you feel manipulated emotionally?
- Apakah you were presented dengan clear information to make own judgment?

---

### **Jebakan dalam Soal tentang Bias**

#### **Jebakan 1: Confusing Strong Opinion with Bias**

**Strong opinion** ≠ Automatically **bias**

- Opinion piece **boleh** strongly opinionated
- **Bias** lebih tentang unfair representation atau manipulation

**Contoh:**
> *"I believe climate action is urgent. However, I acknowledge valid concerns about economic impacts."*

↑ Strong opinion tapi mengakui opposing view = less problematic bias.

---

#### **Jebakan 2: Assuming All Emotional Language = Bad**

Emotional language **tidak selalu** manipulatif:
- ✅ Appropriate dalam persuasive writing
- ✅ Can make text engaging
- ❌ Problematic jika replaces evidence atau misrepresents facts

**Context matters!**

---

### **Tips untuk Soal Bias dan Emotional Language**

✅ **Map out word choices**  
Categorize words sebagai positive, negative, atau neutral untuk see pattern.

✅ **Look for what's missing**  
Kadang bias terlihat dari **apa yang TIDAK disebutkan** (selective omission).

✅ **Compare treatment of different groups**  
Apakah same language dipakai untuk similar actions?

✅ **Check citations**  
Apakah sources varied atau semua from one perspective?

✅ **Assess proportionality**  
Apakah emotional language proportional dengan seriousness dari issue?

**Bacaan:**
> *"The reckless decision to implement this untested policy has predictably resulted in disaster. Despite desperate warnings from concerned experts, stubborn officials blindly pushed forward. Innocent citizens now suffer the catastrophic consequences of this colossal failure. Meanwhile, the architects of this debacle refuse to admit their obvious mistakes."*

**Your turn to analyze:**
1. Identify emotional/loaded words
2. What's the bias?
3. How would neutral version sound?

**Analysis:**
1. **Loaded words**: reckless, untested, disaster, desperate, concerned (for experts), stubborn, blindly, innocent, catastrophic, colossal, debacle, obvious
2. **Bias**: Strongly against the policy dan officials
3. **Neutral version**: "The new policy was implemented despite concerns from some experts. Citizens have experienced challenges as a result. Officials maintain the policy will improve with adjustments."
