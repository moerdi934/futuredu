# SECTION 3: KOSAKATA DAN REFERENSI

## Topik 3.3: Paraphrase dan Restatement

Halo warriors SNBT! 🎯 Siap-siap masuk ke salah satu skill paling **krusial** di SNBT—Paraphrase dan Restatement. Ini bukan cuma soal "ngubah kata", tapi seni mengemas ulang informasi dengan gaya yang beda tapi makna tetap sama persis. Bayangin kayak kamu cerita ulang film ke temen tapi pakai bahasa sendiri—nah, kurang lebih kayak gitu, tapi versi akademiknya!

Kenapa ini penting banget? Karena **hampir semua opsi jawaban di SNBT** adalah bentuk parafrase dari informasi di teks. Jadi kalau kamu masih nyari jawaban yang persis sama kata-katanya dengan teks—good luck, karena kamu bakal kesulitan! 😅

---

### **Materi 3.3.1: Pengenalan Paraphrase dan Restatement**

**Apa sih bedanya Paraphrase sama Restatement?**

Sebenarnya keduanya mirip-mirip, tapi ada nuansa kecil:

- **Paraphrase** = Mengungkapkan informasi yang sama dengan struktur kalimat dan pilihan kata yang berbeda. Bisa lebih panjang atau lebih pendek dari aslinya.
  
- **Restatement** = Menyatakan ulang ide dengan cara berbeda, biasanya lebih ringkas dan to-the-point.

Intinya: **Beda bungkus, isi sama!** 📦

**Contoh gampangnya:**

**Original:** "The research shows that regular exercise significantly improves mental health."

**Paraphrase:** "According to the study, consistent physical activity greatly enhances psychological well-being."

Liat kan? Beda banget kan kata-katanya, tapi maknanya **identik**!

---

**Kenapa Paraphrase Penting di SNBT?**

1. **90% opsi jawaban adalah parafrase** dari informasi di teks
2. Pembuat soal **sengaja** nggak pakai kata yang sama persis biar nge-test comprehension kamu, bukan cuma kemampuan matching kata
3. Kamu harus bisa **recognize essence** dari informasi, bukan cuma recognize kata-katanya
4. Skill paraphrase juga penting buat soal **restatement, summary, dan inference**

---

**Jebakan Umum yang Sering Bikin Kecele:**

❌ **Jebakan 1: Kata yang Sama tapi Makna Berubah**
Pembuat soal kadang pakai kata yang muncul di teks, tapi konteksnya diubah. Jangan langsung tergoda!

**Contoh:**
- Teks: "The study was **significant** because it involved 10,000 participants."
- Opsi jebakan: "The **significant** number of participants was 10,000."
- Opsi benar: "The importance of the study lay in its large sample size."

❌ **Jebakan 2: Parafrase yang Sepertinya Benar tapi Sebenernya Salah**
Kadang ada opsi yang kedengeran bagus dan sophisticated, tapi kalau dicek lagi, maknanya beda!

**Contoh:**
- Teks: "Temperature affects the rate of chemical reactions."
- Opsi jebakan: "Chemical reactions determine temperature levels." (Kebalik!)
- Opsi benar: "The speed of chemical reactions is influenced by temperature."

❌ **Jebakan 3: Informasi Tidak Lengkap**
Opsi cuma ngambil sebagian info, nggak semuanya.

**Contoh:**
- Teks: "Students who study regularly and get enough sleep perform better academically."
- Opsi jebakan: "Regular study habits lead to academic success." (Kehilangan info soal sleep!)
- Opsi benar: "Academic performance improves with consistent studying and adequate rest."

---

**Tips Jitu Recognizing Paraphrase:**

✅ **Fokus pada MAKNA, bukan KATA**
Jangan terpaku pada apakah kata-katanya sama. Yang penting adalah: **apakah informasinya equivalent?**

✅ **Break Down Informasi Jadi Komponen Kecil**
Misalnya kalimat ada 3 poin penting, pastikan ketiga poin itu ada di parafrase.

✅ **Hati-hati dengan Negasi**
"Not unimportant" itu artinya "important"—double negative jadi positive!

✅ **Perhatikan Quantifiers**
"Many" ≠ "Most" ≠ "All" ≠ "Some"—ini beda tingkat generalisasinya!

---

**Skill yang Perlu Dikuasai untuk Paraphrase:**

1. **Synonym Substitution** (ganti kata dengan sinonim)
2. **Structure Change** (ubah struktur kalimat)
3. **Voice Change** (aktif jadi pasif atau sebaliknya)
4. **Word Class Change** (kata kerja jadi kata benda, dll)
5. **Condensing/Expanding** (singkat jadi detail atau sebaliknya)

Tenang, kita bakal bahas satu-satu di materi berikutnya! 💪

---

### **Materi 3.3.2: Analisis Paraphrase dengan Synonym Substitution**

Ini adalah **teknik paling dasar** dalam paraphrase—ganti kata dengan sinonimnya! Tapi jangan salah, ini nggak sesimpel kedengarannya. Ada banyak hal yang harus diperhatikan.

---

**Prinsip Dasar Synonym Substitution:**

✅ **Synonym harus punya makna yang SAMA atau SANGAT MIRIP**
Jangan asal ganti! Pastikan konteksnya cocok.

**Contoh:**
- "important" → "significant" ✅
- "important" → "famous" ❌ (beda makna!)

✅ **Perhatikan Register (Formal vs Informal)**
Di teks akademik, pakai synonym yang formal.

**Contoh:**
- Formal: assist, purchase, commence
- Informal: help, buy, start

Di SNBT, biasanya pakai yang formal karena teksnya akademik.

✅ **Perhatikan Connotation (Konotasi)**
Beberapa synonym punya nuansa berbeda!

**Contoh:**
- "slim" (positif) vs "skinny" (agak negatif) vs "slender" (lebih positif/elegan)

---

**Contoh Synonym Substitution dalam Kalimat:**

**Original:**
"The government implemented new policies to **reduce** pollution."

**Paraphrase:**
"The government introduced fresh regulations to **decrease** environmental contamination."

**Analisis:**
- implemented → introduced
- new → fresh
- policies → regulations
- reduce → decrease
- pollution → environmental contamination

Semuanya diganti, tapi makna **tetap sama persis**!

---

**Common Synonym Pairs yang Sering Muncul di SNBT:**

| **Original** | **Paraphrase** |
|--------------|----------------|
| show | demonstrate, reveal, indicate, display |
| important | significant, crucial, vital, essential |
| increase | rise, grow, escalate, surge, expand |
| decrease | decline, reduce, drop, diminish, fall |
| problem | issue, challenge, difficulty, obstacle |
| solution | answer, resolution, remedy, fix |
| study | research, investigation, examination |
| find | discover, identify, detect, uncover |
| suggest | indicate, propose, imply, recommend |
| effect | impact, consequence, result, outcome |

---

**Jebakan dalam Synonym Substitution:**

❌ **Jebakan 1: False Friends (Kata yang Mirip tapi Beda Makna)**

Hati-hati dengan kata yang keliatan mirip tapi artinya beda!

**Contoh:**
- "economic" (berkaitan dengan ekonomi) ≠ "economical" (hemat/efisien)
- "historic" (penting dalam sejarah) ≠ "historical" (berkaitan dengan sejarah)

❌ **Jebakan 2: Synonym yang Nggak Cocok di Konteks Tertentu**

Nggak semua synonym bisa dipakai di semua situasi!

**Contoh:**
- "Children" bisa diganti "kids" (informal) tapi nggak bisa diganti "offspring" (terlalu formal/biologis)

❌ **Jebakan 3: Partial Synonym**

Beberapa kata cuma sinonim di konteks tertentu!

**Contoh:**
- "Bright" bisa artinya "terang" (bright light) atau "pintar" (bright student)
- "Bright" ≠ "intelligent" kalau konteksnya cahaya!

---

**Tips Pro:**

💡 **Jangan Ganti SEMUA Kata**
Yang diganti cuma **content words** (kata benda, kata kerja, kata sifat). Function words (a, the, is, in, on) biasanya tetap sama atau berubah secara natural sesuai struktur.

💡 **Maintain the Core Meaning**
Inti informasinya harus **identik**—nggak boleh ada yang hilang atau nambah.

💡 **Academic Vocabulary is Your Friend**
Semakin banyak kosakata akademik yang kamu kuasai, semakin gampang recognize paraphrase!

---

### **Materi 3.3.3: Analisis Paraphrase dengan Structure Change**

Nah, ini levelnya naik! Bukan cuma ganti kata, tapi **ubah struktur kalimatnya** juga. Ini yang bikin paraphrase jadi lebih sophisticated dan harder to detect kalau kamu nggak paham konsepnya.

---

**Jenis-Jenis Structure Change:**

**1. Active Voice ↔ Passive Voice**

Ini yang paling sering! Subjek sama objek ditukar tempatnya.

**Active → Passive:**
- **Original (Active):** "The scientist conducted the experiment."
- **Paraphrase (Passive):** "The experiment was conducted by the scientist."

**Passive → Active:**
- **Original (Passive):** "The book was written by Shakespeare."
- **Paraphrase (Active):** "Shakespeare wrote the book."

**Kapan pakai Active vs Passive?**
- **Active:** Kalau mau emphasize **siapa yang melakukan** (the doer)
- **Passive:** Kalau mau emphasize **apa yang terjadi** (the action/result) atau kalau doer-nya nggak penting/nggak diketahui

---

**2. Positive ↔ Negative Form**

Ngubah kalimat positif jadi negatif (atau sebaliknya) tanpa mengubah makna.

**Contoh:**
- **Positive:** "She always arrives on time."
- **Negative:** "She never arrives late."

Atau lebih tricky:
- **Positive:** "This is important."
- **Double Negative:** "This is not unimportant." (artinya tetap "important")

**Hati-hati dengan Double Negative!**
"Not uncommon" = "common"
"Not unlikely" = "likely"

Di SNBT, kadang pembuat soal suka pakai ini buat nge-test apakah kamu beneran paham atau cuma matching kata!

---

**3. Clause Restructuring**

Ngubah struktur klausa: dari complex jadi simple, atau sebaliknya.

**Complex → Simple:**
- **Original:** "Although it was raining, they went to the park."
- **Paraphrase:** "Despite the rain, they went to the park."

**Simple → Complex:**
- **Original:** "Due to heavy traffic, she arrived late."
- **Paraphrase:** "She arrived late because the traffic was heavy."

**Compound → Complex:**
- **Original:** "He studied hard, and he passed the exam."
- **Paraphrase:** "He passed the exam because he studied hard."

---

**4. Noun Phrase ↔ Verb Phrase**

Ngubah bentuk kata tanpa ngubah makna.

**Verb → Noun:**
- **Original:** "He decided to leave."
- **Paraphrase:** "He made a decision to leave."

**Noun → Verb:**
- **Original:** "She has a preference for classical music."
- **Paraphrase:** "She prefers classical music."

**Adjective → Noun:**
- **Original:** "The project was successful."
- **Paraphrase:** "The project was a success."

---

**5. Sentence Combining atau Splitting**

Menggabungkan dua kalimat pendek jadi satu kalimat panjang, atau sebaliknya.

**Splitting:**
- **Original:** "The students, who were tired after the exam, went home early."
- **Paraphrase:** "The students were tired after the exam. They went home early."

**Combining:**
- **Original:** "He is intelligent. He is also hardworking."
- **Paraphrase:** "He is both intelligent and hardworking."

---

**Contoh Kombinasi Structure Change:**

**Original:**
"Many researchers believe that climate change will have serious consequences."

**Paraphrase (beberapa variasi):**

1. **Passive:** "It is believed by many researchers that serious consequences will result from climate change."
2. **Noun phrase:** "The belief among many researchers is that climate change will lead to serious consequences."
3. **Negative form:** "Many researchers do not doubt that climate change will have serious consequences."
4. **Split:** "Climate change will have serious consequences. This is what many researchers believe."

---

**Jebakan dalam Structure Change:**

❌ **Jebakan 1: Mengubah Struktur tapi Makna Berubah**

**Original:** "Few students passed the test."
**Salah:** "Many students passed the test." (Kebalik!)
**Benar:** "Not many students passed the test."

"Few" itu artinya **sedikit/tidak banyak**, jadi kalau mau paraphrase harus tetap negative!

❌ **Jebakan 2: Salah Logic dengan Conjunction**

**Original:** "Although he was sick, he went to school."
**Salah:** "He was sick, so he went to school." (Logic-nya jadi aneh!)
**Benar:** "Despite being sick, he went to school."

"Although" itu contrast, jadi pakai "so" (cause-effect) jadi salah logic!

---

**Tips Pro:**

💡 **Perhatikan Meaning Tetap Sama**
Seberapa pun kamu ubah strukturnya, **inti maknanya harus identik**. Kalau ada perubahan meaning, itu bukan paraphrase yang benar!

💡 **Natural is Key**
Paraphrase yang bagus itu yang kedengeran **natural**, nggak kayak robot atau Google Translate. Jadi latih feeling bahasa Inggris kamu!

💡 **Subject-Verb Agreement Tetap Harus Benar**
Kalau ngubah struktur, pastikan grammar tetap correct! Singular subject tetep pakai singular verb, dst.

### **Materi 3.3.4: Analisis Complex Paraphrase**

Nah, sekarang kita masuk level **ADVANCED**! Complex paraphrase adalah gabungan dari **synonym substitution + structure change** sekaligus. Ini yang paling sering muncul di SNBT dan yang paling challenging!

---

**Apa itu Complex Paraphrase?**

Complex paraphrase adalah parafrase yang melibatkan **banyak perubahan sekaligus**:
- Ganti kata dengan sinonim ✅
- Ubah struktur kalimat ✅
- Ubah word class (verb jadi noun, dst) ✅
- Kadang juga condensing (singkat) atau expanding (detail) ✅

Hasil akhirnya: kalimat yang **totally different** dari aslinya tapi maknanya **100% sama**!

---

**Contoh Complex Paraphrase:**

**Original:**
"Studies have shown that regular physical exercise significantly improves both physical and mental health."

**Complex Paraphrase:**
"Research indicates that consistent engagement in physical activity leads to substantial enhancements in bodily and psychological well-being."

**Analisis perubahan:**
- "Studies have shown" → "Research indicates" (synonym)
- "regular physical exercise" → "consistent engagement in physical activity" (synonym + expansion)
- "significantly improves" → "leads to substantial enhancements" (synonym + structure: verb → noun)
- "both physical and mental health" → "bodily and psychological well-being" (synonym)

Total makeover, tapi **maknanya identik**!

---

**Teknik Condensing (Memadatkan Informasi):**

Kadang informasi panjang dijadikan lebih ringkas tanpa kehilangan makna inti.

**Original (panjang):**
"The main reason why students fail exams is because they do not prepare adequately and they lack proper time management skills."

**Condensed Paraphrase:**
"Exam failure among students primarily stems from inadequate preparation and poor time management."

**Apa yang terjadi:**
- Dipotong yang nggak esensial ("the main reason why... is because")
- Digabung jadi satu kalimat lebih compact
- Makna inti tetap terjaga!

---

**Teknik Expanding (Menjelaskan Lebih Detail):**

Kebalikan dari condensing—informasi ringkas dijelaskan lebih elaborate.

**Original (ringkas):**
"Air pollution causes health problems."

**Expanded Paraphrase:**
"The presence of harmful substances in the atmosphere leads to various adverse effects on human health, including respiratory diseases and cardiovascular complications."

**Apa yang terjadi:**
- "Air pollution" diperjelas jadi "harmful substances in the atmosphere"
- "health problems" diperjelas jadi "adverse effects... respiratory diseases and cardiovascular complications"
- Lebih detail tapi makna dasarnya tetap sama!

---

**Maintaining All Key Information:**

Ini **PENTING BANGET**! Dalam complex paraphrase, kamu boleh ubah struktur dan kata sesuka hati, tapi **semua elemen penting harus tetap ada**!

**Contoh:**

**Original:**
"The study involved 500 participants aged 18-25 from urban areas."

**Paraphrase yang SALAH:**
"Research included 500 people from cities." ❌
→ Kenapa salah? **Hilang info umur (18-25)**!

**Paraphrase yang BENAR:**
"The research comprised 500 young adults, aged between 18 and 25, residing in metropolitan regions." ✅
→ Semua info ada: jumlah (500), umur (18-25), lokasi (urban/metropolitan)!

---

**Different Perspective (Ubah Sudut Pandang):**

Kadang paraphrase mengubah perspektif tapi informasinya tetap sama.

**Original (fokus pada action):**
"The company increased its profits by 20%."

**Paraphrase (fokus pada result):**
"A 20% rise in profits was achieved by the company."

**Original (fokus pada people):**
"Many people prefer online shopping."

**Paraphrase (fokus pada trend):**
"Online shopping has become increasingly popular."

Sudut pandangnya beda, tapi **fakta yang disampaikan sama**!

---

**Contoh Complex Paraphrase Multi-Layer:**

**Original:**
"Research suggests that children who read regularly tend to perform better academically than those who don't."

**Complex Paraphrase:**
"Academic success is more commonly observed among young learners who engage in frequent reading habits compared to their peers who lack such practices."

**Breakdown perubahan:**

| Original | Paraphrase | Jenis Perubahan |
|----------|------------|-----------------|
| Research suggests | is observed | Synonym + Passive |
| children | young learners | Synonym |
| who read regularly | who engage in frequent reading habits | Expansion + Synonym |
| tend to perform better academically | Academic success is more commonly | Structure change (verb → noun) + Passive |
| than those who don't | compared to their peers who lack such practices | Synonym + Expansion |

Liat nggak? Ini udah kombinasi BANYAK teknik sekaligus! 🔥

---

**Jebakan dalam Complex Paraphrase:**

❌ **Jebakan 1: Terlalu Banyak Perubahan Sampai Makna Bergeser**

Kalau terlalu asyik ngubah-ubah, kadang makna bisa **slightly berbeda** dari aslinya!

**Original:** "The majority of students support the new policy."
**Salah:** "All students are in favor of the recent regulation." ❌
→ "Majority" (mayoritas) ≠ "All" (semua)!

❌ **Jebakan 2: Kehilangan Detail Penting**

Karena fokus paraphrase, kadang ada detail yang ke-skip!

**Original:** "The experiment was conducted in controlled laboratory conditions at 25°C."
**Salah:** "The experiment took place in a lab." ❌
→ Kehilangan "controlled" dan "25°C"—itu detail penting!

❌ **Jebakan 3: Menambahkan Informasi yang Nggak Ada**

Paraphrase boleh expand, tapi **jangan ngarang**!

**Original:** "The book was published in 2020."
**Salah:** "The bestselling book was published in 2020." ❌
→ "Bestselling" nggak ada di original!

---

**Tips Pro untuk Complex Paraphrase:**

💡 **Break It Down, Then Rebuild**
Langkah-langkah:
1. **Identify** semua key information points
2. **List** synonym yang mungkin untuk tiap kata kunci
3. **Choose** struktur kalimat baru yang berbeda
4. **Rebuild** dengan kombinasi synonym + structure change
5. **Verify** bahwa semua info penting masih ada dan makna tetap sama

💡 **Practice Makes Perfect**
Latihan paraphrase sendiri! Ambil kalimat dari artikel, coba paraphrase dengan banyak variasi. Makin sering latihan, makin natural skill ini!

💡 **Read the Paraphrase Aloud**
Kalau kedengeran aneh atau awkward, berarti paraphrase-nya kurang bagus. Good paraphrase itu harus **natural** dan **smooth**!

### **Materi 3.3.5: Analisis Jebakan dalam Soal Restatement**

Okay, now we're talking about **JEBAKAN MAUT** yang sering bikin kita salah pilih jawaban! Pembuat soal SNBT itu **expert** dalam bikin opsi yang keliatan benar tapi sebenarnya salah. Let's expose their tricks! 🕵️

---

**Jebakan #1: Sama Kata tapi Beda Makna (Word Trap)**

Ini jebakan paling **licik**! Pembuat soal pakai kata yang **persis sama** dari teks, tapi konteks atau maknanya **diubah**. Banyak siswa langsung tergoda karena "oh ini ada kata yang sama nih!"

**Contoh:**

**Teks:**
"The study was **significant** because it involved a large sample size."

**Opsi Jebakan:**
"A **significant** number of participants were involved in the study." ❌

**Analisis kenapa SALAH:**
- Kata "significant" memang muncul di both places
- TAPI di teks: "significant" = penting/bermakna (mengacu pada studi)
- Di opsi: "significant" = besar/banyak (mengacu pada jumlah)
- **Konteks berbeda, makna berbeda!**

**Opsi yang BENAR:**
"The importance of the study lay in its large sample size." ✅

**How to avoid:**
✅ Jangan langsung excited kalau liat kata yang sama!
✅ Selalu **check konteks**—apa kata itu dipakai dengan makna yang sama?
✅ Fokus pada **meaning, not matching**!

---

**Jebakan #2: Beda Kata tapi Beda Makna Juga (False Paraphrase)**

Ini kebalikannya—pembuat soal pakai kata yang berbeda (terlihat kayak paraphrase), tapi kalau dicek lagi, **maknanya nggak sama**!

**Contoh:**

**Teks:**
"Temperature affects the rate of chemical reactions."

**Opsi Jebakan:**
"Chemical reactions determine temperature levels." ❌

**Analisis kenapa SALAH:**
- Kelihatan kayak paraphrase: "affects" → "determine"
- TAPI **direction-nya kebalik**!
- Teks: Temperature → mempengaruhi → Reactions
- Opsi: Reactions → menentukan → Temperature
- **Cause-effect relationship-nya reversed!**

**Opsi yang BENAR:**
"The speed of chemical reactions is influenced by temperature." ✅

**How to avoid:**
✅ **Check logical relationship**: who does what to whom?
✅ Perhatikan **cause-effect direction**—jangan sampai kebalik!
✅ Verify: apakah subject dan object tetap sesuai?

---

**Jebakan #3: Informasi Tidak Lengkap (Partial Information)**

Opsi cuma ngambil **sebagian** informasi dari teks, nggak semuanya. Ini jebakan favorit karena technically nggak salah, tapi **incomplete**!

**Contoh:**

**Teks:**
"Students who study regularly and get enough sleep tend to perform better academically."

**Opsi Jebakan:**
"Regular study habits lead to academic success." ❌

**Analisis kenapa SALAH:**
- Info tentang "study regularly" ✅ ada
- Info tentang "get enough sleep" ❌ HILANG!
- Padahal di teks disebutkan **DUA faktor**: study + sleep
- Opsi cuma mention satu faktor = **incomplete**!

**Opsi yang BENAR:**
"Academic performance improves with consistent studying and adequate rest." ✅

**How to avoid:**
✅ **Count the key points** di original sentence
✅ Make sure **ALL points** ada di paraphrase
✅ Kalau ada yang hilang, bukan restatement yang lengkap!

---

**Jebakan #4: Menambahkan Informasi Baru (Added Information)**

Kebalikan dari #3—opsi **nambah** informasi yang sebenarnya nggak ada di teks!

**Contoh:**

**Teks:**
"The book was published in 2020."

**Opsi Jebakan:**
"The bestselling book was released in 2020." ❌

**Analisis kenapa SALAH:**
- "Published in 2020" ✅ benar
- TAPI "bestselling" ❌ nggak ada di teks!
- Ini **added information** yang nggak bisa di-verify dari original

**Opsi yang BENAR:**
"The book came out in 2020." ✅

**How to avoid:**
✅ **Jangan assume** informasi yang nggak explicitly stated!
✅ Kalau ada added detail—red flag! 🚩
✅ Stick to **only what's mentioned** di original

---

**Jebakan #5: Distorsi Makna (Twisted Meaning)**

Ini yang paling subtle! Opsi pakai banyak kata dari teks, struktur juga mirip, tapi ada **twist kecil** yang mengubah makna.

**Contoh:**

**Teks:**
"Most experts agree that the policy will be beneficial."

**Opsi Jebakan:**
"Experts are certain that the policy will bring benefits." ❌

**Analisis kenapa SALAH:**
- "Most experts" ≠ "Experts" (generalisasi berlebihan!)
- "Agree" ≠ "are certain" (tingkat keyakinan berbeda!)
- "Most... agree" = mayoritas setuju (masih ada yang nggak setuju)
- "Experts are certain" = semua ahli yakin (100% confident)
- **Slight twist, big difference!**

**Opsi yang BENAR:**
"The majority of specialists believe the policy will have positive effects." ✅

**How to avoid:**
✅ Perhatikan **quantifiers**: most ≠ all, some ≠ many
✅ Perhatikan **degree words**: agree ≠ certain, might ≠ will
✅ **Precision matters**—even small changes can distort meaning!

---

**Jebakan #6: Terlalu Umum atau Terlalu Spesifik (Scope Error)**

Opsi yang **too broad** (terlalu luas) atau **too narrow** (terlalu sempit) dibanding original statement.

**Contoh Too Broad:**

**Teks:**
"Regular exercise improves cardiovascular health."

**Opsi Jebakan:**
"Physical activity is beneficial for health." ❌

**Kenapa salah:**
- Original: spesifik tentang cardiovascular health
- Opsi: terlalu general—"health" itu luas banget!
- **Scope terlalu melebar!**

**Contoh Too Narrow:**

**Teks:**
"Social media affects people's mental health."

**Opsi Jebakan:**
"Instagram impacts teenagers' anxiety levels." ❌

**Kenapa salah:**
- Original: semua social media, semua orang, semua aspek mental health
- Opsi: cuma Instagram, cuma teenagers, cuma anxiety
- **Scope terlalu menyempit!**

**How to avoid:**
✅ Match the **level of generality**
✅ Jangan generalisasi yang spesifik, jangan spesifikkan yang general
✅ Keep the same **scope and range**!

---

**Jebakan #7: Salah Logical Connector (Wrong Relationship)**

Mengubah **hubungan logis** antar ide—misalnya cause-effect jadi contrast, atau sebaliknya.

**Contoh:**

**Teks:**
"Although the project was challenging, the team completed it successfully."

**Opsi Jebakan:**
"The project was challenging, so the team completed it successfully." ❌

**Analisis kenapa SALAH:**
- Original: "Although" (contrast) → challenging TAPI berhasil
- Opsi: "so" (cause-effect) → challenging KARENA ITU berhasil
- **Logic relationship-nya totally different!**

**Opsi yang BENAR:**
"Despite the project's difficulty, the team managed to finish it." ✅

**How to avoid:**
✅ Perhatikan **transition words**: although, however, because, so, therefore
✅ Pastikan **logical relationship** tetap sama
✅ Contrast tetap contrast, cause-effect tetap cause-effect!

---

**Jebakan #8: Mengubah Certainty Level (Modality Shift)**

Mengubah tingkat **kepastian** atau **possibility**—misalnya dari "might" jadi "will", atau "some" jadi "all".

**Contoh:**

**Teks:**
"The vaccine may reduce the risk of infection."

**Opsi Jebakan:**
"The vaccine will prevent infection." ❌

**Analisis kenapa SALAH:**
- Original: "may reduce risk" (kemungkinan, nggak pasti, cuman mengurangi risiko)
- Opsi: "will prevent" (pasti, 100%, mencegah total)
- **Certainty level-nya beda banget!**

**Opsi yang BENAR:**
"The vaccine could potentially lower the likelihood of getting infected." ✅

**How to avoid:**
✅ Perhatikan **modal verbs**: may, might, could, should, will, must
✅ Perhatikan **hedging language**: possibly, likely, probably, certainly
✅ Match the **degree of certainty**!

**Strategy Ultimate untuk Menghindari Semua Jebakan:**

**Step 1: Identifikasi Key Information** 🔍
- Subjek siapa/apa?
- Verb/action apa?
- Objek apa?
- Ada details tambahan? (numbers, qualifiers, conditions)

**Step 2: Check SETIAP Elemen** ✅
Untuk setiap opsi, verify:
- Subjek sama? ✓
- Action/verb sama? ✓
- Objek sama? ✓
- Details lengkap? ✓
- Logical relationship sama? ✓
- Certainty level sama? ✓

**Step 3: Eliminate yang Jelas Salah** ❌
Buang opsi yang:
- Ada added info yang nggak di-mention
- Kehilangan key information
- Scope terlalu luas/sempit
- Logical relationship berbeda

**Step 4: Compare Remaining Options** 🆚
Kalau masih ada 2-3 opsi yang mirip benar, compare detail by detail. Yang paling **complete, accurate, dan precise** adalah jawaban yang benar!

**Step 5: Read in Context** 📖
Baca opsi yang kamu pilih dalam konteks soal. Does it make sense? Does it accurately represent the original? Kalau ada yang aneh, mungkin bukan jawaban yang tepat!

**Quick Checklist Before Selecting an Answer:**

✅ Semua key points dari original ada?  
✅ Nggak ada added information?  
✅ Logical relationship tetap sama?  
✅ Scope/generality level sama?  
✅ Certainty/modality level sama?  
✅ Kedengeran natural dan make sense?  

Kalau semua ✅, most likely itu jawaban yang benar! 💯
