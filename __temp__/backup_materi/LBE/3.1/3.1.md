# SECTION 3: KOSAKATA DAN REFERENSI

## TOPIK 3.1: SYNONYM (PADANAN KATA)

---

### **Materi 3.1.1: Pengenalan Synonym dalam Konteks**

#### **Apa Itu Synonym dan Mengapa Penting?**

Bayangin kamu lagi ngobrol sama teman, terus dia bilang "Wah, filmnya **brilliant** banget!" Nah, kamu langsung ngerti kan maksudnya? Kira-kira sama dengan "amazing", "excellent", atau "outstanding". Nah, itulah synonym—kata-kata yang punya makna mirip atau hampir sama.

Tapi tunggu dulu! Di dunia SNBT, synonym nggak sesederhana itu. Kenapa? Karena **konteks is the king!** 

Coba lihat kata "**light**" ini:
- *"The room was filled with **light**."* → cahaya
- *"This bag is very **light**."* → ringan
- *"Can you **light** the candle?"* → menyalakan

Tuh kan? Satu kata bisa punya banyak arti tergantung di mana dan bagaimana dia dipakai. Makanya, dalam soal SNBT, kamu **WAJIB** baca konteks kalimatnya, bukan cuma apal arti kata dari kamus doang.

---

#### **Polysemy: Satu Kata, Sejuta Makna**

Ini dia musuh bebuyutan peserta SNBT: **polysemy**—fenomena di mana satu kata punya beberapa arti berbeda. Kata-kata kayak gini sering banget jadi jebakan empuk di soal synonym.

**Contoh klasik:**

**"Run"**
- *"I **run** every morning."* → berlari
- *"She **runs** a successful business."* → mengelola
- *"The program **runs** smoothly."* → berjalan/beroperasi
- *"The paint started to **run** down the wall."* → meleleh

Nah, kalau soalnya nanya "The word 'run' in line 5 is closest in meaning to...", kamu HARUS baca kalimat line 5 itu dulu. Jangan langsung jawab "sprint" atau "jog" kalau ternyata konteksnya tentang bisnis!

---

#### **Context is Everything!**

Ini mantra yang harus kamu hafal: **"Arti sebuah kata 100% tergantung konteksnya."**

Bayangkan kamu dapat soal kayak gini:

> *"The company decided to **address** the environmental concerns raised by local communities."*

Nah, "address" di sini artinya apa?
- **BUKAN** "alamat" ❌
- **BUKAN** "menyapa" ❌
- **TAPI** "menangani/menghadapi" ✅

Kalau kamu cuma apal "address = alamat", langsung salah deh!

**Tips Jitu:**
1. **Baca kalimat lengkap** yang mengandung kata tersebut
2. **Baca kalimat sebelum dan sesudahnya** untuk konteks lebih luas
3. **Pahami topik paragrafnya** secara umum
4. **Perhatikan kata-kata di sekitarnya** (collocation)

---

#### **Jebakan Umum dalam Soal Synonym SNBT**

**Jebakan #1: Arti Paling Familiar Bukan Selalu Benar**

Contoh kata "**present**":
- Arti familiar: hadiah 🎁
- Tapi bisa juga: sekarang (the present moment), menghadirkan (present a paper), hadir (be present)

Kalau soalnya: *"The researcher will **present** his findings next week."*
Synonym-nya bukan "gift" dong, tapi "show" atau "demonstrate"!

**Jebakan #2: Kata yang Kelihatan Mirip**

Ini nih yang sering ngejebak:
- **Effect** (hasil) ≠ **Affect** (mempengaruhi)
- **Compliment** (pujian) ≠ **Complement** (pelengkap)
- **Principal** (kepala sekolah/utama) ≠ **Principle** (prinsip/aturan)

Satu huruf beda, artinya beda jauh!

**Jebakan #3: Register/Tingkat Formalitas**

Kadang ada beberapa kata yang artinya mirip tapi tingkat formalitasnya beda:
- **Start** (informal) = **Commence** (formal)
- **Buy** (informal) = **Purchase** (formal)
- **Help** (informal) = **Assist** (formal)

Dalam teks akademik SNBT, biasanya yang dipilih adalah yang formal. Jadi kalau ada pilihan "commence" vs "start", dan teksnya formal, pilih yang "commence".

---

#### **Part of Speech Matters!**

Ini penting banget: **synonym yang kamu pilih HARUS sama part of speech-nya dengan kata asli.**

Misalnya kata "**significant**" (adjective):
- ✅ **Important** (adjective) → BENAR
- ❌ **Significance** (noun) → SALAH, beda kelas kata
- ❌ **Significantly** (adverb) → SALAH, beda kelas kata

Quick reference:
- **Noun** (kata benda): person, idea, concept, development
- **Verb** (kata kerja): develop, increase, demonstrate, analyze
- **Adjective** (kata sifat): important, significant, major, critical
- **Adverb** (kata keterangan): importantly, significantly, majorly, critically

---

#### **Variasi Pertanyaan Synonym dalam SNBT**

Kenali berbagai bentuk pertanyaan biar nggak kaget waktu ujian:

1. *"The word '...' in line X is **closest in meaning** to..."*
2. *"The word '...' could be **best replaced** by..."*
3. *"Which of the following has a **similar meaning** with '...'?"*
4. *"The word '...' in paragraph 2 is **synonymous** with..."*
5. *"What is the meaning of '...' as used in line X?"*

Semua pertanyaan di atas pada dasarnya nanya hal yang sama: **synonym!**

---

#### **Strategy Checklist untuk Synonym Questions**

✅ **Langkah 1:** Baca kalimat yang mengandung kata tersebut secara lengkap
✅ **Langkah 2:** Baca kalimat sebelum dan sesudahnya untuk konteks
✅ **Langkah 3:** Identifikasi part of speech kata tersebut (noun/verb/adj/adv)
✅ **Langkah 4:** Coba ganti kata tersebut dengan opsi jawaban satu per satu
✅ **Langkah 5:** Pilih yang paling pas secara makna DAN gramatikal

**Pro Tip:** Kalau kamu nggak familiar dengan kata yang ditanyakan, **JANGAN PANIK!** Fokus ke konteks kalimatnya. Kadang kamu bisa nebak arti dari situation atau topic yang dibahas.

---

#### **Hubungan Synonym dengan Topik Lain**

Synonym ini ibarat fondasi untuk memahami materi lain:

🔗 **Topik 3.3 (Paraphrase):** Paraphrase itu pada dasarnya menggunakan synonym + perubahan struktur kalimat. Kalau kamu jago synonym, paraphrase jadi lebih gampang!

🔗 **Topik 1.3 (Detail Information):** Sering banget informasi di opsi jawaban di-paraphrase dengan synonym. Jadi kalau kamu nggak ngerti synonymnya, bisa miss jawabannya.

🔗 **Topik 3.1.4 (Academic Vocabulary):** Banyak synonym yang dipilih berdasarkan register formal/informal. Ini juga dibahas lebih dalam di Materi 3.1.5.

---

#### **Fun Fact: Kenapa Bahasa Inggris Punya Banyak Synonym?**

Tahukah kamu kalau bahasa Inggris itu "rakus"? Dia suka banget nyerap kata dari bahasa lain! Makanya banyak banget kata dengan arti mirip tapi asal-usul berbeda:

- **Help** (dari Old English) = **Assist** (dari Latin) = **Aid** (dari Old French)
- **Ask** (dari Old English) = **Inquire** (dari Latin) = **Question** (dari Latin)

Biasanya yang dari Old English lebih informal/casual, yang dari Latin/French lebih formal/academic. Makanya dalam teks SNBT yang akademis, sering muncul kata-kata Latin-based.

---

**Ingat:** Synonym bukan cuma tentang hafalan! Ini tentang **pemahaman konteks**, **kepekaan terhadap nuansa makna**, dan **kemampuan berpikir fleksibel**. Dengan latihan yang cukup, kamu bakal bisa "feeling" mana synonym yang tepat bahkan untuk kata yang baru pertama kali kamu lihat.

### **Materi 3.1.2: Analisis Synonym dari Context Clues**

#### **Context Clues: Senjata Rahasiamu!**

Pernah nggak sih kamu baca teks bahasa Inggris, terus ketemu kata yang nggak kamu tau artinya, tapi entah kenapa kamu bisa "nebak" artinya? Nah, itu namanya kamu lagi pakai **context clues** tanpa sadar!

Context clues itu kayak petunjuk tersembunyi dalam teks yang membantu kamu memahami arti kata yang nggak familiar. Ini skill SUPER PENTING buat SNBT karena:

1. **Hemat waktu** – nggak perlu mikir keras atau ragu-ragu
2. **Nggak perlu kamus** – teksnya sendiri yang kasih clue
3. **Lebih akurat** – arti sesuai konteks spesifik, bukan arti umum

Mari kita bedah satu per satu jenis context clues yang paling sering muncul!

---

#### **1. DEFINITION CLUES (Petunjuk Definisi)**

Ini yang paling gampang! Penulis langsung kasih definisi atau penjelasan dari kata yang mungkin asing.

**Penanda khas:**
- **is/are** → *"Photosynthesis **is** the process by which plants convert sunlight into energy."*
- **means** → *"Resilient **means** able to recover quickly from difficulties."*
- **refers to** → *"Deforestation **refers to** the clearing of forests on a massive scale."*
- **is defined as** → *"Altruism **is defined as** selfless concern for others."*
- **in other words** → *"The experiment failed, **in other words**, it didn't work."*
- **that is** → *"The fauna, **that is**, the animal life of the region, is diverse."*

**Contoh dalam teks:**

> *"The scientists observed bioluminescence, which **is** the emission of light by living organisms, in the deep ocean."*

Nah, kalau kamu nggak tau "bioluminescence", langsung dikasih tau kan: "the emission of light by living organisms"!

**Trik praktis:**
Kalau ketemu kata sulit diikuti sama tanda koma + "which is/are", "or", "that is", atau dash (—), biasanya itu definition clue! Baca terus aja, pasti dikasih penjelasan.

---

#### **2. EXAMPLE CLUES (Petunjuk Contoh)**

Kadang penulis nggak kasih definisi langsung, tapi kasih contoh-contoh yang bisa bikin kamu paham.

**Penanda khas:**
- **such as** → *"Citrus fruits **such as** oranges, lemons, and grapefruits..."*
- **for example** → *"Renewable energy sources, **for example**, solar and wind power..."*
- **for instance** → *"Various artifacts, **for instance**, pottery and tools, were found."*
- **like** → *"Predators **like** lions, tigers, and wolves..."*
- **including** → *"Many factors, **including** age, diet, and lifestyle..."*

**Contoh dalam teks:**

> *"The patient exhibited severe symptoms of the disease, **such as** high fever, fatigue, and difficulty breathing."*

Dari contoh-contoh gejala (high fever, fatigue, difficulty breathing), kamu bisa simpulkan "exhibited" berarti "menunjukkan" dan "symptoms" berarti "gejala".

**Contoh lagi:**

> *"The garden was filled with fragrant flowers, **like** roses, jasmine, and lavender."*

Meskipun kamu nggak tau "fragrant", dari contohnya (roses, jasmine, lavender) yang notabene bunga-bunga yang wangi, kamu bisa simpulkan "fragrant" berarti "harum/wangi".

**Pro Tip:** Kalau ada kata sulit + "such as/for example/like" + daftar contoh konkret, analyze contoh-contohnya! Cari kesamaan karakteristik dari contoh-contoh tersebut untuk deduce arti kata sulit tadi.

---

#### **3. CONTRAST CLUES (Petunjuk Kontras)**

Ini agak tricky tapi powerful! Kalau ada kata yang kontras/berlawanan dengan kata yang kita tau, kita bisa inference artinya.

**Penanda khas:**
- **but** → *"She's usually shy, **but** today she was quite gregarious."*
- **however** → *"The first attempt failed; **however**, the second was successful."*
- **although/though** → *"**Although** he was affluent, he lived modestly."*
- **unlike** → *"**Unlike** her meticulous sister, Jane is rather careless."*
- **on the other hand** → *"Some prefer urban life; **on the other hand**, others love rural settings."*
- **whereas** → *"**Whereas** the north is arid, the south is quite humid."*
- **instead of** → *"**Instead of** being lethargic, the team was energetic."*
- **rather than** → *"He chose to collaborate **rather than** compete."*

**Contoh analisis:**

> *"**Unlike** his gregarious brother who loves parties, Tom is quite introverted."*

Nah, "gregarious" di-contrast-kan dengan "introverted" (introvert/pemalu). Jadi bisa disimpulkan "gregarious" itu kebalikannya = ekstrovert/suka bergaul!

**Contoh lagi:**

> *"The first solution was complicated and time-consuming. **In contrast**, the new method is remarkably **facile**."*

"Facile" di-kontras-kan dengan "complicated" (rumit). Jadi "facile" artinya = simple/mudah!

**Hati-hati:** Jangan cuma fokus sama contrast word-nya. Perhatikan juga APA yang di-contrast-kan. Kadang yang kontras itu bukan kata yang ditanyakan, tapi kata lain dalam kalimat.

---

#### **4. RESTATEMENT CLUES (Petunjuk Pengulangan)**

Penulis ngulang ide yang sama tapi dengan kata berbeda. Biasanya ada penanda kayak "or", "that is", "in other words".

**Penanda khas:**
- **or** → *"The physician, **or** doctor, will see you now."*
- **that is** → *"She's an ornithologist, **that is**, a bird expert."*
- **in other words** → *"He's parsimonious, **in other words**, very stingy with money."*
- **i.e.** → *"The lab uses H₂O, **i.e.**, water, in the experiment."*

**Contoh dalam teks:**

> *"The archipelago, **or** group of islands, stretches for hundreds of miles."*

"Archipelago" langsung dijelaskan dengan "group of islands"!

**Contoh lagi:**

> *"The CEO decided to divest, **that is**, sell off some company assets."*

"Divest" dijelaskan ulang dengan "sell off some company assets".

**Bonus trick:** Kadang restatement pakai tanda baca comma (,) atau dash (—) tanpa kata penanda:

> *"The teacher emphasized the importance of tenacity **—** persistence in pursuing goals."*

---

#### **5. INFERENCE FROM GENERAL CONTEXT**

Ini yang paling challenging tapi juga paling sering dipake! Kadang nggak ada clue eksplisit, tapi dari **keseluruhan situasi, topik, atau tone** kamu bisa nebak artinya.

**Contoh 1: Dari situasi**

> *"After hiking for six hours without water under the scorching sun, they were completely **parched**."*

Dari situasinya (hiking lama, no water, hot sun), logikanya mereka kehausan/haus banget. Jadi "parched" = sangat kehausan.

**Contoh 2: Dari topik/subject matter**

> *"In marine biology, researchers study the **fauna** of coral reefs, including colorful fish and invertebrates."*

Konteksnya tentang marine biology + coral reefs + contohnya fish dan invertebrates. Jadi "fauna" pasti tentang animal life.

**Contoh 3: Dari tone**

> *"Despite facing numerous obstacles, she remained **undaunted** and continued her research."*

Tone-nya positif (she continued despite obstacles). Jadi "undaunted" pasti something positive = tidak gentar/tidak menyerah.

**Strategy:**
1. Identifikasi **topik umum** paragraf (science? economy? social issue?)
2. Perhatikan **situasi atau scenario** yang dideskripsikan
3. Cari **logical relationship** dengan informasi sekitar
4. Gunakan **common sense** dan **background knowledge** kamu

---

#### **Combining Multiple Clues**

Yang paling powerful adalah **kombinasi beberapa clues sekaligus**!

**Contoh kombinasi:**

> *"The manager's decision to terminate the project was **laudable**; **in other words**, it was praiseworthy. **Unlike** previous managers who wasted resources, she made a smart choice."*

Banyak clue di sini:
- **Restatement:** "in other words" + "praiseworthy"
- **Contrast:** "Unlike previous managers who wasted resources" vs "smart choice"
- **General context:** tone positif tentang decision

Triple clue = super clear bahwa "laudable" = pantas dipuji!

---

#### **Common Pitfalls (Jebakan Umum)**

**Pitfall #1: Mengabaikan Konteks Lokal**

Jangan cuma fokus ke kata yang ditanyakan. Baca minimal 1 kalimat penuh sebelum dan sesudahnya.

❌ **SALAH:** Baca cuma kalimat yang ada kata "X", langsung tebak
✅ **BENAR:** Baca 3 kalimat (sebelum, yang mengandung X, sesudah) untuk full context

**Pitfall #2: Terjebak dengan Arti yang Familiar**

Kata seperti "address", "present", "bank", "bear" punya multiple meanings. Jangan auto-pilot ke arti yang paling kamu kenal!

**Pitfall #3: Overthinking**

Kadang clue-nya jelas, tapi kamu mikir "ah masa sih segampang ini?" Percaya sama clue yang ada! SNBT nggak selalu jebakan kok.

---

#### **Practice Strategy: The "Cover and Guess" Method**

Cara latihan yang efektif:

1. **Cover** kata yang ditanyakan dengan jari/kertas
2. Baca kalimat lengkapnya tanpa kata itu
3. **Guess** kata apa yang kira-kira cocok di posisi itu
4. **Reveal** kata aslinya
5. Compare apakah tebakan kamu close dengan arti sebenarnya

Contoh:
> *"The discovery was ________, attracting worldwide attention from scientists."*

Guess: "significant" atau "important" atau "groundbreaking"
Reveal: "remarkable"

See? Tebakan kamu close kan? That's the power of context!

---

#### **Level Up Your Context Clue Skills**

**Beginner Level:** Identify definition dan example clues → paling obvious
**Intermediate Level:** Recognize contrast dan restatement clues → butuh sedikit analysis
**Advanced Level:** Inference from general context → butuh critical thinking

Practice makes perfect! Semakin sering kamu latihan recognize context clues, semakin natural skillnya.

---

**Key Takeaway:**
Context clues itu kayak treasure map yang udah disediain sama penulis. Tugas kamu cuma jeli membaca petunjuknya. **Don't memorize, ANALYZE!**

### **Materi 3.1.3: Analisis Multiple Meanings**

#### **The Tricky World of Multiple Meanings**

Okay, sekarang kita masuk ke zona bahaya: **kata-kata yang punya lebih dari satu arti!** Ini adalah jebakan favorit pembuat soal SNBT karena bisa bikin peserta yang nggak hati-hati langsung salah.

Bayangin kamu lihat kata "**bank**" dalam soal. Langsung kepikiran bank tempat nabung kan? Padahal bisa jadi konteksnya:
- *"They sat on the **bank** of the river."* → tepi sungai
- *"The pilot had to **bank** the plane to the left."* → memiringkan
- *"We **bank** on your support."* → mengandalkan

Tuh kan? Satu kata, banyak banget kemungkinan artinya!

---

#### **Kata-Kata dengan Multiple Meanings yang Sering Muncul**

Mari kita bedah kata-kata yang paling sering jadi jebakan:

---

**1. RUN** (Juaranya Multiple Meanings!)

Ini kata yang punya 600+ arti di kamus! (No kidding!) Tapi yang sering di SNBT:

📌 **Run = berlari (motion)**
> *"She **runs** five kilometers every morning."*

📌 **Run = mengelola/menjalankan (manage/operate)**
> *"He **runs** a successful restaurant."*
> *"Who **runs** this organization?"*

📌 **Run = berjalan/beroperasi (function)**
> *"The machine **runs** smoothly."*
> *"The program **runs** on Windows."*

📌 **Run = mengalir (flow)**
> *"Tears **ran** down her cheeks."*
> *"The river **runs** through the city."*

📌 **Run = meleleh (melt/spread)**
> *"The paint **ran** down the wall."*
> *"Don't let the colors **run** in the wash."*

📌 **Run = berlangsung (last/continue)**
> *"The show **runs** for two hours."*
> *"The contract **runs** until December."*

**Cara bedain:** Lihat subject dan object-nya!
- Subject = person → probably berlari atau mengelola
- Subject = thing/program → probably beroperasi
- Subject = liquid/color → probably mengalir/meleleh

---

**2. SET** (Close Second dengan 400+ arti!)

📌 **Set = menempatkan/meletakkan (put/place)**
> *"She **set** the book on the table."*

📌 **Set = menetapkan (establish/determine)**
> *"The government **set** new regulations."*
> *"Let's **set** a date for the meeting."*

📌 **Set = mengatur (adjust)**
> *"**Set** your alarm for 6 AM."*

📌 **Set = terbenam (go down – untuk matahari)**
> *"The sun **sets** at 6 PM."*

📌 **Set = kumpulan/satu set (group of similar items)**
> *"A **set** of tools"*
> *"A **set** of dishes"*

📌 **Set = siap (ready)**
> *"Are you all **set**?"*

**Trik:** Perhatikan preposition setelah "set"!
- Set **on** → put on a surface
- Set **up** → establish/organize
- Set **out** → begin a journey

---

**3. LIGHT**

📌 **Light = cahaya (brightness)**
> *"Turn on the **light**."*
> *"The room is filled with natural **light**."*

📌 **Light = ringan (not heavy)**
> *"This suitcase is very **light**."*
> *"She prefers **light** meals."*

📌 **Light = menyalakan (ignite)**
> *"Can you **light** the candles?"*

📌 **Light = cerah/terang (pale)**
> *"She wore a **light** blue dress."*

📌 **Light = enteng/tidak serius (not serious)**
> *"Let's keep the conversation **light**."*
> *"It's just **light** reading."*

**Cara bedain:** Check part of speech!
- Noun → cahaya atau sesuatu yang menghasilkan cahaya
- Adjective → ringan, cerah, atau tidak serius
- Verb → menyalakan

---

**4. FAIR**

📌 **Fair = adil (just/equitable)**
> *"The judge made a **fair** decision."*

📌 **Fair = lumayan/cukup baik (reasonably good)**
> *"He has a **fair** chance of winning."*
> *"Her English is **fair**."* (= cukup baik, not excellent)

📌 **Fair = cerah/terang (light-colored)**
> *"She has **fair** skin."*
> *"**Fair** hair"* (= rambut pirang)

📌 **Fair = pameran/festival (exhibition)**
> *"We went to the book **fair**."*
> *"The science **fair** is next week."*

**Context is key:** 
- Tentang keadilan/justice → fair = adil
- Tentang kualitas/kemampuan → fair = lumayan
- Tentang appearance → fair = cerah/terang
- Noun form → pameran

---

**5. PRESENT**

📌 **Present = hadiah (gift)**
> *"She gave me a birthday **present**."*

📌 **Present = sekarang/saat ini (current time)**
> *"Let's focus on the **present** moment."*
> *"All members are **present** at the meeting."* (= hadir)

📌 **Present = menghadirkan/menyajikan (show/demonstrate)**
> *"He will **present** his research findings."*
> *"The company **presented** a new product."*

📌 **Present = memberikan secara formal (give formally)**
> *"The dean will **present** the awards."*

**Pro tip:** 
- Kalau ada context gift-giving → hadiah
- Kalau ada context showing/explaining → menyajikan/menghadirkan
- Kalau tentang time → sekarang/saat ini
- Kalau tentang attendance → hadir

---

**6. ADDRESS**

📌 **Address = alamat (location)**
> *"What's your home **address**?"*

📌 **Address = menangani/menghadapi (deal with)**
> *"The government needs to **address** this issue."*
> *"We must **address** the problems immediately."*

📌 **Address = menyapa/berbicara kepada (speak to)**
> *"The president will **address** the nation."*
> *"How should I **address** you?"* (= memanggil Anda)

**Quick check:**
- Noun form + physical location → alamat
- Verb + problem/issue → menangani
- Verb + person/audience → berbicara kepada

---

**7. BEAR**

📌 **Bear = beruang (animal)**
> *"A grizzly **bear** approached."*

📌 **Bear = menanggung/menahan (endure/tolerate)**
> *"I can't **bear** the pain."*
> *"She **bears** the responsibility."*

📌 **Bear = melahirkan/menghasilkan (give birth/produce)**
> *"The tree **bears** fruit."*
> *"She **bore** three children."*

📌 **Bear = membawa (carry – formal)**
> *"**Bear** this in mind."* (= ingat ini)

**Context matters:**
- Animal context → beruang
- Pain/responsibility/burden → menanggung
- Children/fruit/results → melahirkan/menghasilkan

---

**8. POINT**

📌 **Point = titik/poin (dot/mark)**
> *"Put a **point** at the end."*

📌 **Point = poin/skor (score)**
> *"Our team scored 10 **points**."*

📌 **Point = hal penting/inti (main idea/purpose)**
> *"What's your **point**?"*
> *"The **point** is we need to act now."*

📌 **Point = menunjuk (indicate direction)**
> *"She **pointed** to the door."*

📌 **Point = menunjukkan/mengindikasikan (suggest/show)**
> *"The evidence **points** to his guilt."*

#### **Strategi Menghadapi Multiple Meanings**

**Step 1: Identify Part of Speech**

Ini langkah PERTAMA dan PALING PENTING!

- **Noun?** Fokus ke arti "benda" atau "konsep"
- **Verb?** Fokus ke arti "aksi" atau "proses"
- **Adjective?** Fokus ke arti "sifat" atau "kualitas"

**Example:**
> *"The **light** in the room was dim."* → NOUN → cahaya
> *"Please **light** the fire."* → VERB → menyalakan
> *"The bag is **light**."* → ADJECTIVE → ringan

---

**Step 2: Check the Subject and Object**

Subjek dan objek sering kasih clue penting!

> *"The company **runs** three factories."* 
- Subject: company (organization)
- Object: factories
- Meaning: **mengelola** ✅ (bukan "berlari" ❌)

> *"The river **runs** through the valley."*
- Subject: river (water body)
- Through: valley (place)
- Meaning: **mengalir** ✅

---

**Step 3: Look for Prepositions**

Preposition setelah verb often changes meaning dramatically!

- **Run out** = kehabisan
- **Run into** = bertemu secara kebetulan atau menabrak
- **Run over** = menabrak dengan kendaraan
- **Run through** = melewati atau memeriksa cepat
- **Run up** = menimbulkan (hutang/biaya)

Beda preposition, beda arti!

---

**Step 4: Consider the Topic/Field**

Different fields use same words differently:

**"Current":**
- In general context: sekarang/terkini
- In electricity context: arus listrik
- In water context: arus air

**"Cell":**
- In biology: sel
- In prison context: sel tahanan
- In phone context: (cell phone) telepon seluler

---

#### **Red Flags: Warning Signs of Multiple Meanings**

🚩 **Warning Sign #1: Kata pendek dan umum**
Kata-kata seperti get, set, run, make, take usually punya banyak arti. Extra hati-hati!

🚩 **Warning Sign #2: Bisa jadi berbagai part of speech**
Kata seperti light, present, address bisa noun, verb, atau adjective. Check dulu POS-nya!

🚩 **Warning Sign #3: Technical vs General Context**
Kata "cell", "current", "field" artinya bisa beda banget tergantung context academic atau general.

---

#### **Common Mistakes to Avoid**

❌ **Mistake #1: Auto-pilot ke arti paling familiar**

Contoh SALAH:
> *"The company will **present** a new proposal."*
> Salah pikir: "present = hadiah"

Harusnya analyze dulu: subject "company" + verb "present" + object "proposal" → pasti "menyajikan/menghadirkan"

---

❌ **Mistake #2: Ignore context sepenuhnya**

Contoh SALAH:
> *"The solution is **fair**."*
> Salah pikir: "fair = pameran"

Harusnya: context tentang solution → tidak mungkin pameran → pasti "adil" atau "cukup baik"

---

❌ **Mistake #3: Tidak check POS**

Contoh SALAH:
> *"Turn on the **light**."* (NOUN)
> Synonym: "bright" (ADJECTIVE) ❌

Harusnya: light (noun) → lamp, illumination (noun juga) ✅

---

#### **Practice Technique: The Substitution Method**

Cara test apakah synonym kamu benar:

1. Ganti kata asli dengan synonym pilihan kamu
2. Baca ulang kalimatnya
3. Does it make sense? Grammar-nya benar? Meaning-nya sama?

**Example:**
> Original: *"She **runs** a small business."*
> Test 1: *"She **jogs** a small business."* → ❌ Doesn't make sense
> Test 2: *"She **operates** a small business."* → ✅ Makes perfect sense!

---

#### **The Ultimate Checklist untuk Multiple Meanings**

Setiap kali ketemu kata yang potentially punya multiple meanings:

✅ Step 1: Apa POS-nya? (noun/verb/adj/adv)
✅ Step 2: Siapa subject-nya? Apa object-nya?
✅ Step 3: Ada preposition nggak? (affect meaning?)
✅ Step 4: Topik/field apa yang dibahas?
✅ Step 5: Test pilihan synonym dengan substitution method

Follow checklist ini = minimize chances of error!

---

#### **Level Up: Homographs vs Homophones vs Homonyms**

Bonus knowledge buat yang mau deep dive:

**Homographs:** Spelling sama, pronunciation bisa beda, meaning beda
- **Read** (present: /riːd/) vs **Read** (past: /red/)
- **Lead** (to guide: /liːd/) vs **Lead** (metal: /led/)

**Homophones:** Pronunciation sama, spelling beda, meaning beda
- **Their / There / They're**
- **To / Too / Two**
- **Right / Write**

**Homonyms:** Umbrella term untuk kata dengan spelling/pronunciation sama tapi meaning beda (includes homographs)

Di SNBT, yang paling sering adalah **polysemy** (satu kata dengan related multiple meanings) dan **homonyms**.

Jangan takut sama kata-kata dengan multiple meanings! They're actually predictable kalau kamu tau pola-polanya. **Context + POS + Logic = Answer!**

### **Materi 3.1.4: Analisis Synonym untuk Different Word Classes**

#### **Word Class Matters: Kenapa Part of Speech Itu Crucial?**

Okay, bayangin kamu disuruh cari synonym dari kata "**develop**" (verb). Terus kamu jawab "**development**" (noun). Salah dong! Meskipun artinya related, tapi beda kelas katanya.

Ini adalah **GOLDEN RULE** dalam soal synonym SNBT:

> **Synonym yang kamu pilih HARUS SATU KELAS KATA dengan kata aslinya!**

Verb harus diganti verb, noun harus diganti noun, adjective harus diganti adjective, adverb harus diganti adverb. Simple? IYA! Tapi banyak yang lupa dan kena jebakan.

---

#### **Quick Refresher: 4 Major Word Classes**

**1. NOUN (Kata Benda)**
- Menyatakan orang, tempat, benda, atau konsep
- Bisa jadi subject atau object dalam kalimat
- Contoh: person, idea, development, happiness

**2. VERB (Kata Kerja)**
- Menyatakan aksi atau keadaan
- Inti dari predicate
- Contoh: develop, increase, demonstrate, analyze

**3. ADJECTIVE (Kata Sifat)**
- Mendeskripsikan atau memodifikasi noun
- Menjawab pertanyaan "what kind?" atau "which one?"
- Contoh: important, significant, major, critical

**4. ADVERB (Kata Keterangan)**
- Memodifikasi verb, adjective, atau adverb lain
- Sering (tapi tidak selalu) berakhiran -ly
- Contoh: importantly, significantly, quickly, very

---

### **NOUN SYNONYMS**

#### **Pattern Recognition untuk Nouns**

Noun synonyms biasanya lebih straightforward karena merujuk ke concrete things atau abstract concepts.

**Common Noun Pairs:**

**People/Person-related:**
- person = individual, human being
- expert = specialist, professional, authority
- leader = director, manager, head, chief
- worker = employee, staff member, laborer
- student = learner, pupil, scholar

**Concept-related:**
- problem = issue, challenge, difficulty, obstacle
- idea = concept, notion, thought, proposal
- method = approach, technique, strategy, way
- result = outcome, consequence, effect, finding
- goal = objective, aim, target, purpose

**Change/Development:**
- growth = development, expansion, increase, progress
- change = alteration, modification, transformation, shift
- improvement = enhancement, betterment, advancement, upgrade

**Abstract concepts:**
- importance = significance, relevance, consequence, weight
- difference = distinction, disparity, variation, contrast
- advantage = benefit, merit, plus, strength
- disadvantage = drawback, downside, weakness, flaw

---

#### **Strategi Identifikasi Noun Synonyms**

**Clue #1: Position dalam kalimat**
Noun biasanya ada di:
- Setelah artikel (a, an, the): *"the **problem**"*
- Setelah possessive (my, your, his, her): *"his **idea**"*
- Setelah adjective: *"important **factor**"*
- Sebagai subject: *"The **study** shows..."*
- Sebagai object: *"They discussed the **issue**."*

**Clue #2: Determiner test**
Kalau bisa ditambah "a/an/the" di depannya, kemungkinan besar noun:
- ✅ "a/the person"
- ✅ "an/the idea"
- ✅ "the problem"

**Clue #3: Pluralization**
Kalau bisa dibuat plural (tambah -s/-es), itu noun:
- person → persons/people
- idea → ideas
- problem → problems

---

#### **Jebakan Umum dengan Noun Synonyms**

**Jebakan #1: Word Family Trap**

> Kata: "The **significance** of this discovery..."
> ❌ SALAH: significant (adjective)
> ❌ SALAH: significantly (adverb)
> ✅ BENAR: importance (noun)

**Jebakan #2: Similar Spelling Different POS**

> Kata: "The **development** of technology..."
> ❌ SALAH: develop (verb)
> ❌ SALAH: developmental (adjective)
> ✅ BENAR: advancement (noun), progress (noun)

---

### **VERB SYNONYMS**

#### **Pattern Recognition untuk Verbs**

Verbs express actions, states, or occurrences. Identifying verb synonyms butuh perhatian khusus ke tense dan form.

**Common Verb Pairs:**

**Communication/Expression:**
- show = demonstrate, display, exhibit, reveal, indicate
- explain = clarify, elucidate, describe, illustrate
- say = state, mention, declare, assert, express
- tell = inform, notify, advise, instruct

**Change/Movement:**
- increase = rise, grow, expand, escalate, surge
- decrease = decline, fall, drop, diminish, reduce
- develop = evolve, progress, advance, grow, mature
- change = alter, modify, transform, convert, shift

**Research/Analysis:**
- examine = analyze, investigate, inspect, study, scrutinize
- find = discover, detect, identify, locate, uncover
- prove = demonstrate, verify, confirm, validate, establish
- suggest = indicate, imply, propose, recommend, hint

**Causation:**
- cause = produce, generate, create, bring about, lead to
- affect = influence, impact, alter, modify, change
- help = assist, aid, support, facilitate, enable

**Continuation/Maintenance:**
- continue = proceed, persist, carry on, maintain, sustain
- keep = maintain, retain, preserve, hold, sustain

---

#### **Strategi Identifikasi Verb Synonyms**

**Clue #1: Position dalam kalimat**
Verbs biasanya:
- Setelah subject: *"The study **shows**..."*
- Dengan helping verb: *"will **demonstrate**"*, *"has **increased**"*
- Dengan "to": *"to **explain**"*, *"to **develop**"*

**Clue #2: Tense test**
Kalau bisa diubah ke past tense (-ed atau irregular), itu verb:
- show → showed
- increase → increased
- find → found

**Clue #3: Progressive test**
Kalau bisa ditambah -ing untuk continuous form, itu verb:
- explaining, developing, increasing, showing

---

#### **Jebakan Umum dengan Verb Synonyms**

**Jebakan #1: Noun Form yang Mirip**

> Kata: "The research **demonstrates** the effectiveness..."
> ❌ SALAH: demonstration (noun)
> ✅ BENAR: shows (verb), proves (verb)

**Jebakan #2: Adjective Form yang Deceptive**

> Kata: "The data **indicates** a trend..."
> ❌ SALAH: indicative (adjective)
> ✅ BENAR: suggests (verb), shows (verb)

**Jebakan #3: Tense Consistency**

Hati-hati! Synonym harus compatible dengan tense yang dipakai:

> "The study **showed** significant results."
> ✅ demonstrated (past)
> ❌ demonstrate (present)

### **ADJECTIVE SYNONYMS**

#### **Pattern Recognition untuk Adjectives**

Adjectives describe or modify nouns. They answer "what kind?", "which one?", "how many?"

**Common Adjective Pairs:**

**Size/Magnitude:**
- large = big, huge, substantial, considerable, extensive
- small = little, tiny, minor, slight, minimal

**Quality/Evaluation:**
- important = significant, crucial, vital, essential, critical
- good = excellent, fine, superior, favorable, beneficial
- bad = poor, inferior, unfavorable, negative, detrimental
- difficult = hard, challenging, tough, complex, demanding
- easy = simple, straightforward, effortless, uncomplicated

**Change/Innovation:**
- new = novel, recent, modern, fresh, contemporary
- old = ancient, aged, outdated, traditional, former

**Extent/Degree:**
- major = significant, substantial, considerable, primary, principal
- minor = small, insignificant, trivial, negligible, slight
- complete = total, full, entire, comprehensive, absolute

**Frequency:**
- common = frequent, widespread, prevalent, usual, typical
- rare = uncommon, unusual, scarce, infrequent, exceptional

---

#### **Strategi Identifikasi Adjective Synonyms**

**Clue #1: Position dalam kalimat**
Adjectives biasanya:
- Sebelum noun: *"an **important** factor"*
- Setelah be/linking verb: *"is **significant**"*, *"seems **difficult**"*

**Clue #2: Degree test**
Kalau bisa ditambah "very" atau "more/most", biasanya adjective:
- very important, more significant
- very difficult, most challenging

**Clue #3: "-ly" adverb test**
Banyak adjective bisa dijadikan adverb dengan tambah -ly:
- important → importantly
- significant → significantly
- (Tapi hati-hati! Tidak semua kata -ly adalah adverb; "friendly", "lovely" adalah adjective)

---

#### **Jebakan Umum dengan Adjective Synonyms**

**Jebakan #1: Adverb yang Kelihatan Mirip**

> Kata: "This is **significant** research."
> ❌ SALAH: significantly (adverb)
> ✅ BENAR: important (adjective)

**Jebakan #2: Noun Form dari Word Family**

> Kata: "The study reached a **critical** stage."
> ❌ SALAH: criticism (noun)
> ❌ SALAH: critic (noun)
> ✅ BENAR: crucial (adjective), vital (adjective)

### **ADVERB SYNONYMS**

#### **Pattern Recognition untuk Adverbs**

Adverbs modify verbs, adjectives, atau adverbs lain. Many (but not all) end in -ly.

**Common Adverb Pairs:**

**Manner (how?):**
- quickly = rapidly, swiftly, speedily, fast
- slowly = gradually, steadily
- carefully = cautiously, attentively, meticulously
- easily = effortlessly, readily, simply

**Degree (to what extent?):**
- very = extremely, highly, remarkably, exceedingly, considerably
- quite = rather, fairly, pretty, somewhat
- almost = nearly, practically, virtually

**Frequency (how often?):**
- often = frequently, regularly, commonly, repeatedly
- rarely = seldom, infrequently, hardly ever
- always = constantly, continually, invariably, consistently
- sometimes = occasionally, periodically, at times

**Time (when?):**
- immediately = instantly, right away, at once, promptly
- eventually = ultimately, finally, in the end
- recently = lately, not long ago

**Certainty:**
- certainly = definitely, surely, undoubtedly, absolutely
- probably = likely, presumably, possibly, perhaps

---

#### **Strategi Identifikasi Adverb Synonyms**

**Clue #1: Modifies verb**
> *"The economy grew **rapidly**."* (modifies "grew")
> *"She speaks **fluently**."* (modifies "speaks")

**Clue #2: Modifies adjective**
> *"It's **extremely** important."* (modifies "important")
> *"The result is **highly** significant."* (modifies "significant")

**Clue #3: Modifies another adverb**
> *"She works **very** carefully."* ("very" modifies "carefully")

**Clue #4: The -ly pattern**
Banyak adverbs dibentuk dari adjective + ly:
- quick → quickly
- significant → significantly
- careful → carefully

**HATI-HATI:** Tidak semua kata -ly adalah adverb!
- friendly, lovely, lonely → adjectives!

---

#### **Jebakan Umum dengan Adverb Synonyms**

**Jebakan #1: Adjective Form**

> Kata: "The temperature increased **significantly**."
> ❌ SALAH: significant (adjective)
> ✅ BENAR: considerably (adverb), substantially (adverb)

**Jebakan #2: Confusion dengan Similar Forms**

> Kata: "She completed the task **efficiently**."
> ❌ SALAH: efficient (adjective)
> ❌ SALAH: efficiency (noun)
> ✅ BENAR: effectively (adverb)

Setelah pilih synonym, always double-check dengan teknik ini:

**Step 1: Substitute the word**

**Step 2: Read aloud (mentally)**
Baca kalimat dengan kata baru. Does it flow naturally?

**Step 3: Check grammar**
- Tense-nya cocok? (untuk verb)
- Plural/singular-nya sesuai? (untuk noun)

**Step 4: Verify meaning**
Arti keseluruhannya tetap sama atau tidak?

#### **Denotation vs Connotation: Subtle but Important**

**Denotation** = literal dictionary meaning
**Connotation** = emotional/cultural associations with a word

Contoh:

**"Cheap" vs "Inexpensive"**
- Denotation: kedua-duanya = low price
- Connotation:
  - **Cheap** → negative (poor quality, murahan)
  - **Inexpensive** → neutral/positive (affordable, terjangkau)

**"Skinny" vs "Slender" vs "Thin"**
- Denotation: semua = not fat
- Connotation:
  - **Skinny** → negative (terlalu kurus, unhealthy)
  - **Thin** → neutral (kurus secara objektif)
  - **Slender** → positive (langsing dengan kesan graceful)

**Dalam SNBT:** Academic texts prefer words dengan **neutral atau positive connotation**. Jadi kalau ada pilihan antara "cheap" dan "inexpensive" untuk formal context, choose "inexpensive"!

---

#### **Precision: Academic Words Are More Specific**

Academic vocabulary itu nggak cuma formal, tapi juga lebih **precise** (tepat/spesifik).

**Example 1:**

Casual: *"The weather affected the crops."*
Academic: *"The drought **adversely affected** crop yields."*

"Adversely affected" lebih specific daripada just "affected"—jelas menunjukkan negative impact.

**Example 2:**

Casual: *"Many people think this way."*
Academic: *"A **substantial proportion** of respondents **subscribe to** this viewpoint."*

"Substantial proportion" lebih precise dari "many people", dan "subscribe to this viewpoint" lebih formal dari "think this way".

#### **Latin & Greek Roots: The Secret Weapon**

Banyak academic vocabulary berasal dari Latin dan Greek. Kalau kamu recognize roots-nya, kamu bisa infer meaning!

**Common Latin Roots:**

| Root | Meaning | Examples |
|------|---------|----------|
| **port** | carry | transport, import, export, portable |
| **dict** | say | predict, dictate, contradict, indicate |
| **scrib/script** | write | describe, prescribe, manuscript, transcribe |
| **spect** | see/look | inspect, respect, spectator, perspective |
| **mit/miss** | send | transmit, submit, admission, dismiss |
| **fact/fect** | make/do | manufacture, effect, defect, perfect |
| **duc/duct** | lead | produce, reduce, conduct, educate |

**Common Greek Roots:**

| Root | Meaning | Examples |
|------|---------|----------|
| **bio** | life | biology, biography, biodegradable |
| **geo** | earth | geography, geology, geometry |
| **graph** | write | graphic, photograph, paragraph |
| **phone** | sound | telephone, microphone, phonetics |
| **psych** | mind | psychology, psychiatry, psychic |
| **tech** | skill/art | technology, technical, technique |
| **log** | word/study | logic, dialogue, biology (study of life) |

**Pro Strategy:** Kalau ketemu unfamiliar academic word, try to break it down by roots!

Example: **"Biodegradable"**
- **bio** (life) + **de** (down) + **grad** (step) + **able** (can be)
- = can be broken down by living organisms

#### **Collocation: Words That Go Together**

Academic words punya **preferred partners**—kata-kata yang naturally sering dipasangkan.

**Strong Collocations:**

**With "conduct":**
- conduct **research** ✅
- conduct **a study** ✅
- conduct **an experiment** ✅
- conduct **an investigation** ✅
- ❌ (jarang:) conduct a test, conduct work

**With "significant":**
- **significant** impact/effect/difference/increase ✅
- **significant** role/contribution/progress ✅

**With "demonstrate":**
- demonstrate **ability/skill** ✅
- demonstrate **evidence/proof** ✅
- demonstrate **effectiveness** ✅

**Kenapa penting?** Dalam soal synonym, opsi yang punya collocation match dengan kata lain dalam kalimat lebih likely correct!

Force yourself to use academic words when writing. Practice makes permanent!

#### **Common Pitfalls dengan Academic Vocabulary**

**Pitfall #1: Overusing Fancy Words (Thesaurus Syndrome)**

Jangan asal ganti semua kata dengan yang fancy! Harus natural dan appropriate.

❌ TOO MUCH: *"The individual commenced to ambulate toward the domicile."*
✅ BETTER: *"The person started to walk toward the house."*

**Pitfall #2: Mixing Registers**

Don't mix casual dan formal words awkwardly:

❌ MIXED: *"The research kinda demonstrates that..."* (kinda = casual!)
✅ CONSISTENT: *"The research demonstrates that..."*

**Pitfall #3: False Friends**

Beberapa academic words mirip dengan kata lain tapi beda arti:

- **Affect** (verb: mempengaruhi) ≠ **Effect** (noun: hasil)
- **Complement** (melengkapi) ≠ **Compliment** (pujian)
- **Principle** (prinsip) ≠ **Principal** (kepala sekolah/utama)

#### **Quick Recognition Checklist**

Untuk identify apakah suatu text academic or not:

✅ Uses formal vocabulary (obtain vs get, demonstrate vs show)
✅ Avoids contractions (do not vs don't, cannot vs can't)
✅ Uses passive voice frequently (it is believed that...)
✅ Uses hedging language (may, might, possibly, potentially)
✅ Cites sources and evidence (according to, research shows)
✅ Uses technical/discipline-specific terms
✅ Longer, complex sentences dengan multiple clauses
