# TOPIK 3.2: REFERENCE (KATA GANTI)

## Materi 3.2.1: Pengenalan Reference

### Apa Itu Reference dan Kenapa Penting?

Bayangin kamu lagi baca novel atau artikel, terus setiap kali nyebut nama tokoh atau benda, harus ngulang-ngulang nama lengkapnya. Annoying banget kan? Nah, makanya ada yang namanya **reference** atau kata ganti!

Reference itu kayak "pengganti" buat kata benda (noun) yang udah disebutin sebelumnya. Jadi kita nggak perlu ngulang-ulang kata yang sama. Contohnya:

> "Sarah is a brilliant student. **She** always gets straight A's."

Nah, kata **"she"** di sini itu reference yang ngganti "Sarah". Simple, kan?

### Kenapa Soal Reference Sering Muncul di SNBT?

Di SNBT, soal reference ini **PASTI** muncul! Biasanya bentuknya kayak gini:

- "The word 'they' in line 15 refers to..."
- "What does 'it' in paragraph 2 refer to?"
- "The pronoun 'this' in the last sentence refers to..."

Soal ini sebenarnya **ngetes ketelitian** kamu. Banyak yang kecele karena:
1. **Asal njawab** tanpa baca kalimat sebelumnya
2. **Ketipu** sama kata benda yang paling deket (padahal belum tentu itu jawabannya!)
3. **Nggak ngecek** singular/plural atau gender

### Jenis-Jenis Pronouns (Kata Ganti)

Ada beberapa jenis pronoun yang bakal kamu temuin:

**1. Personal Pronouns** (Yang paling sering!)
- Subject: I, you, he, she, it, we, they
- Object: me, you, him, her, it, us, them
- Possessive: my, your, his, her, its, our, their (+ mine, yours, hers, ours, theirs)

**2. Demonstrative Pronouns** (Yang tricky!)
- This, that (singular)
- These, those (plural)

**3. Relative Pronouns** (Yang academic!)
- Who, whom, whose, which, that

**4. Indefinite Pronouns**
- One, ones, some, others, another

### The Golden Rule: SELALU BACA KALIMAT SEBELUMNYA!

Ini **kunci utama** buat nge-solve soal reference:

🔑 **90% jawaban ada di kalimat SEBELUM pronoun itu muncul!**

Kadang juga bisa di kalimat yang sama, tapi sebelum pronoun-nya. Jarang banget jawabannya di kalimat SESUDAHNYA (kecuali dalam kasus tertentu).

### Antecedent: Si "Tokoh Asli" yang Digantikan

**Antecedent** itu istilah fancy buat "kata benda yang digantikan oleh pronoun". 

Contoh:
> "**The researchers** conducted multiple experiments. **They** discovered something groundbreaking."

Yang mana antecedent-nya? YUP, **"The researchers"**! Karena kata "they" nggantiin "the researchers".

### Quick Tips Buat Identifikasi Reference:

✅ **Cek number (singular/plural)**
- It, this, that, he, she → cari noun SINGULAR
- They, these, those → cari noun PLURAL

✅ **Cek gender (kalau pakai he/she)**
- He → laki-laki
- She → perempuan

✅ **Proximity principle**
- Biasanya (tapi nggak selalu!) pronoun merujuk ke noun yang terdekat

✅ **Logic check**
- Apakah secara logika masuk akal? Coba substitute pronoun dengan noun pilihan kamu, make sense nggak?

### Jebakan Umum yang Harus Diwaspadai!

⚠️ **JEBAKAN #1: Multiple Nouns Sebelumnya**

Kadang di satu kalimat ada beberapa noun, terus kamu bingung mana yang dimaksud. Contoh:

> "The **company** announced that their **employees** would receive bonuses. **They** were thrilled."

"They" itu siapa? Company atau employees? 
→ **Employees!** Karena company itu singular (seharusnya "it"), dan yang "thrilled" itu logically para karyawan, bukan perusahaannya.

⚠️ **JEBAKAN #2: Pronoun Merujuk ke WHOLE IDEA**

Sometimes, pronoun nggak merujuk ke satu noun spesifik, tapi ke SELURUH kalimat atau ide sebelumnya!

> "The government increased taxes, reduced public services, and cut education funding. **This** caused widespread protests."

"This" merujuk ke apa? → Bukan cuma "education funding", tapi ke **SELURUH KEBIJAKAN** pemerintah!

⚠️ **JEBAKAN #3: Distractor dengan Kata yang Sama**

Kadang opsi jawaban pakai kata yang sama dari teks, padahal bukan itu maksudnya!

> "Scientists believe that climate change is accelerating. **This belief** is supported by data."

Kalau ditanya "this belief" refers to apa, jawabannya BUKAN "Scientists" atau "climate change" secara terpisah, tapi **"Scientists' belief that climate change is accelerating"** → complete idea!

### Variasi Pertanyaan dalam Soal SNBT:

1. **Direct reference**: "The word 'they' refers to..."
2. **Indirect reference**: "What does the author mean by 'it' in line...?"
3. **Complex reference**: "The phrase 'such activities' in paragraph 3 refers to..."

### Link ke Topik Lain:

Reference ini sebenarnya **bagian dari coherence devices** (Topik 2.3). Pronoun itu salah satu cara penulis bikin teks jadi kohesif dan nggak ngebosenin karena ngulang-ngulang kata yang sama.

Plus, understanding reference juga ngebantu banget buat:
- **Paraphrase** (Topik 3.3): Karena kamu ngerti siapa/apa yang dimaksud
- **Main Idea** (Topik 1.1): Pronoun ngebantu kamu track subject utama dalam paragraf
- **Text Coherence** (Topik 2.3): Pronoun = salah satu connector antar kalimat

## Materi 3.2.2: Analisis Personal Pronouns Reference

### What Are Personal Pronouns?

Personal pronouns itu kata ganti yang paling **umum** dan paling **sering keluar** di soal SNBT. Mereka nggantiin orang atau benda tertentu dalam kalimat.

### The Complete Chart of Personal Pronouns:

| Function | Singular | Plural |
|----------|----------|--------|
| **Subject** (pelaku) | I, you, he, she, it | we, you, they |
| **Object** (penerima) | me, you, him, her, it | us, you, them |
| **Possessive Adjective** (kepemilikan + noun) | my, your, his, her, its | our, your, their |
| **Possessive Pronoun** (kepemilikan standalone) | mine, yours, his, hers, - | ours, yours, theirs |

### Subject Pronouns: The Doers

Subject pronouns itu yang **melakukan** aksi dalam kalimat. Posisinya di awal kalimat atau clause, sebelum verb.

**Ciri-ciri:**
- Selalu diikuti oleh VERB
- Jadi subject dari kalimat/clause

**Example dalam konteks bacaan:**

> "**Dr. Martinez** specializes in neuroscience. **She** has published over 50 research papers on brain plasticity."

"She" = subject pronoun yang refers to **Dr. Martinez**.

**Tips Identifikasi:**
✅ Cek verb setelahnya → singular atau plural?
- He/She/It + singular verb (is, has, does, -s)
- They/We + plural verb (are, have, do)

**Common Trap:**

> "The **team** of researchers presented their findings. **They** had worked for three years on the project."

JANGAN ketipu! "They" bukan merujuk ke "researchers" meskipun lebih deket! Tapi merujuk ke **"team"** (as a collective group). Tergantung context—kalau dianggap satu kesatuan = "it", kalau dianggap individu-individu = "they".

Di SNBT, biasanya:
- **American English**: "The team is..." (singular)
- **British English**: "The team are..." (plural)

Tapi untuk soal reference, **cek logic dan verb agreement**!

### Object Pronouns: The Receivers

Object pronouns itu yang **menerima** aksi dalam kalimat. Posisinya setelah verb atau preposisi (in, on, at, with, for, to, etc.)

**Ciri-ciri:**
- Muncul setelah VERB (direct object)
- Muncul setelah PREPOSITION (object of preposition)

**Example:**

> "The **professor** gave the **students** a challenging assignment. He warned **them** about the deadline."

"Them" = object pronoun yang refers to **"students"**.

**Another Example with Preposition:**

> "**Climate activists** have been protesting for months. The government finally agreed to meet with **them**."

"Them" (setelah preposisi "with") = **"climate activists"**.

**Tips Identifikasi:**
✅ Kalau pronoun setelah verb action/preposisi → kemungkinan besar OBJECT pronoun
✅ Cek: siapa yang menerima aksi? Itu antecedent-nya!

**Common Trap:**

> "The **CEO** asked the **board members** to reconsider. She wanted **them** to delay the decision."

"Them" refers to siapa? → **Board members**, bukan CEO!

**Why?** Karena yang diminta untuk delay decision itu board members, bukan CEO-nya sendiri.

### Possessive Adjectives: Showing Ownership (+ Noun)

Possessive adjectives itu nunjukin kepemilikan dan **SELALU** diikuti oleh noun.

**Formula:**
**Possessive Adjective + NOUN**

**Example:**

> "**Indonesia** is known for its cultural diversity. **Its** traditions have been preserved for centuries."

"Its" = possessive adjective yang refers to **"Indonesia"**.
Notice: "its" diikuti oleh noun "traditions"!

**Complete Example:**

> "**The scientist** dedicated her entire career to cancer research. **Her** discoveries have saved countless lives."

"Her" (possessive adjective) + "discoveries" (noun) = refers to **"the scientist"**.

**Super Important Note tentang ITS vs IT'S:**

⚠️ **ITS** = possessive (kepemilikan) → "its benefits", "its impact"
⚠️ **IT'S** = it is (contraction) → BUKAN possessive!

**Common Mistakes:**
❌ "The company increased it's profits" → SALAH!
✅ "The company increased **its** profits" → BENAR!

**Di SNBT, jebakan "its" itu sering muncul!** Kalau ada soal reference tentang "its", antecedent-nya pasti singular noun (biasanya benda, organisasi, hewan, atau konsep).

### Possessive Pronouns: Standalone Ownership

Possessive pronouns itu kayak possessive adjectives, tapi **NGGAK diikuti noun** karena dia standalone (berdiri sendiri).

**Formula:**
**Possessive Pronoun** (tanpa noun setelahnya!)

**Example:**

> "This **research** is groundbreaking, but **ours** is more comprehensive."

"Ours" = possessive pronoun (standalone, nggak diikuti noun) yang refers to **"our research"**.

**Perbedaan dengan Possessive Adjective:**

| Possessive Adjective + Noun | Possessive Pronoun (standalone) |
|------------------------------|--------------------------------|
| This is **my** book | This book is **mine** |
| **Your** idea is great | The great idea is **yours** |
| **Their** study is important | The important study is **theirs** |

**Di soal SNBT, possessive pronouns agak jarang, tapi tetep bisa muncul!**

### Agreement Rules: The Key to Correct Answers

**RULE #1: NUMBER AGREEMENT (Singular vs Plural)**

Pronoun harus match dengan number antecedent-nya!

✅ Singular antecedent → singular pronoun
- The **student** → he/she/his/her
- The **book** → it/its

✅ Plural antecedent → plural pronoun
- The **researchers** → they/them/their

**Common Trap dengan Collective Nouns:**

Collective nouns (group, team, committee, family, government, company, class) bisa singular ATAU plural tergantung context!

> "The **committee** made **its** decision." → dianggap satu unit
> "The **committee** were divided in **their** opinions." → dianggap individu-individu

**Di SNBT:** Kalau ada soal reference tentang collective noun, **cek verb-nya**! Singular verb = singular pronoun, plural verb = plural pronoun.

**RULE #2: GENDER AGREEMENT (He vs She)**

Ini cuma berlaku kalau antecedent-nya ORANG dengan gender jelas.

✅ Male person → he/him/his
✅ Female person → she/her/hers
✅ Gender-neutral/unknown → they/them/their (modern usage)

**Example:**

> "**Dr. Emily Chen** has extensive experience in virology. **She** led the research team during the pandemic."

Gender-nya clear → female → "she"!

**Modern Usage Note:**

Sekarang, singular "they" udah diterima buat refer to a person dengan unknown gender atau gender-neutral:

> "If a **student** wants to succeed, **they** should study regularly."

(Dulu: "he or she", sekarang lebih common: "they")

**RULE #3: PERSON AGREEMENT (First/Second/Third Person)**

Jangan mixed person!

❌ "If **one** wants to succeed, **you** should work hard." → INCONSISTENT!
✅ "If **one** wants to succeed, **one** should work hard." → CONSISTENT!
✅ "If **you** want to succeed, **you** should work hard." → CONSISTENT!

### Special Cases: IT

"It" itu pronoun yang **paling fleksibel** dan kadang tricky!

**Uses of IT:**

**1. Merujuk ke benda/hewan (singular)**
> "The **theory** is controversial, but **it** is supported by evidence."

**2. Merujuk ke organisasi/institusi**
> "The **university** announced that **it** would increase scholarships."

**3. Merujuk ke konsep/ide abstrak**
> "**Global warming** is accelerating, and **it** poses serious threats."

**4. Weather, time, distance (impersonal it)**
> "**It** is raining." → "it" nggak merujuk ke apa-apa, cuma grammatical
> "**It** is 5 PM."

**5. Merujuk ke SELURUH kalimat sebelumnya (whole idea)**
> "The government raised taxes significantly. **It** angered many citizens."

Wait, "it" merujuk ke apa? Bukan "taxes" atau "government", tapi **"the fact that government raised taxes"** → whole action!

**Common Trap dengan IT:**

Di soal SNBT, kalau ditanya "it" refers to apa, jangan langsung pilih noun terdekat! **Cek dulu context dan logic**:

- Apakah "it" merujuk ke satu noun spesifik?
- Atau merujuk ke whole idea/situation?

**Contoh Bacaan:**

> "Scientists discovered a new species of frog in the Amazon rainforest. The species exhibits unique color patterns that help **it** camouflage in the environment. This discovery is significant because **it** suggests biodiversity in the region is higher than previously thought."

**Question:** "What does 'it' in the second sentence refer to?"

**Trap answers:**
- Scientists (❌ plural, would be "them")
- Amazon rainforest (❌ nggak make sense secara logic)
- Color patterns (❌ plural, would be "they")

**Correct answer:**
- **The species** (✅ singular, secara logic yang camouflage itu species-nya!)

**Question 2:** "What does 'it' in the third sentence refer to?"

**Trap answers:**
- The region (❌ logically wrong)
- The discovery (✅ CORRECT! Discovery itu singular noun dan secara logic, discovery itu yang "suggests")

### Practice Approach for Personal Pronouns:

**Step 1:** Identify the pronoun dan cek jenis-nya (subject/object/possessive)

**Step 2:** Cek number (singular or plural?)

**Step 3:** Cek gender (kalau applicable: he/she)

**Step 4:** Scan kalimat SEBELUMNYA untuk possible antecedents

**Step 5:** Eliminate yang number/gender-nya nggak match

**Step 6:** Dari sisa options, pilih yang **paling make sense secara LOGIC**

**Step 7:** VERIFY dengan substitute pronoun dengan noun pilihan kamu → make sense nggak?

### Mini Contoh Bacaan (Tanpa Soal):

> "**Artificial intelligence** is transforming industries worldwide. **Its** applications range from healthcare to transportation. Many **experts** believe that **it** will continue to evolve rapidly in the coming decades. However, **they** also warn about potential ethical concerns. **Their** recommendations emphasize the need for regulation."

**Analysis:**
- "Its" → refers to **"Artificial intelligence"** (singular possessive)
- First "it" → refers to **"Artificial intelligence"** (singular subject)
- "they" → refers to **"experts"** (plural subject)
- "Their" → refers to **"experts"** (plural possessive)

See the pattern? Selalu **match number**, selalu **cek logic**, dan selalu **verify**!

## Materi 3.2.3: Analisis Demonstrative Pronouns Reference

### What Are Demonstrative Pronouns?

Demonstrative pronouns itu kata ganti yang **menunjuk** ke sesuatu (demonstrate = menunjukkan). Mereka kayak "pointing words" yang nge-refer ke noun atau idea tertentu.

**The Four Demonstratives:**
- **This** (singular, dekat)
- **That** (singular, jauh)
- **These** (plural, dekat)
- **Those** (plural, jauh)

### Why Are They TRICKY in SNBT?

Demonstrative pronouns itu **lebih tricky** dibanding personal pronouns karena:

1. **Bisa merujuk ke ONE NOUN** atau **WHOLE IDEA/CLAUSE**
2. **Bisa merujuk ke something yang belum disebutkan** (forward reference)
3. **Proximity (dekat/jauh) bisa temporal ATAU spatial**

Makanya, soal tentang demonstrative pronouns ini **sering jadi jebakan**!

### THIS dan THAT: The Singular Pointers

**Basic Difference:**
- **THIS** = dekat (near, recent, just mentioned)
- **THAT** = jauh (far, earlier mentioned, distant in time/space)

**Tapi di teks akademik, perbedaan ini sering blur!** Yang lebih penting: **apa yang dirujuk?**

### Case 1: Merujuk ke Specific NOUN (Singular)

**Example Bacaan:**

> "The **research methodology** was rigorous and well-designed. **This** ensured the validity of the findings."

**Analysis:**
"This" refers to → **"research methodology"** (singular noun)

Kenapa bukan "findings"? Karena:
- "Findings" itu plural (seharusnya "these")
- Secara logic, yang ensure validity itu methodology-nya, bukan findings-nya sendiri

**Verification Trick:**
Substitute "this" dengan noun pilihan kamu:
"The research methodology ensured the validity of the findings." ✅ Make sense!

### Case 2: Merujuk ke WHOLE IDEA/CLAUSE (Paling Sering!)

Ini yang **paling tricky** dan **paling sering keluar di SNBT**!

**Example Bacaan:**

> "The government implemented strict environmental regulations, increased penalties for violations, and established monitoring systems. **This** led to significant improvements in air quality."

**Question:** "This" refers to what?

**Trap Answers:**
- Monitoring systems (❌ too specific, hanya satu bagian)
- Penalties (❌ too specific)
- Environmental regulations (❌ too narrow)

**Correct Answer:**
- **The government's comprehensive environmental policies** (✅ keseluruhan tindakan!)

"This" di sini merujuk ke **SELURUH rangkaian aksi** pemerintah, bukan cuma satu komponen!

**How to Recognize Whole-Idea Reference:**

🔍 **Clue #1:** "This" followed by verb
> "**This** caused..." → kemungkinan besar merujuk whole idea
> "**This method** was used..." → merujuk specific noun "method"

🔍 **Clue #2:** Kalimat sebelumnya complex dengan banyak informasi
> Kalau kalimat sebelumnya panjang dengan beberapa clause/points → kemungkinan "this" refers to the whole thing

🔍 **Clue #3:** Logic check
> Tanya: "What CAUSED the result?" Biasanya bukan satu noun, tapi kombinasi actions/factors

### Case 3: Demonstrative + NOUN (Demonstrative Adjective)

Wait, ini bukan pronoun lagi! Ini **demonstrative ADJECTIVE** karena diikuti noun.

**Example:**

> "Several **theories** explain the phenomenon. However, **this theory** is the most widely accepted."

"This theory" → "this" itu adjective yang modifies "theory", jadi bukan pronoun reference question!

**Tapi kadang di soal:**

> "Researchers proposed various **solutions**. Among **these solutions**, the most feasible is renewable energy adoption."

**Question:** "These solutions" refers to what?

**Answer:** "Various solutions proposed by researchers"

**Key Point:**
Kalau demonstrative diikuti noun, yang dirujuk itu **noun dengan karakteristik tertentu** yang disebutkan sebelumnya!

### THESE dan THOSE: The Plural Pointers

**Basic Difference:**
- **THESE** = plural, dekat (recently mentioned)
- **THOSE** = plural, jauh (mentioned earlier)

### Case 1: Merujuk ke Specific PLURAL NOUNS

**Example Bacaan:**

> "**Advanced technologies** such as AI, blockchain, and quantum computing are revolutionizing industries. **These** are transforming how businesses operate."

"These" refers to → **"Advanced technologies"** (atau bisa juga "AI, blockchain, and quantum computing")

**Verification:**
"AI, blockchain, and quantum computing are transforming..." ✅ Makes sense!

### Case 2: Merujuk ke MULTIPLE IDEAS/POINTS

**Example Bacaan:**

> "The study identified three major **challenges**: insufficient funding, lack of qualified personnel, and outdated equipment. **These** must be addressed urgently."

"These" refers to → **"three major challenges"** atau specifically "insufficient funding, lack of qualified personnel, and outdated equipment"

**Which one is the better answer?**

Tergantung opsi yang tersedia! Kalau ada opsi "three major challenges" → itu lebih general dan safe. Kalau opsi-nya list semua → itu juga bisa correct.

**Di SNBT, pilih yang:**
✅ **Paling complete** (mencakup semua elemen)
✅ **Paling concise** (ringkas tapi tetep accurate)

### Case 3: Contrasting THESE vs THOSE

Kadang dalam satu teks ada "these" DAN "those" untuk membedakan dua groups!

**Example Bacaan:**

> "There are two types of **research methods**: qualitative and quantitative. **Those** (quantitative methods) rely on numerical data, while **these** (qualitative methods) focus on descriptive information."

**Analysis:**
- "Those" → quantitative methods (mentioned first, thus "farther")
- "These" → qualitative methods (mentioned second, thus "nearer")

**Tapi hati-hati!** Contrast ini nggak selalu strict. Kadang "these" dan "those" dipakai interchangeably kalau context-nya clear.

### Special Feature: "SUCH" as Demonstrative

"Such" itu technically bukan demonstrative pronoun, tapi functionally similar!

**Example:**

> "The **experiment** involved complex procedures and required specialized equipment. **Such** endeavors demand significant resources."

"Such endeavors" refers to → **"experiments that involve complex procedures and require specialized equipment"**

"Such" itu merujuk ke **type atau category** yang disebutkan sebelumnya!

### The Infamous "THIS" at the Beginning of Sentence

Di teks akademik, penulis sering banget mulai kalimat dengan "This..." tanpa ngasih clear antecedent. Ini bisa bikin bingung!

**Strategy:**

**Step 1:** Baca SELURUH kalimat sebelumnya

**Step 2:** Identify: What is the MAIN POINT or ACTION in that sentence?

**Step 3:** "This" kemungkinan besar refers to that main point

**Example:**

> "Researchers discovered that sleep deprivation significantly impairs cognitive function, reduces memory consolidation, and weakens immune response. **This** highlights the importance of adequate sleep."

**Question:** "This" refers to?

**Think:** What "highlights the importance"? 
→ The FACT that sleep deprivation has all those negative effects!

**Answer:** "The finding that sleep deprivation impairs cognitive function, reduces memory consolidation, and weakens immune response"

(Atau simplified: "The research findings" / "The discovery about sleep deprivation's effects")

### Common Traps with Demonstratives:

⚠️ **TRAP #1: Nearest Noun Assumption**

Jangan langsung assume "this/that" refers to noun yang paling deket!

**Example:**

> "The **policy** failed to address income inequality despite the government's **promises**. **This** disappointed many citizens."

"This" refers to apa?
- Promises? (nearest noun) ❌
- Policy? ❌
- **The fact that the policy failed** ✅ (whole idea!)

⚠️ **TRAP #2: Number Mismatch**

Jangan pilih plural noun untuk "this/that" atau singular noun untuk "these/those"!

**Example:**

> "Various **studies** have confirmed the link. **This** is now widely accepted."

Wait, "this" itu singular tapi "studies" plural! "This" refers to apa?
→ **"The link"** (singular) atau **"The fact that studies confirmed the link"** (whole idea, treated as singular)

⚠️ **TRAP #3: Options dengan Kata dari Teks**

Kadang opsi jawaban pake exact words dari teks, tapi wrong reference!

**Example:**

> "**Scientists** developed a new **vaccine** using mRNA technology. **This** could revolutionize medicine."

Options:
A. Scientists
B. Vaccine
C. mRNA technology
D. The development of the vaccine using mRNA technology

"This could revolutionize" → apa yang bisa revolutionize? 
→ Bukan "scientists" atau "technology" alone, tapi **"the vaccine"** (B) atau **"the development"** (D)

Di sini, B (vaccine) dan D (development) sama-sama reasonable, tapi D lebih complete!

### Demonstratives dan Proximity (Near vs Far)

**Temporal Proximity:**
- **This/These** → just mentioned, recent, current
- **That/Those** → mentioned earlier, past, previous

**Example:**

> "Last year, researchers proposed a new **theory**. More recently, they conducted experiments to test it. **That theory** has now been validated."

"That theory" → refers to theory yang disebutkan di kalimat pertama (farther back in time)

**Tapi di praktiknya:**

Di modern English, especially dalam academic writing, perbedaan "this" vs "that" untuk proximity udah nggak strict! Yang lebih penting: **clarity dan logic**.

### Advanced Pattern: Demonstrative Followed by Summary Noun

Ini pattern yang sophisticated dan sering di teks SNBT level tinggi!

**Example:**

> "The economy is growing, unemployment is falling, and consumer confidence is rising. **These positive indicators** suggest a strong recovery."

"These positive indicators" refers to → **"economic growth, falling unemployment, and rising consumer confidence"**

**Pattern:** These/Those + **summary noun** (indicators, factors, challenges, benefits, etc.)

Summary noun itu kayak "label" buat group of things yang disebutkan sebelumnya!

### How to Approach Demonstrative Reference Questions:

**STEP-BY-STEP PROCESS:**

**1. Identify the demonstrative** (this, that, these, those)

**2. Check number:**
- This/That → antecedent MUST be singular OR whole idea (treated as singular)
- These/Those → antecedent MUST be plural OR multiple ideas

**3. Read the PREVIOUS sentence carefully**

**4. Ask: Specific noun or whole idea?**
- Specific noun → look for matching singular/plural noun
- Whole idea → summarize the main point of previous sentence

**5. Eliminate wrong options:**
- Number mismatch (❌)
- Gender mismatch if applicable (❌)
- Logically wrong (❌)

**6. VERIFY by substitution:**
Replace demonstrative dengan pilihan jawaban kamu → make sense?

**7. When in doubt, lean towards WHOLE IDEA interpretation** (karena ini paling sering!)

### Mini Practice Bacaan:

> "**Climate change** is causing unprecedented environmental shifts. Glaciers are melting at alarming rates, sea levels are rising, and extreme weather events are becoming more frequent. **These changes** threaten coastal communities and ecosystems worldwide. Scientists warn that without immediate action, **this** could lead to irreversible damage. **That** is why international cooperation is crucial."

**Analysis:**
1. "These changes" → refers to **"glaciers melting, sea levels rising, and extreme weather events becoming more frequent"** (multiple effects, plural)

2. First "this" → refers to **"the situation/condition of climate change and its effects"** (whole idea, treated as singular)

3. "That" → refers to **"the potential for irreversible damage"** or **"the urgency of the climate crisis"** (mentioned in previous sentence, thus "that")

See how demonstratives create **cohesion** throughout the text? They connect ideas across sentences!

## Materi 3.2.4: Analisis Relative Pronouns dan Other References

### What Are Relative Pronouns?

Relative pronouns itu kata penghubung yang **introduce relative clauses** (klausa yang ngasih informasi tambahan tentang noun).

**The Gang:**
- **WHO** → untuk ORANG (subject)
- **WHOM** → untuk ORANG (object)
- **WHOSE** → untuk KEPEMILIKAN
- **WHICH** → untuk BENDA/HEWAN
- **THAT** → untuk ORANG atau BENDA (versatile!)

### WHO: The People Person

"Who" digunakan untuk refer to ORANG dan functions as SUBJECT dari relative clause.

**Formula:**
**Noun (person) + WHO + VERB**

**Example Bacaan:**

> "**Dr. Sarah Johnson**, **who** has dedicated 20 years to cancer research, recently received a Nobel Prize."

"Who" refers to → **Dr. Sarah Johnson**

**Key Point:** "Who" diikuti oleh VERB karena dia subject!

**Common Pattern:**

> "**Scientists who** study climate patterns have observed significant changes."

"Who" → connects "scientists" with "study climate patterns"

### WHOM: The Formal Object

"Whom" untuk ORANG tapi sebagai OBJECT dari verb atau preposisi.

**Formula:**
**Noun (person) + WHOM + SUBJECT + VERB**

**Example:**

> "The **researcher whom** the university hired last year is already making breakthroughs."

"Whom" refers to → **"researcher"** (researcher itu OBJECT of "hired")

**Important Note:**

Dalam **modern conversational English**, "whom" udah jarang dan sering diganti "who":
- Formal: "the person **whom** I met"
- Casual: "the person **who** I met" atau "the person I met" (relative pronoun omitted!)

**Tapi di SNBT**, teks akademik masih pakai "whom" secara proper, jadi kamu harus recognize!

**Pattern with Preposition:**

> "The **scientist to whom** the award was given is very humble."

"Whom" refers to → **"scientist"** (scientist itu object of preposition "to")

**Alternative (less formal):**
> "The scientist **who** the award was given **to**..."

### WHOSE: The Possessive Connector

"Whose" untuk nunjukin KEPEMILIKAN dan bisa untuk orang ATAU benda!

**Formula:**
**Noun + WHOSE + NOUN + VERB**

**Example (Person):**

> "**The author whose** book won the Pulitzer Prize will speak at the conference."

"Whose" refers to → **"the author"** (dan "book" adalah milik author tersebut)

**Example (Thing):**

> "**The company whose** headquarters is in Silicon Valley is a tech giant."

"Whose" refers to → **"the company"** (headquarters adalah milik company tersebut)

**Key Feature:**
"Whose" SELALU diikuti oleh NOUN (yang dimiliki)!

### WHICH: For Things and Animals

"Which" untuk refer to BENDA, HEWAN, atau IDEAS (bukan orang!).

**Formula:**
**Noun (thing/animal) + WHICH + VERB/SUBJECT + VERB**

**Example (Defining Clause):**

> "The **theory which** Einstein proposed revolutionized physics."

"Which" refers to → **"theory"**

**Example (Non-Defining Clause):**

> "**The research**, **which** took five years to complete, has significant implications."

"Which" refers to → **"the research"**

**Difference:**
- **Defining clause** (no commas): restrictive, essential info
- **Non-defining clause** (dengan commas): additional, non-essential info

**Special Use: "Which" Referring to Whole Clause**

Super important! "Which" bisa refer to ENTIRE previous clause, bukan cuma satu noun!

**Example:**

> "The experiment failed repeatedly, **which** surprised the researchers."

"Which" refers to apa?
→ **"The fact that the experiment failed repeatedly"** (WHOLE IDEA, bukan cuma "experiment" atau "researchers")

**How to Know:**
Kalau ada **COMMA + which**, dan secara logic "which" nggak masuk akal kalau cuma refers to nearest noun → kemungkinan merujuk whole clause!

### THAT: The Universal Connector

"That" itu paling versatile! Bisa untuk ORANG atau BENDA.

**Example (Person):**

> "The **scientist that** discovered the cure is now famous."

**Example (Thing):**

> "The **method that** researchers used is innovative."

**Important Rules:**

✅ "That" untuk **defining (restrictive) clauses** → NO commas
❌ "That" TIDAK untuk **non-defining clauses** (use "which" instead)

**Example:**
✅ "The book **that** I read was fascinating." (defining, no comma)
✅ "The book, **which** I read last month, was fascinating." (non-defining, with commas)
❌ "The book, **that** I read last month, was fascinating." (❌ WRONG!)

**In SNBT:**
Kalau ada clause dengan commas → use "which" (for things) or "who" (for people)
Kalau NO commas → could be "that", "which", or "who"

### ONE dan ONES: The Generic References

"One" dan "ones" itu substitutes untuk NOUN yang udah disebutkan, untuk avoid repetition.

**ONE (Singular):**

**Example:**

> "I need a **pen**. Do you have **one**?"

"One" = a pen (generic, bukan pen yang spesifik)

**In Academic Text:**

> "Traditional **methods** are effective, but modern **ones** are faster."

"Ones" refers to → **"methods"** (modern methods)

**Pattern:**
**Adjective + ONE/ONES**
> "The **red car** is expensive, but the **blue one** is affordable."

"One" = car (blue car)

### SOME, OTHERS, ANOTHER: The Subset References

Ini pronouns yang refer to SUBSETS dari group yang udah disebutkan.

**SOME:**

**Example:**

> "The **participants** were divided into groups. **Some** received the treatment, while others served as controls."

"Some" refers to → **"some of the participants"**

**OTHERS:**

"Others" refers to → **"other participants"** (yang nggak dapat treatment)

**ANOTHER:**

**Example:**

> "One **theory** suggests genetic factors. **Another** focuses on environmental causes."

"Another" refers to → **"another theory"** (different from the first one)

**Pattern Recognition:**
- **Some... others...** → dividing a group into subsets
- **One... another... yet another...** → listing different items/ideas

### SUCH: The Type Indicator

"Such" refers to TYPE atau KIND yang disebutkan sebelumnya.

**Example:**

> "The **study** used randomized controlled trials, double-blind procedures, and peer review. **Such** rigorous methods ensure reliability."

"Such rigorous methods" refers to → **"randomized controlled trials, double-blind procedures, and peer review"** (those TYPES of methods)

**Pattern:**
**SUCH + NOUN** → refers to examples of that type mentioned before

### FORMER dan LATTER: The First and Last

"Former" dan "latter" untuk refer to dua hal yang disebutkan, especially dalam academic/formal writing.

**FORMER** = yang pertama disebutkan
**LATTER** = yang terakhir disebutkan

**Example:**

> "Both **wind energy** and **solar power** are renewable. The **former** is more suitable for coastal areas, while the **latter** works better in sunny regions."

- "Former" refers to → **"wind energy"** (mentioned first)
- "Latter" refers to → **"solar power"** (mentioned last)

**Important:**
Only use untuk DUA items! Kalau lebih dari dua, use "the first", "the second", "the last" instead.

### Reference Chains: Following the Thread

Kadang dalam satu paragraf, ada chain of references yang connect back!

**Example Bacaan:**

> "**Marie Curie** was a pioneering scientist. **She** conducted groundbreaking research on radioactivity. **Her** discoveries earned **her** two Nobel Prizes. **This achievement** made **her** the first woman to win **the award**, and the only person to win in two different sciences."

**Analysis:**
- "She" → Marie Curie
- "Her" (first) → possessive, Marie Curie's
- "Her" (second) → object pronoun, Marie Curie
- "This achievement" → winning two Nobel Prizes
- "Her" (third) → Marie Curie
- "The award" → Nobel Prize

See how pronouns create a **connected narrative**? Understanding reference chains helps you follow complex texts!

### Ambiguous References: When It's Unclear

Sometimes, reference could be ambiguous (bisa beberapa kemungkinan). How to resolve?

**Example:**

> "**John** told **Mark** that **he** should prepare the presentation."

"He" refers to siapa? John atau Mark?
→ **Ambiguous from this sentence alone!**

**Resolution strategies:**

**1. Look at NEXT sentence for clarification:**
> "John told Mark that he should prepare the presentation. **Mark** agreed and started working immediately."
→ Ah, "he" = Mark!

**2. Use logic/context:**
> "John told Mark that he should prepare the presentation. **After all**, it was Mark's area of expertise."
→ Logic: "he" = Mark (karena expertise-nya Mark)

**3. Look for verb agreement clues:**
> "The **committee** told the **researchers** that **they** should revise."
→ "They" = researchers (plural, committee biasanya singular "it")

**In SNBT:**
Kalau reference ambiguous, text biasanya ngasih **extra context clues**. Baca carefully!

### Common Traps dengan Relative Pronouns:

⚠️ **TRAP #1: "Which" vs "That" Confusion**

Remember: 
- "Which" bisa untuk defining ATAU non-defining (dengan commas)
- "That" HANYA untuk defining (NO commas)

⚠️ **TRAP #2: "Who" vs "Which" for Organizations**

Organizations/companies/institutions → bisa "which" ATAU "who/that" (kalau dianggap sebagai people)!

> "The **company which**..." ✅ (company as entity)
> "The **company that**..." ✅ (versatile)
> "The **team who**..." ✅ (team as people)
> "The **team which**..." ✅ (team as entity)

Context determines! Tapi umumnya:
- Singular organization → "which" atau "that"
- Organization as people → "who" atau "that"

⚠️ **TRAP #3: Misidentifying "Which" Clause Reference**

**Example:**

> "Scientists analyzed the **data**, which revealed unexpected patterns."

"Which" refers to:
A. Scientists (❌ "which" bukan untuk orang!)
B. Data (✅ CORRECT!)

**But:**

> "Scientists analyzed the data **incorrectly**, which led to wrong conclusions."

"Which" refers to:
A. Data (❌ data nggak "lead to conclusions")
B. The fact that analysis was incorrect (✅ whole clause!)

### Strategy for Relative Pronoun Questions:

**STEP 1:** Identify the relative pronoun type

**STEP 2:** Determine what it CAN refer to:
- Who/whom → PERSON
- Which → THING/ANIMAL/WHOLE CLAUSE
- That → PERSON or THING
- Whose → POSSESSION (person or thing)

**STEP 3:** Look at the NOUN immediately before the relative pronoun (usually that's the antecedent!)

**STEP 4:** Check logic: Does it make sense?

**STEP 5:** If it's "which" with comma, consider whole clause interpretation

**STEP 6:** Verify by substitution

### Mini Practice Bacaan:

> "**Dr. Martinez**, **who** has extensive experience in neuroscience, leads a **team** **that** investigates brain plasticity. The **research**, **which** has received international recognition, focuses on neural regeneration. **One** of the breakthroughs involved stem cells, while **another** utilized gene therapy. **Such** innovations offer hope for patients **whose** conditions were previously untreatable."

**Analysis:**
1. "Who" → Dr. Martinez
2. "That" → team
3. "Which" → research (or potentially the whole fact about research)
4. "One" → one (breakthrough)
5. "Another" → another (breakthrough)
6. "Such innovations" → stem cell and gene therapy breakthroughs
7. "Whose" → patients' (possessive)

Perfect example of how multiple reference types work together in academic text!

## Materi 3.2.5: Analisis Ambiguous Reference

### What Is Ambiguous Reference?

**Ambiguous reference** itu kondisi di mana satu pronoun bisa merujuk ke **lebih dari satu possible antecedent**, bikin makna jadi unclear atau open to interpretation.

**Example:**

> "**Sarah** told **Emma** that **she** got the promotion."

"She" itu siapa? Sarah atau Emma?
→ **AMBIGUOUS!** Bisa either one!

**In SNBT:**

Good news: Teks SNBT biasanya **nggak deliberately ambiguous**! Penulis akademik trained untuk write clearly. Tapi, kadang complexity dari sentence structure bisa bikin reference terlihat ambiguous at first glance.

**Your job:** Learn to **resolve** ambiguity dengan context clues!

### Common Scenarios Causing Ambiguity:

### Scenario 1: Multiple Nouns dengan Same Number/Gender

**Example:**

> "**The professor** told **the student** that **he** needed to revise the paper."

"He" → professor atau student?
→ Both masculine, both singular → grammatically bisa either!

**How to Resolve:**

**Clue #1: Next sentence provides clarification**

> "The professor told the student that he needed to revise the paper. **The student** immediately started working on the corrections."
→ Aha! "He" = student!

**Clue #2: Logic/Context**

> "The professor told the student that he needed to revise the paper **before the deadline**."
→ Logically, student yang revise paper, bukan professor!

**Clue #3: Verb form/tense consistency**

> "The professor told the student that he **had made** several errors."
→ "Had made" suggests the one who did the action → student!

### Scenario 2: Pronoun dengan Multiple Possible Singular Antecedents

**Example:**

> "The **study** examined the **effectiveness** of the new **drug**. **It** showed promising results."

"It" refers to apa?
A. Study
B. Effectiveness
C. Drug

→ Grammatically, semua singular, semua bisa!

**Resolution:**

**Logic check:** Apa yang "showed results"?
- Study showed results? ✅ Makes sense (study yang provide results)
- Effectiveness showed results? ❌ Weird (effectiveness itu abstract, nggak "show" results)
- Drug showed results? ⚠️ Possible, tapi usually "study/research" yang "show"

**Answer:** "It" most likely = **Study** (paling make sense dalam academic writing context)

**Confirmation from next sentence:**

> "...It showed promising results. **The research team** plans to conduct further trials."
→ "Research team" connects to "study" → confirms "it" = study!

### Scenario 3: "This" atau "That" dengan Multiple Ideas

**Example:**

> "The **government** increased **taxes**, cut public **spending**, and reduced **subsidies**. **This** angered many citizens."

"This" refers to apa?
A. Government
B. Taxes
C. Spending cuts
D. Subsidy reduction
E. ALL of the above policies

**Common Mistake:** Picking the nearest noun (subsidies)

**Correct Approach:**

**Ask:** What "angered citizens"?
→ Bukan satu specific policy, tapi **combination of all policies**!

**Answer:** "This" = **The government's economic policies** atau **All these measures** (whole idea!)

### Scenario 4: Chain of References

**Complex Example:**

> "**Professor Lee** asked **her assistant** to analyze **the data**. **She** wanted **it** completed by Friday so that **she** could include **it** in **her** presentation."

Multiple pronouns! Who/what does each refer to?

**Breaking it down:**

1. "She" (first) → Professor Lee (subject of wanting)
2. "It" (first) → the data analysis / the task (to be completed)
3. "She" (second) → Professor Lee (could include)
4. "It" (second) → the data / the analysis (to be included)
5. "Her" → Professor Lee's (possessive)

**Strategy untuk chain references:**

**Step 1:** Map out all pronouns dalam paragraph
**Step 2:** Start dengan yang paling obvious (possessive usually easiest)
**Step 3:** Use logic untuk link each pronoun
**Step 4:** Check consistency (does the story make sense?)

### Proximity Principle: Near vs Far

**General Rule:** Pronoun biasanya refers to **nearest compatible noun**.

**Example:**

> "The **company** announced new **policies** regarding employee **benefits**. **They** will take effect next month."

"They" → policies (nearest plural noun)

**BUT, Proximity ≠ Always Correct!**

**Counter-Example:**

> "The **CEO** discussed the financial **reports** with the board **members**. **He** emphasized the need for cost reduction."

"He" → CEO (bukan "reports" atau "members", meskipun lebih deket!)

**Why?** Logic! Reports dan members nggak bisa "emphasize", but CEO bisa!

**LESSON:** **Proximity helps, BUT logic trumps proximity!**

### Number Agreement sebagai Clue

Number agreement itu **powerful clue** untuk eliminate ambiguity!

**Example:**

> "The **research team** analyzed various **samples**. **They** discovered significant anomalies."

"They" → samples (plural) BUKAN team (usually singular "it")

**BUT:**

> "The **research team** worked tirelessly. **They** published their findings last month."

"They" → team (treated as plural, individuals)

**British vs American English:**
- **British:** Collective nouns often plural ("the team are...")
- **American:** Collective nouns usually singular ("the team is...")

**In SNBT:** Follow the verb agreement yang digunakan dalam text itu sendiri!

### Gender Agreement sebagai Clue

Kalau ada pronoun dengan gender (he/she), that's a **strong clue**!

**Example:**

> "**Dr. Amanda Chen** and **Professor Robert Smith** collaborated on the project. **She** provided the theoretical framework while **he** conducted the experiments."

"She" → Dr. Amanda Chen (female)
"He" → Professor Robert Smith (male)

Easy! Gender makes it clear!

**BUT, watch out for:**

Modern texts sometimes use "they" as singular gender-neutral:

> "Each **researcher** must submit **their** report."

"Their" (singular) → each researcher (gender-neutral)

### Verb Agreement sebagai Clue

Verb forms bisa help identify antecedent!

**Example:**

> "The **scientist** and his **team** presented their findings. **He** explained the methodology while **they** answered questions."

- "He explained" → singular subject → scientist
- "They answered" → plural subject → team

**Tense Consistency:**

> "The **experiment** failed, and the **researchers** were disappointed. **It** had been conducted under strict conditions."

"It" → experiment (singular, "had been conducted" matches "experiment")

### Context and World Knowledge

Sometimes, **common sense** dan **world knowledge** resolve ambiguity!

**Example:**

> "The **patient** consulted the **doctor** about the **symptoms**. **She** prescribed medication."

"She" → most likely doctor (karena doctors yang prescribe, bukan patients!)

(Unless context sebelumnya establish gender, default assumption: doctor yang prescribe)

**Example 2:**

> "The **tree** fell on the **car** during the storm. **It** was completely destroyed."

"It" → probably the car (trees don't get "destroyed" easily, but cars do!)

### The "Whole Clause" Reference

Salah satu penyebab ambiguity: kadang pronoun refers to ENTIRE clause, bukan specific noun!

**Example:**

> "The government ignored expert warnings and proceeded with the plan anyway. **This** proved to be a costly mistake."

"This" refers to:
A. Government (❌)
B. Warnings (❌)
C. Plan (⚠️ Possible but incomplete)
D. **The fact that government ignored warnings and proceeded** (✅ WHOLE ACTION!)

**Clue:** "Proved to be a mistake" → apa yang bisa jadi mistake? Bukan noun, tapi **action/decision**!

### Resolution Strategies Recap:

When faced dengan potentially ambiguous reference:

**STRATEGY 1: READ NEXT SENTENCE(S)**
Often provides clarification through restatement atau continuation

**STRATEGY 2: CHECK NUMBER/GENDER AGREEMENT**
Eliminate options that don't match

**STRATEGY 3: APPLY LOGIC/COMMON SENSE**
Which option makes most sense dalam context?

**STRATEGY 4: CHECK VERB AGREEMENT**
Tense, form, subject-verb agreement bisa ngasih clues

**STRATEGY 5: CONSIDER WHOLE-IDEA INTERPRETATION**
Especially untuk "this", "that", "which" dengan comma

**STRATEGY 6: USE PROXIMITY AS GUIDE (not rule!)**
Nearest compatible noun often correct, but verify dengan logic

**STRATEGY 7: TRACE BACK THROUGH SENTENCE STRUCTURE**
Follow grammatical connections (subject, object, possessive)

### Common Patterns yang Bikin Ambiguous (dan How to Handle):

**PATTERN 1: Two people, one pronoun**

> "**John** asked **Michael** if **he** could help."

**Resolution:** Look for who is in position to help → likely Michael

**PATTERN 2: Multiple singular nouns**

> "The **report** included the **recommendation** and the **conclusion**. **It** was well-received."

**Resolution:** What could be "well-received"? → The report (entire document)

**PATTERN 3: Organization + People**

> "The **company** told **employees** that **they** would benefit."

**Resolution:** "They" → employees (people benefit, not abstract company)

**PATTERN 4: Abstract noun + Concrete noun**

> "The **success** of the **project** was remarkable. **It** exceeded expectations."

**Resolution:** What "exceeded"? → Success or project? Both possible, but "success" more likely (success yang exceed expectations)

### Practice Approach for Ambiguous Reference:

**WHEN YOU SEE A REFERENCE QUESTION:**

**STEP 1:** Read the sentence containing the pronoun

**STEP 2:** Read the sentence BEFORE it

**STEP 3:** Identify ALL possible antecedents (nouns that match number/gender)

**STEP 4:** Eliminate based on:
- Number mismatch
- Gender mismatch (if applicable)
- Logical impossibility

**STEP 5:** From remaining options, choose the one that:
- Makes most logical sense
- Fits the context
- Maintains coherence dalam narrative

**STEP 6:** VERIFY by substitution (replace pronoun dengan pilihan kamu → make sense?)

**STEP 7:** If still uncertain, check next sentence for confirmation

### Real Example Breakdown:

> "**Climate scientists** have been warning about rising temperatures for decades. Despite their warnings, **global emissions** continue to increase. **This** has led to accelerating ice melt in polar regions. **They** are now calling for immediate action before **it** becomes irreversible."

**Let's identify each reference:**

1. "This" (led to accelerating ice melt) refers to:
   - Possible: warnings (❌ logic fail)
   - Possible: emissions increasing (✅ makes sense!)
   - Answer: **"The fact that global emissions continue to increase"** (whole idea)

2. "They" (calling for action) refers to:
   - Possible: temperatures (❌ temperatures don't call)
   - Possible: emissions (❌ emissions don't call)
   - Possible: polar regions (❌ regions don't call)
   - Possible: climate scientists (✅ makes sense!)
   - Answer: **"Climate scientists"**

3. "It" (becomes irreversible) refers to:
   - Possible: action (❌ action can't be irreversible in this context)
   - Possible: ice melt (✅ possible!)
   - Possible: the situation/damage (✅ most likely!)
   - Answer: **"The climate damage/situation"** (implied from context)

See? Step by step, dengan logic dan context clues, ambiguity bisa resolved!

### Final Tips:

✅ **Don't overthink!** Teks SNBT dirancang untuk bisa dipahami. Kalau kamu bingung, usually context akan ngasih clue yang cukup jelas.

✅ **Trust logic over proximity.** Nearest noun bukan always the answer.

✅ **Read full context.** Jangan cuma focus on satu kalimat. Surrounding sentences provide crucial clues!

✅ **When in doubt, whole-idea interpretation** often works untuk "this", "that", "which"!

✅ **Practice makes perfect.** Semakin banyak kamu practice, semakin cepat kamu recognize patterns!
